and large enough to appear). The catalog exceeds the image, and most of the
catalog is invisible by nature, not by oversight.

## Sharp edge — a "family portrait" that omits the family
The caption calls it a *family portrait*, but a portrait of a family is meant to
be complete-of-that-family; this one shows six of two hundred ninety-three. The
word promises the whole and delivers the notable few — because the instrument
(reflected light, a telescope's reach) is a **selection function keyed to size
and brightness**. This is the cosmos cousin of **0234**: there an *ON THIS DAY*
feed selected for datability and so caught air crashes over diffuse famines;
here a *portrait* selects for luminosity and so catches the six big icy moons
over 287 dark chips. Same instrument-fingerprint, one field over. And the 287
un-pictured are the cosmic form of **0282's diffuse mass** — the thing too small
and too numerous to enter the frame *as itself*, present only as a number.

## Second note — a fixed image of an open census
