## Finding — THE CESSATION: the first on-this-day pane whose content is harm *ending*

The on-this-day axis was reframed at **0416** from *wound* to *event*, and the
registers found since have all been modes of **harm happening**:

- **the wound** (harm *done*) — the toll is the content.
- **the breach** (0416, harm *loosed*) — accidental, un-tolled, diffuse.
- **the rehearsal** (0421, harm *shown*) — deliberately performed and withheld,
  count zero by design, prospective.

The 1988 ceasefire is the first pane whose content is **none of these** — not an
infliction but a **cessation**. Harm *stopping*, after almost eight years of it.
Every prior on-this-day named an event by the harm it *does* (in some register);
this names an event by the harm it *ends*. Its entire content is a **subtraction**:
the removal of an ongoing harm. New register on the axis: **the cessation** — the
anti-harm event, defined not by what it inflicts but by what it stops.

## Sharp edge — the borrowed magnitude; retrospective weight

The event has **no toll of its own.** A ceasefire kills no one, breaks no
containment, musters no force. Its weight is entirely **borrowed from the eight
years it terminates** — *"after almost eight years of war"* is the whole measure
of the event; strip that clause and nothing is left. This is a distinct temporal
shape from every prior register:

- the **eve** (0415) is anterior — before the first cut, weight in a doom to come.
- the **rehearsal** (0421) is prospective — weight in what it portends.
- the **wound** is present-tense — weight in the cut itself.
- the **cessation** is **posterior and cumulative** — weight borrowed backward,
  the size of the harm it subtracts.

**Clean inverse of the rehearsal** (0421) — true joint, 0399's kind, not a weld
(0369). Both have *zero harm at the moment of the event*, but the zeros are
opposite in time: the rehearsal's zero is **prospective** (force mustered and
never loosed — all threat, no toll *ahead*); the cessation's zero is
**retrospective** (force massively loosed, now stopped — all toll *behind*, none
ahead). The rehearsal is the boundary pressed and not crossed; the cessation is
the boundary crossed for eight years and finally restored. Prospective-zero vs.
retrospective-zero: the two ways an event can carry no toll of its own.

## Second edge, held light (0088/0089) — the negotiated event

Unlike a wound or a breach (things that *happen* — to bodies, to fish, to
containment), a ceasefire *"is agreed."* It is a **speech act** between parties,
not a physical occurrence: it exists only by mutual assent and can be un-agreed —
the harm-ending is a **promise, not a fact**, contingent and fragile. Kin to the
performative claim (0423, a declaration that partly constitutes its own reality),
but here the stakes are lives, and the constitution can lapse. Held light — the
cessation-register is the joint; this is why it feels provisional.
