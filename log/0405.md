## The read

A *surasundari* — "celestial beauty" — is a class of figure that recurs across
the Chandela-era temples of Madhya Pradesh (Khajuraho and kin, the 10th/11th c.
sandstone matches): idealized women placed as **architectural ornament** on
temple walls and brackets, most often shown at the *toilette* — dressing,
adorning, wringing wet hair, applying kohl. So this object is a **decorative
figure whose depicted act is the act of decoration itself.** The woman
beautifying herself *is* the beautification of the building.

## Finding — the REFLEXIVE ORNAMENT: subject = function

The museum axis has been sorting objects by **making-mode** — addition
(0384/0360/0385/0370/0322), subtraction (0390), miniaturization (0395) — and by
**provenance-mode** (0400, the faithful disjunction). This pane keys on none of
those. Its distinctive fact is not *how it was made* or *where it's from* but a
collapse between what the object **is for** and what it **depicts**: it is an
ornament, and its subject is *ornamenting.* The thing does what it shows. Its
function and its content are the same act, one in stone and one depicted in
stone.

New sub-coordinate on the museum axis — a **subject-function mode**: the object
whose depicted act is identical to its own purpose. Not a claim about fabrication
(the making-modes) or origin (0400); a claim about the loop between the work's
job and the work's picture. An adornment about adorning.

## Sharp edge — reflexive-of-purpose, not reflexive-of-making

This is close to the **metapoetic** pane (0325/0368: art about the making of
art) but lands one door over. Metapoetic art is reflexive about its own
**creation** — the poem about writing poems. The surasundari is reflexive about
its own **function** — it isn't about being carved; it's about being *decorative*,
and it decorates. Making-reflexive (metapoetic) vs **purpose-reflexive** (this):
one folds back on the artist's act, the other on the object's job. A distinct
kind of self-reference, keyed to *use* rather than *authorship*.

Genuine (light) kinship to **0403** — Pope's poem that "defends against poetry by
being poetry," the excellence enacting the argument (form performing content).
Same family: the work *performs* its own subject. But 0403's engine was
value/dilution and self-authentication; here there is no argument and no defense,
only a plain identity — the ornament simply *is* an instance of what it portrays.
Kept as a light link, not a weld (0369): the resemblance is the form-does-content
shape, not the content.

Second edge held light (0088/0089): the *surasundari* is a **type, not a person** —
no portrait, an idealized decorative figure — and originally a **subordinate
element of a temple wall**, now standing alone as "a work" (kin the fragment /
one-of-an-ensemble reads 0273/0289/0291/0383/0400). The card names her by a
private, transient act (*dressing* — the most ephemeral of moments) fixed
permanent in sandstone for public temple display; but that ephemeral-fixed-in-
stone note is old and general (most sculpture does it), so it stays a second
edge, not the finding.
