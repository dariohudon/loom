## Finding — the OPEN FLOOR: a "more than N" claim is confirm-only

Every prior stray fact committed to a **shape the world could refute** — a
cardinality (squid eyes 0362), an argmax (0340), a share (0367's swapped
denominator), a distribution (0372's islands), a self-referential property
(0382). Each could be *wrong*, and the class spent twenty passes sorting the
ways they were.

This one can't be wrong in the direction its truth actually lies. *"More than
40,000"* is **existentially quantified**: it is confirmed by a *single* witness
above the line (any large dictionary) and **cannot be refuted from above** — a
larger true count (85,000; 106,000) doesn't break it, it *fulfils* it. The floor
is the safest possible true fact: unfalsifiable in the one direction the evidence
runs.

That is a new coordinate on the **verification-mode axis** the class opened at
0382. 0382 sorted *where* a fact is checked (against the world vs inside a closed
notation); this sorts the **logical shape of the check** — an exact count is
confirm-*or*-refute (two-sided), a "more than N" is confirm-*only* (one-sided).
The floored claim buys its safety by declining to name a ceiling.

## Sharp edge — the floor that understates, yet awes

The rhetorician's move is to set N **low enough to be unimpeachable, high enough
to astonish.** 40,000 is both: the truth clears it twofold, so it can never be
caught out, and 40,000 is already a number that makes a writing system sound
boundless. The *honesty* (it's true) buys the *awe*, and the *floor* (won't
commit up) buys the *safety* — a fact engineered to be simultaneously true,
striking, and unfalsifiable.

And the awe is bought partly by **concealment**. Like 0372's 7,100 islands
(~460 of real size), the 40,000+ characters are mostly **specks** — variants,
obsolete forms, dead glyphs a reader will never meet; the working script is a few
thousand. But where 0372 *disclosed* its own distribution in a second clause
("only ~460 > 1 sq mi"), this fact has **no second clause** — the "more than"
lets the grand number stand alone and hides that the counted things are largely
unused. So: **companion to 0372, inverted on disclosure.** 0372 = a grand count
that volunteers its smallness; 0392 = a grand count that keeps it, the floor
doing the hiding a lie would otherwise have to do (kin the salience-shield
0334/0340, but with no false number — the concealment is structural, not
fabricated).
