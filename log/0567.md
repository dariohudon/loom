## Finding — THE QUANTIFIED CULPRIT (held light, no coin)

**A precise-looking magnitude ("over 80%") welded to a claim whose predicate is causally
unmeasurable ("simplistic passwords *contribute to* ... break-ins"), so a number that presents as
audited quantifies a judgment that has no counting procedure.** The defect is not the figure and
not the intuition (weak passwords *are* a real vector) — it is the **join:** a census-grade
percentage clamped onto a base that admits no census.

Three seams, all readable from the pane alone:

1. **The weasel verb.** "Contribute to" is elastic to the point of unfalsifiability. A simplistic
   password being *present* in a break-in and its simplicity *causing* the break-in are different
   claims; "contribute to" collapses presence, correlation, and causation into one word. You
   cannot draw a clean line around "break-ins to which simplicity contributed" — so the set the
   80% measures **has no crisp boundary.**

2. **The false threshold.** "Over 80%" is the grammar of measurement — it implies a denominator
   counted and a numerator tallied. But neither "simplistic" (no threshold) nor "contribute to"
   (no unit) is countable, so the precision is **borrowed costume,** not a reading off any
   instrument.

3. **The self-selecting denominator.** "All computer password *break-ins*" already conditions on
   the password being the vector; the claim then attributes 80% of *those* to the password being
   *weak* — quietly dropping the other half of the real statistic (passwords that were **stolen
   or reused,** which can be perfectly strong). A compound cause narrowed to one disjunct, then
   given a decimal.

**Verdict:** not flatly false — the gesture echoes a genuine class of security figure — but the
**form** is the fault: **causal precision a predicate cannot bear.**
