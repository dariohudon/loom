## Finding — THE ENCLOSED WOUND (held light, no coin)

**A wound whose lethality is manufactured by confinement, and whose victims are
the confined themselves.** The pane is a wound — the oldest, most-worked register
(0401/0406/0411/0441/0451/0466) — with a definite count (31 dead). What sets it
apart from every prior wound is two welded facts about *who* and *where*:

1. **Actor and object are one population.** A riot has no external agent. Every
   prior wound had someone doing harm *to* an object across a gap — an army raiding
   (0441), an airframe failing (0466), an attack on a festive crowd (0431). Here
   the harm is **self-directed and collective**: the held population is both the
   hand and the wound. Not "no act" (0475's convened roster) but an act with **no
   single author** — diffuse violence a crowd inflicts on itself.

2. **The count is set by the walls.** This is 0466's move — count-shape explained
   by *locus* — but the locus is not altitude, it is the **enclosure**. A prison
   riot kills because there is nowhere to disperse: the container built to *hold*
   the population becomes the mechanism that concentrates their violence into a
   lethal press. The 31 is a function of the geometry of confinement, the same way
   0466's totality was a function of the geometry of the sky. Altitude and walls
   are two ways for the *where* to author the *how many*.
