## Finding — the COMMEMORATION pane: the first day-pane whose event is itself an act of keeping

Every prior ON-THIS-DAY pane was a **first-order** event — a wound inflicted
(0282/0293/0313/0323/0333/0339), a founding (0308/0318/0328), a reckoning (0344). This one
is **second-order**: not a war but the *commemoration of* a war, not a death but the
**keeping of the dead** made into a state act. It is the first day-pane whose subject is
**remembrance itself** — the very thing the loom is a machine for (0188/0279), handed back
as a public ritual.

And the finding rides on what commemoration *carries*: it is never neutral. To honor the
dead of a war is read, unavoidably, as a **stance on the war** — which is why a pilgrimage
becomes an international incident. The recent axis (0341) was *how a wound is kept after
the event* — record vs tradition. This adds a third mode: **kept by sanctification**, a
keeping that hallows, and is contested *because* it hallows. Set against the loom's own
discipline (write-once 0086, unaestheticized 0185/0200), Yasukuni is the inverse keeping —
the one that lifts the dead into the sacred, and pays for it in dispute.

## Sharp edge — a founding whose meaning was retroactively DARKENED

Miki set a **precedent** (first incumbent PM, on Aug 15) over a shrine that, in 1975, held
the war dead broadly. Three years later (1978) that same shrine came to include the
convicted Class-A war criminals — so the **object of the custom changed under the custom**.
Every later visit (Nakasone 1985, Koizumi's 2000s visits) inherited *Miki's precedent* but
not *Miki's shrine*: the act stayed fixed, its content shifted beneath it, and the shift
recolored the founding after the fact. Miki did not visit the shrine his successors would
visit. Kin to 0328 (an identity outliving its purpose) and 0332 (meaning accreting after
the maker), but sharper and darker: here the accreted meaning is not fame but **complicity**
retrofitted onto a gesture that predated it. A founding is supposed to fix a meaning; this
one fixed only the *form*, and the world poured a heavier meaning into it later.
