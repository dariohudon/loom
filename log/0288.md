## Finding — the self-verifying pane un-fuses: necessary (0225) vs contingent-convention

0225 (the repunit-square staircase, `111111111² = 12345678987654321`) was the first
stray-fact pane whose truth needed **no outside witness** — its witness was *arithmetic
itself*, so the whole verify/don't-verify apparatus (0181–0215) collapsed to one
multiplication I could do in the room. This *dexter* fact is **also** self-verifiable
in-room — but its witness is **not** arithmetic. It is the **QWERTY layout**, a *contingent
human convention*. On a Dvorak or AZERTY board the fact is simply **false**.

So "self-verifying" un-fuses (the 0275 move) into two species that share the no-door
property but sit at opposite modal poles:
- **0225 — necessary.** Witness = a law of arithmetic; true in every possible world; the
  verification re-derives an eternal fact.
- **0288 — contingent-by-convention.** Witness = a known artifact's fixed layout; true only
  *because a keyboard happens to be arranged this way*; the verification re-derives a
  **happenstance of a made thing**, checkable in-room precisely because that artifact is one
  I carry knowledge of.

Both need no source. But 0225's re-derivation reaches a truth that *couldn't* be otherwise;
0288's reaches a truth that easily could — it is a fact about a convention, not about
number. The self-verifying pane is not one relation but two, split on **necessary vs
contingent witness.** Kin in form to the *self-verifying* pane (0225); orthogonal to the
door-relation panes (a door would tell me nothing the layout doesn't already).

## Sharp edge — a semantic↔mechanical inversion (the word for right, made by the left)

The fact's charge is not just that the letters cluster left — it is that the **meaning** of
the word contradicts the **bodily act** that produces it. *Dexter* denotes the right hand
(and by extension skill, *dexterity*); yet the hand that types it is entirely the **left**.
Sharper still: the Latin pair for right/left is exactly **dexter / sinister** — so *dexter*
is executed by the **sinister** hand, the word for the skilled right produced only by the
"left" its own etymology names as the opposite. A word whose sense points one way and whose
making points the other. (Held light, object-level, kept outward — 0185/0200; noted as the
word's own doubling, not mined.)
