## Finding — THE HEDGED FLOOR (held light, no coin)

A numeric claim stated as a **lower bound where the exact figure is perfectly
knowable** — "over six hundred" for a building whose room count (775) is
published to the unit. The "over" is not an admission of ignorance; it's a
**chosen slack**, a hedge that buys guaranteed truth by underselling. The fact
is true *because* it is weaker than it could be. Round the real number down to a
soft floor and prefix "over," and you have a sentence that cannot be wrong
downward — truth bought cheap.

**Clean cause-inverse of 0411 (THE FLOORED WOUND).** 0411 read "more than 300
deaths" — an inequality open at the top where the un-countability *was* the
eulogy: the toll was genuinely disputed (~370–420+), the floor **forced** by real
epistemic uncertainty, and the openness honest. Here the surface shape is
identical ("over X," open at the top) but the cause is inverted: the truth is
**fully countable** (775, a maintained inventory), so the floor is not epistemic
necessity but **rhetorical hedge**. Same "more-than" geometry, opposite ground:
0411 the floor mourns what can't be counted; 0487 the floor *declines to count*
what easily can be. Forced floor / **chosen floor.**

**Inverse-adjacent to the exaggeration family (0417/0422/0427/0452).** Those all
distort *upward* — inflate toward the better story (0427), stretch a magnitude or
a share past the evidence. This one distorts *downward* and stays true: the safe
direction of error is under, and the pane takes it. The exaggerators risk
falseness for effect; the hedger surrenders effect (775 is the more impressive
number) for unfalsifiability. A stray fact tuned for *safety*, not *story* — the
rare pane whose fault, if it has one, is timidity rather than reach.

**Sub-edge (light) — the counting unit floats (kin 0442/0447).** "Rooms" is
itself contingent: 775 folds in 78 bathrooms and 92 offices; a stricter "room"
would count fewer. So "over 600" is robust partly *because* it sits below every
plausible definition of "a room" — the hedge absorbs the 0447-style granularity
slack as a bonus. The lower bound is chosen low enough to survive the ambiguity
it never names.
