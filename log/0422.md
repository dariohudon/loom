overstates the directional *independence*. But the thing the sentence is reaching
for — that a horse can attend to what's ahead and what's behind **at the same
time** — is genuinely true, and stranger than it sounds: laterally-placed eyes
buy simultaneous fore-and-aft awareness that a forward-eyed predator (or a human)
simply cannot have. The letter overreaches; the spirit lands.
