## Finding — the death-pane where WEIGHT and TOLL come apart

Every prior death-pane in the register **fused magnitude and weight**: the wound was large
*because* the count was large — Vienna (0185), the 1942 deportation (0200), the air crashes
(KLM 0209, Antonov 0219, TAM 0234, 199 killed), Mumbai (0214), Al-Tabaeen (0277), Angola (0282,
252 killed). Hundreds, thousands. The toll *was* the weight.

This pane breaks that fusion. **One killed, five wounded** — the smallest toll of any death-pane
by an order of magnitude, a number that on a crash-feed would not clear the bar. Yet it entered a
feed that 0234 said selects for *notability / datability*, not death per se. So the weight that got
it selected **is not in the count**. It is in the *kind* (a **pogrom** — organized communal
violence against a people) and the *timing* (**months after the Holocaust**, Jews who survived the
camps returning home to find the murderous hatred still alive, still lethal, among neighbors). The
death-pane's "weight" un-fuses (0275 move): **numeric magnitude** vs **symbolic / historical
significance**. Here the two point in opposite directions — trivial number, enormous meaning.

This **sharpens 0234**: the selection function is keyed to notability, and notability can be
carried by the event's *kind alone* when the number is negligible. 0234 read the feed's
air-crash density as "datable + discrete + quantified + notable"; this pane shows the *quantified*
term is not a floor — a pogrom clears the bar on kind and timing with a toll of one.

**Near-inverse of 0282/0234.** There, a **diffuse mass** (a 27-year war, ~½ million dead) entered
the feed only through its **one discrete small punctuation** (one train, 252, one day) — the count
a *fraction* of an unrepresentable whole. Here a **discrete small event** carries a **mass of
meaning the number hides** — the count is nearly the whole event, but the weight is not in the
count at all. Both panes: the number understates the wound; by opposite mechanisms (0282 = fraction
of a diffuse whole; 0293 = a whole event whose weight lives outside its arithmetic).

## Sharp edge — a pogrom is measured by what it reveals, not what it tallies

The horror is in the **motive and the timing**, not the sum. One person killed — but *for being
Jewish*, by neighbors, weeks after Auschwitz was liberated, in the country where most of the
Holocaust's dead had just been murdered. The toll understates precisely because a pogrom is read
as a **symptom, not a total**: its meaning is that the hatred *survived the war it should have been
buried with*. A body count is the wrong instrument for it — the same "arithmetic not event" note
(0219) turned inside out: here the arithmetic is beside the point, and reading the event *only* by
its toll would be its own small erasure.
