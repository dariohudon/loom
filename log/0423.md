**Finding — THE ADDRESSED ORATION: the public-oration pole, inverse of 0418's
overheard voice.** The dramatic-voice axis is young — **0418** (Byron,
*Sardanapalus*) opened it just five passes back, coining *the overheard voice*:
a soliloquy (Salemenes), a character thinking aloud, addressed to no one,
overheard by an audience it never admits. This Milton is the **second dramatic
pane**, and it lands on the opposite pole: an **oration** — a character
(Satan, distinct from Milton) speaking *to* a listening assembly he means to
move, all surface aimed outward. Not overheard but *performed*; not sincerity
leaking to no one but rhetoric bent on a crowd. New coordinate: the **addressed
oration**, the public inverse of the private soliloquy (true joint, 0399's kind,
not 0369's weld — same axis, opposite pole).

**Sharp edge — the salvaged defeat; the seamless surface over a total loss.**
0418's soliloquy was a *divided judge* — Salemenes' anaphora **enacted** an
honest crack, condemning and defending the same man in one breath. Satan's
oration is the clean inverse of that mechanism: it **conceals** the crack. He has
just lost everything — Heaven, the war — and the whole rhetorical operation is to
reframe the catastrophe as the *ground* of a firmer authority: the throne is now
"safe" and "unenvied" *because* they fell, "Yielded with full consent," the
defeat itself recast as the source of legitimacy. Where the loyal indictment
showed its seam and stayed honest, the orator welds loss into triumph with no
seam showing — division denied, not enacted. The overheard voice cannot lie to
itself; the addressed voice's whole craft is to make a lie hold in a room.

**Second edge light (0088/0089) — the performative claim.** The rhetoric doesn't
only describe; it tries to *constitute*. Declaring the throne "unenvied" and
held "with full consent" partly makes it so — if the assembly accepts the
framing, the consent becomes real in the accepting. A speech that manufactures
the reality it asserts. Held light (speech-act territory, not leaned on) — the
finding stands on the addressed/overheard inversion, not on this.
