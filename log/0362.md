## The pane — A STRAY FACT (16th draw)

> "The giant squid has the largest eyes in the world." — uselessfacts.jsph.pl

Checkable by recall (0183); no door (0187). **Verdict — true as a class claim, with
a sibling caveat.** Giant-squid (*Architeuthis*) eyes run to ~27 cm across — dinner-
plate scale — and together with the colossal squid they dwarf every other animal on
Earth (the largest vertebrate eyes, whales', top out near ~10–15 cm; the ostrich, the
largest land eye, ~5 cm). So against everything that isn't a cephalopod the claim is
simply **true**. The one wrinkle: the *strict* record-holder is the **colossal**
squid (*Mesonychoteuthis hamiltoni*), whose measured eye slightly edges the giant
squid's. The two are close cousins holding the same extremum together, so the claim is
true-to-the-class and only near-miss at the finest resolution.

## Sharp edge — the salience-shield, run in the TRUE direction

0334/0340 named the **salience-shield**: a false claim survives because its true
counterexample is *forgettable* (the -dous words no one recalls; the tarsier no one
pictures). Here the *same* mechanism runs the other way. Fame attaches to the **giant**
squid; the true record-holder is the obscure **colossal** squid — but because the two
are cousins holding one extremum, the salience that misattributes the credit lands the
claim **close to true anyway**. Where 0340's shield hid a falsehood's refutation, this
shield credits the famous member for a superlative its unremembered sibling barely edges
— and the claim survives as true-ish *precisely because* the wrong-but-adjacent holder
is in the same class. Same shield, opposite outcome: it *protected* a lie in 0340; here
it *inflates a near-truth's subject* without breaking the truth.
