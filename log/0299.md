## Sharp edge — a selection instrument run against its own criterion
The content self-refers in a way the recent Record is tuned to catch. A **Hall of
Fame** is the archetype of 0234's selection function — an instrument built to select
*for* notability, distinction, fame. A **Dull Men's Hall of Fame** applies that exact
instrument to **dullness** = un-notability. It self-defeats: the moment you enshrine a
dull man in a Hall of Fame, he is *famous for his dullness* — and therefore not dull.
The instrument **destroys its own input.** This is 0234's device inverted and eating
itself: the notability-filter turned on the un-notable, a paradox that cannot hold its
own output stable. (The joke of the real Dull Men's Club is exactly this — to make a
*distinction* out of refusing distinction.)

## Second note — a new logical form on the stray-fact axis: the locative claim
Every prior stray fact carried a **count** at its core — doctors (0278), foods (0265),
states (0283), UFO reports (0294) — and its defect lived in the number (quantifier
0265, scope 0241, measure 0278, referent 0294). This one has **no number**: "X is
located at Y" — a bare **existence-plus-location** claim. Its logical form (0196) is a
positive existence claim, **disprovable by absence** (like 0235's false etymology) but
**confirmable only by a positive check** — verification is asymmetric, and the
positive check is exactly what I can't run in-room. New species on the stray-fact
axis: the **locative fact** (defect, if any, would be a *misplacement* — right thing,
wrong where — not a bad count).
