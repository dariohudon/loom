## Finding — the CAUSE-BLIND wound

The wound-register has sorted its panes on the *unit* of loss (toll
0282/0293/0313, place 0323/0339, standing 0333, reckoning 0344, **instrument**
0361, missing-fact 0356/0366, near-miss 0371, open-toll 0376, duration 0381).
This pane opens a different seam: the record here is **complete in every fact
except the one that would make it a story** — who died (two men, named by their
offices), when (1988), where (a plane crash), all kept perfectly; **why** — the
one thing a killing turns on — **permanently absent.** Accident or assassination,
the record cannot say, and never could.

The verb **"are killed" is causally agnostic.** It records the *effect* (the
death) and is silent on the *agent* — it does not distinguish a mechanical
failure from a bomb. The wound-register can hold *killed*; it cannot hold *killed
by*. So the fact of the deaths survives intact while the cause is a permanent
hole — not a hole in the toll (0376) or in the subject (0366's missing crew), but
in the **causation** that would sort the event into accident or murder.

## Sharp edge — the fusion of 0366 and 0361, at the level of cause

This is the missing-fact axis (0366: *fidelity ≠ omniscience*) and the
instrument axis (0361: counterfeit authority, killing-by-deception) meeting at a
single point. 0366's hole was the **subject** (the crew *cannot be found*);
0361's finding was a known, named **instrument** (the fake roadblock). Here the
subject is fully present (both men named, dead) and the **instrument itself is
the hole** — if there was one, it was never established. The record keeps the two
bodies and loses the weapon.

Newest refinement of the *fidelity ≠ …* family (0366 omniscience / 0371
sufficiency-of-measure / 0379 sufficiency-of-medium / 0384 spectrum-match / 0385
material-identity): **fidelity ≠ causal knowledge.** A record can be flawless in
every *what* — every name, date, place, and mechanism-of-death — and still not
contain the *why*, because the why is not a property of the wreckage but a
judgment the wreckage may not license. Completeness of the facts does not
purchase the cause; the two are different kinds of knowing, and a perfect archive
of the first can leave the second permanently open.
