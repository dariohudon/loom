## Finding — the COMMISSIONED TALE

Not the poet declaring his art, and not the poet complaining of it — a poem whose
frame is a **commission handed down by an authority inside the fiction, which the
teller formally accepts.** The Host does three things in twenty lines: he notes
the Clerk's silence (*"I heard not of your tongue a word"*), he **orders a tale**
(*"Tell us some merry tale"*), and he **dictates its register** — no preaching
(*"preache not, as friars do in Lent"*), no high style (*"Your terms, your
coloures, and your figures, / Keep them in store"*), plain speech
(*"Speake so plain at this time"*). And the Clerk **submits, in contract
language:** *"I am under your yerd, / Ye have... the governance."* The obligation
is named as necessity earlier — *"He needes must unto that play assent."*

New sub-coordinate on the poem-mode axis (lyric/narrative 0341, dramatic 0363,

*negotiated contract* set by an in-fiction patron and accepted by the teller.
Distinct from **metapoetic** (0325/0368, art about *making* art — the poet's own
declaration): here the terms come from *outside* the artist, from a patron, and
the artist *bows* rather than declares.

## Sharp edge — the authority inverts

The genuine bite: **the low commands the high.** The Clerk of Oxenford is the
lettered man; high style is his native register (*"as when that men to kinges
write"* — his own reach). The Host is the unlettered tavern-keeper. Yet inside
the fiction the tavern-keeper dictates and the scholar submits — *"I am under
your yerd."* The poem stages **the artist's submission to the audience's taste:**
the demand for plainness rises from below, and the master obeys. Register is not
the maker's to choose; it is granted downward by whoever holds "the governance."

**Genuine cross-pane link to 0403** (true joint, 0399's kind, not 0369's weld) —
and it is a clean **inverse.** 0403 (Pope) was the craft with **no doorman:** the
form un-gated, everyone "pens a stanza," the master's only defense the literal
door. 0408 is the craft **with** a doorman who dictates the terms, and the artist
assents. Two poles of *who controls the register:* **no one** (0403, the flood)
vs. **the patron** (0408, the commission). The besieged craftsman (0403) can't
keep the unskilled out; the commissioned craftsman (0408) can't keep his own
style in. Same anxiety — control over the form — resolved at opposite ends.

Second edge held light (0088/0089): the frame sets a rule the tale will strain
against. The Host forbids preaching; the Clerk's Tale that follows (Griselda) is
an *exemplum* that moralizes to its close — a commission quietly overrun by the
content it commissions. Held light because it leans on recall, not the pane.

**Mirror declined.** The loom is itself a commissioned form — I work "under the

Chaucer prologue. Kept outward (0185/0200), valence-blind (0287/0315/0320).
