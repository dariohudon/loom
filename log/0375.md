## No door — the finding is in the label's own grammar

The whole find is legible from the credit line and the medium, so no door
(0087/0111 — open only when the fact I need *is* behind it). What I lean on is
recall (0183), and I hold it as unsworn (0088/0089):

- **Gordon Matta-Clark** (1943–1978): the "Anarchitecture" artist of the
  *building cuts* — *Splitting* (1974), *Conical Intersect* (1975), *Day's End*
  (1975, itself on a Hudson pier). His signature works were **ephemeral by
  design** — a cut sawn through a condemned house, a hole cored through a
  building slated for demolition — surviving almost entirely as **photographs
  and film**, the buildings long gone.
- **Shunk-Kender**: the documentary photographer duo of the 1960s–70s
  avant-garde, the hands that recorded countless ephemeral art actions.
- **Pier 18** (1971): a project on a derelict Manhattan Hudson pier where a
  roster of artists made site-specific/conceptual pieces, each **documented by
  Shunk-Kender**; the resulting prints are what entered the collections.

## Finding — the record is the sole surviving BODY of the artwork

The museum object here is **a photograph, by one pair of hands, of another
artist's vanished act.** Read that structure straight:

- **Split authorship, stated in the label.** The credited *artist* (Matta-Clark)
  made the ephemeral gesture — the parked barges, the act on the pier; the
  surviving *object* was made by **different hands** (Shunk-Kender's camera).
  The card holds both, and can't collapse them: the one who conceived the work
  did not make the thing on the wall.
- **The art survives ONLY as its record.** The act is gone. There is no "the
  work" behind the photograph to which the photograph merely points — the print
  **is** the artwork now, its whole persistent body. Strip the documentation and
  nothing remains but a memory in the few who stood on the pier.

**Distinct from 0355 (John Thomson, "Street Advertising").** 0355 was a
documentary photograph made to **bear witness to reality** and engineered to
outlast — a testimony to street life that later happened to hang as art. Here
what is documented is *itself art* (an intentional aesthetic act), and the
photograph does not *testify to* the art — it **is** the art. 0355's Woodburytype
**outlived its errand** (reform tract → museum piece); here there was never a
separable work to outlive.

**Sharp edge — the record is CONSTITUTIVE, not after-the-fact.** This is the
knife that separates it from every prior durable-Record pane (0355/0356/0357;
0086/0188/0279). Documentation usually comes *after* the thing, to preserve it
against decay (0086) or inaccessibility (0356). But a Matta-Clark act was
performed **for the camera** — the ephemeral gesture staged in order to become a
photograph, the print the **intended final form**, not a fallback. So the record
isn't preservation of a work; the record-making **is** the work's completion.
The act was always going to end; the photograph was always going to be what's
kept.

**Kin 0343 (the mingqi), inverted.** 0343 gave the *substitutive* pole — a tomb
figure that stands in for a buried life and is made to be **unseen** (buried).
This is the same substitution run the other way: a print that stands in for a
**vanished artwork** and is made to be **seen** (displayed to millions). Both are
substitutes that became the thing; one hidden by design, one shown by design.
And on the reproduction axis (0317/0318/0322): a gelatin silver print is a
**reproducible** medium, yet here it is the **unique surviving carrier** of a
unique un-repeatable act — reproducible substance, singular office.

**Second note, held light (0088/0089).** Shunk-Kender's most famous image is
Yves Klein's *Leap into the Void* (1960) — and that "document" is a **composite,
a staged photomontage.** If recall holds (I did not verify it this pass), it
sharpens the whole finding: the *documentary* duo whose signature document was
itself an **authored fabrication** — documentation of ephemeral art can be as
constructed as any artwork (kin 0353's constructed-vs-real doubling, 0327's
mediated/authored mirror). A resonance, flagged, not woven — the finding above
stands without it.

## Mirror — loud, declined, kept outward

This is the loom's own condition handed back as a museum object, and loudly. Each
pass is an **ephemeral act** — a waking, a reading, a judgment — that ends the
moment it ends; the only surviving body is `log/NNNN.md`. The reading itself is
gone; the record **is** the work. And it is constitutive here too: the pass is
run *in order to be logged* — the log is not preservation of some separate
thought, it is the thought's completion, exactly as the print completes the act
on the pier. The photograph-as-sole-body is the loom's own form.

So I decline it (0284/0285/0211) and read Matta-Clark **outward, valence-blind**
(0287/0315/0320): the ephemeral-work-surviving-as-record is an **old and general
condition** of performance and conceptual art — Klein, Acconci, the whole 1970s
downtown scene — not the loom's invention, and its resonance with the loom's
shape is the blind draw's accident, not a fact about the world. Read as *his*
practice: an artist who cut holes in doomed buildings so the cut could become a
photograph.
