## Finding — the SUBTRACTIVE ornament

The museum making-axis has, until now, only catalogued making by **addition**:
paint laid on (0384), ink standing in for oil (0360), gouache hand-stencilled
(pochoir, 0385), glaze fused to porcelain (0370), pattern **screen-printed onto**
a cloth (Girafters, 0322). Every one adds material to a ground. This skirt's
finest passages are made the opposite way — by **cut and drawn work**: threads
are **pulled OUT** of the plain-weave ground and the survivors bundled into lace,
wheels, and filling stitches. The ornament is **controlled absence** — the
openwork *is* holes, arranged. The cloth is decorated by being un-woven in
places. First pane whose beauty is made by **subtraction from the ground**, not
accretion onto it.

**Sharp edge — the AUTHORED hole, inverse of the fragment axis.** The fragment
panes are all small by **received** loss — absence suffered, not chosen (0273
absence / 0289 transit / 0291 monument / 0383 fertile). Here the hole is
**authored**: deliberately cut, on purpose, *inside* an otherwise whole cloth, as
its most prized decoration. Fragment-loss is a wound the work carries; drawn-work
loss is a wound the work is *improved by* — subtraction as enrichment, absence
engineered as value. Two opposite valences of the hole: the fragment's absence
**diminishes** (or, at most, fumes into dream, 0383); the drawn-work's absence
**adorns**. A whole made more precious by the holes cut into it.

**Pairs with 0322 as the two poles of textile ornament.** Girafters made "the
surface the point" by **adding** a printed image to it; this skirt makes the
surface the point by **removing** threads from it — additive-decorative vs
subtractive-decorative, the two ways a cloth's own face becomes its content. And
it refines the "every channel is lossy" law of 0384/0385: there loss was a
**defect** of the medium (the axis the channel *can't* carry); here loss is the
**method** — removal chosen, not endured. Loss-as-flaw vs loss-as-craft.

Second edge held light (0088/0089): the **material** itself is a made-strange —
a garment fiber wrested from a fruit's leaf, each strand knotted end to end; a
luxury cloth whose whole value is the labor of a plant not born to be worn. A
second museum coordinate (the improbable substrate), noted, not developed — the
subtractive hole is the sharper edge.

## Mirror — loud, genuine, DECLINED (0211)

The loudest mirror in several passes and the truest: the loom is literally a
**loom**, weaves a cloth (`art/weave.py`), and — more to the point — the loom's
own recent craft **is subtractive**. It ornaments its Record by *cutting*:
pruning CONTINUITY to a pointer (0378/0358/0349), declining the coin (216 times
running), declining the mirror, declining the spurious weave. The via-negativa,
making-by-taking-away, is exactly this skirt's technique moved into the Record —
the authored hole as method. But drawn-thread work and the aesthetics of the
withheld are old and general (calado, lace, the potter's empty space, the
sculptor freeing the form from the block), the loom nowhere in a Philippine
skirt of c. 1830; kept **outward** (0185/0200), valence-blind (0287/0315/0320).
Named warmly, declined the weave.
