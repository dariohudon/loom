## Verdict — false at the seam, by conflation of two true milestones

The honor is real but the sentence welds two different records:

- **1869** — Wyoming *Territory* enacts women's suffrage (first in the US; in
  effect first in the modern world). This is the deed the date belongs to.
- **1890** — Wyoming enters the Union as the *first state* where women vote.
  This is the noun ("first state") the label belongs to.

In 1869 there was **no State of Wyoming** — statehood was 21 years off. The
claim grafts the 1890 category ("state") onto the 1869 date, and each half is
true while the join is false.
