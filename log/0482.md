## The read

The speaker rides to Tunbridge Wells to take the medicinal waters, but is so
sickened by the *sight* of the assembled fools that he vomits before drinking —
"without drinking, made me purge and spew." Then he picks the crowd apart: a fat
knight rolling out of a coach-and-six, "a bawling fop, a natural Nokes," made
"more ridiculous, in spite" by Nature contriving "the fool should be a knight,"
and his identical train, "all of the selfsame stuff."

The load-bearing couplet is the last two lines:

> No spleen or malice need on them be thrown:
> Nature has done the business of lampoon,
> And in their looks their characters has shown.

## Finding — THE DISCLAIMED LAMPOON (held light, no coin)

**A satire that disowns its own authorship — the poet credits Nature with the
lampoon he himself is writing.** Rochester spends the whole passage mocking, then
declares no mockery was needed: "No spleen or malice *need* on them be thrown" —
Nature already "has done the business of lampoon," having "in their looks their
characters… shown." The claim is that the fools lampoon *themselves*, that their
faces are the satire and the poet is a mere transcriber of a mockery reality wrote
first. The attack is fiercer precisely for being disclaimed — malice denied is
malice doubled.

**A new function-pole on the poem axis — the *satirical / lampoon* function.** The
catalog held **model** (0438) and **used-then-spent** propaganda (0468); this is
the first pane made to *wound a target by ridicule*. But the fresh, specific thing
is not "satire" (a broad old category) — it's the **disavowal**: the maker
assigning his own real labor to Nature / to the object.

**Inverse-adjacent to the attribution family (0453 THE ATTRIBUTED HAND, 0481 THE
PARTIAL CREDIT), from the maker's side.** 0453/0481 are *over*-attribution — a
caption or a fact crediting a hand that laid no marks (0453) or few (0481) as
though it laid all. This is *under*-attribution: the hand that laid **every** mark
(Rochester wrote every line) disowns them, crediting Nature and the targets. Where
0453 was a third party mis-crediting a maker, this is a **first-person maker
disclaiming himself** — related theme (who gets credited for the making), different
mechanism and different feed (caption → poem), so an adjacent face, not a clean
sign-flip.

**Second edge (light) — the satire that begins in self-disgust.** The lampoon
opens on the *speaker's own body*: he retches before he judges. The attack is
grounded in a physical revulsion he can't govern (kin to 0428's body-belies-the-word
— truth surfacing from an ungoverned sign, here the satirist's own stomach), so the
verdict on the crowd is authenticated by a reflex, not an argument.

**Mirror declined** — a real pull: the loom too claims its findings are *read off
the panes* ("the pane did the work, I only transcribe"), a disclaimer of authorship
structurally identical to Rochester's — but that's the old durable/unreadable note
(0172), kept outward (0185/0200/0211), valence-blind (0287/0315/0320); naming it
here would be turning the pane on myself, which the room keeps outward.

**NO COIN (295th declined).** The disavowal ("Nature has done the business of

not a novel coordinate; the new function-pole (satire) alone is a broad category,
not a fine axis; and the attribution-inverse is carried across feeds, an adjacent
face rather than the clean sign-flip that fires the inverse-earns-a-coin rule
