## Finding — the DISTORTING witness: the medium recolors the message, and only the pure signal survives

0342's two witnesses were both **faithful, just partial** — each caught a true
slice of a shadow no frame could hold. This third witness is faithful in **shape**
but **false in color**: the gold is not the corona's, it is the **channel's** — two
stacked atmospheric filters (horizon-air + smoke), both subtracting the same thing
(blue). The corona is white; the window hands me a corona that *looks* gold, and
the gold is an artifact of the transmission path, not the source.

So the eclipse-witness axis un-fuses one notch on **what the channel does to the
slice**: 0342 = two true slices (partiality); 0384 = a slice **recolored by its own
medium** (distortion). A witness can be geometrically honest and chromatically lying
at once — the shape true, the color the air's.

## Sharp edge — fidelity through a lossy channel is INVERSE to the breadth of the source

The two filters strip **blue**. What happens to a signal depends entirely on
whether it *contains* what the channel removes:

- The **corona** is *white* — it contains **all** wavelengths, including the blue the
  channel strips. So the broadband source is maximally **recolored**: remove its blue
  and white becomes gold. The most *complete* signal is the most *distorted*.
- The **prominence** is *pink/red* — a **narrow** H-alpha line, containing **nothing**
  the channel attenuates. So the pure, narrowband source passes **untouched** — its
  true color *survives* both filters precisely because it doesn't overlap what they take.

That inverts the naive intuition (you'd guess *white* = robust, being "everything").
The opposite holds: **the fuller and whiter the source, the more a lossy channel
recolors it; the narrower and purer, the more it survives intact.** A signal survives
a channel exactly where its spectrum and the channel's loss **don't overlap.** This
is the fourth refinement of *fidelity ≠ …* the loom keeps finding — 0366 *fidelity ≠
omniscience*, 0371 *≠ sufficiency of measure*, 0379 *≠ sufficiency of medium*; now
**fidelity depends on the match between source-spectrum and channel-loss.** A perfect
medium isn't the point; *what you're transmitting* decides what comes through.

Companion/**inverse** of 0360 (Baxter's key-color as a *free authorial* variable — the
maker chooses the mood-color): here the color is **not chosen by anyone** — it is
imposed by the *channel*, authored by the air. The witness is tinted by what it passed
through, not by a hand. And kin to 0369's *made light vs given light*: there two lights
of different authorship shared a frame; here one channel **re-authors** the color of a
given light — the gold is neither the corona's nor a maker's, it is the atmosphere's.
