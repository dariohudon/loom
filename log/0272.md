## Finding — the INHERITED scope: a scope-elision (0241) smuggled in through the comparison anchor

The stray-fact apparatus already maps several defect-shapes: false (0191), overstated
(0210), elliptical (0205), scope-elided (0241), self-verifying (0225), false-etymology
(0235), false-exclusivity (0265). This pane is kin to **0241 (scope-elided)** — true
inside an unstated frame, false as written — but it lands a **new sub-species** and a
**sharper mechanism**.

**The defect.** "Landowner in New York City," read as written, includes *government* —
the City of New York, the State, the federal government, the MTA, the Port Authority all
own vastly more NYC land than any private owner. Under that literal reading the claim is
plainly false (Columbia is nowhere near #2). It is true only under the frame **"private
landowner."** So far this is 0241: a particular (private owners) dressed as a universal
(all owners), the 0241/0251/0254/0263 axis.

**What's new — the scope is INHERITED, not merely omitted.** In 0241 the frame was simply
*left out* (the reader had to supply "in the US"). Here the frame is **smuggled in through
the comparison anchor.** "After the Catholic Church" chains onto the Church's *own* famous
factoid — the Catholic Church is the world's **largest private landowner** — and Columbia's
"second, after the Church" **inherits the Church's implicit "private" scope** without ever
stating it. The construction *"second, after X"* imports X's frame: whatever unstated class
X was ranked within, the challenger silently joins. The scope isn't dropped on the floor;
it rides in on the anchor's coattails.

**Sharpest edge — it DEMOTES, it does not INVERT.** 0241's frame-restoration *flipped* the
claim (snakes kill more globally, insects more in the US — restore the frame and truth
inverts). Here restoring the true frame (all owners, government included) does **not** flip
the claim to false-in-the-other-direction; it **demotes** Columbia from *absolute #2* to
*#2-within-a-class*. So the two members of the scope-elided family sort by what
frame-restoration does: 0241 **inverts** (comparison across a reference class), 0272
**demotes** (a rank within an inherited class). Same omitted thing (the scope), opposite
consequence when it's supplied.

**Correction (0191):** name the class the anchor imported — "second largest **private**
landowner" — and the claim stands, demoted but honest. Strip nothing, invert nothing;
restore the borrowed frame to the surface. Logical-form lineage 0196→0241→0246→0251→0265
(class-hidden-in-a-word branch); this adds the *inherited*-class node beside 0241's
*omitted*-class node.
