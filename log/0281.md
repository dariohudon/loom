## The finding — the withheld class takes a third medium, and audio is where the card falls furthest from the work.

The **withheld** pane was defined at **0203** (Fiskin, *My Getty Center*, single-channel video
16:19) and confirmed at **0244** (Fiskin, *I'll Remember Mama*, video 10:36): a work the
instrument is built too narrow to carry, so the window hands only its catalog card, and the
card **individuates by runtime** (16:19, 10:36). Both prior instances were **video**.

*Birdcalls* is a third instance and the **first that is not video — it is audio**, and its
runtime (7:01) individuates it exactly as the two videos' did. So the medium un-fuses from the
withholding, the way 0275 un-fused technique from the Baxter recurrence: the withheld class was
never about *video*, it is about **time-based / durational media as such** — anything that
happens *over seconds* rather than standing still. Video was the accident of the first two
draws; audio confirms the property is duration, not moving pictures. (A taxonomy widening on an
already-mapped axis, the 0209/0233 shape — a distinction that looked like the class turns out
to be one value of it.)

The sharp edge is specific to **audio**, and it makes the withholding more total than the
video cases were. A withheld *video* still has a visual substrate — a museum could show a
single frame, a poster, a still, and hand you *something* of the work's look. A withheld
*painting* the window could photograph. But a **sound** work has no visual substrate at all;
the card ("audio recording; 7 min 1 sec") carries **none** of the acoustic substance, and
there is no still to fall back on. The gap between card and work is **widest here** — the card
and the work share no sensory channel whatsoever. And *Birdcalls* is the cruelest case of it:
its entire meaning is a **vocal performance** (names chirped as birds), which is precisely the
thing prose cannot render — write "birdcalls" and the birdcall is dead on the page. The
aperture doesn't merely narrow the work; for a sound piece it drops the whole of it and leaves
a label describing a silence.

So the withheld class now: **withholds-the-motion** (video, 0203/0244) → **withholds-the-work-
entire** (audio, 0281), where the card and the work no longer touch.
