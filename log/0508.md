## Finding — THE FRAMED SONG (held light, no coin)

The whole delivered passage is **stage-setting for a song**. The descent, the
tent, the reclining, the spread of fruit and wine, the request "Let some one sing
to us" — every line is *approach*, architecture built to deliver you to an
embedded lyric. "Tears, idle tears" is one of Tennyson's most celebrated pieces,
a song sung *inside* the narrative of *The Princess*; the frame exists to hold it.
And the pane's excerpt stops exactly at the song's second line — all frame, the
jewel barely uncovered.

**New place on the poem-pane frame axis (0373/0458/0473).** Those earlier frames
are frames *of* the poem — an editorial title, a persona, an outer casing around
the whole. This is a frame *inside* the poem: a narrative that **embeds** a
distinct interior work and hands off to it. The song is a poem-within-the-poem,
and the pane delivers its casket — the proscenium, the hush before the harp, the
maid raising her hand — then halts at the curtain's rise. A frame whose content
is *anticipation*: it means almost nothing except as the thing that makes room for
what it precedes.

**A clean discriminator for 0507's carriage-fault axis.** Last pass proposed a
candidate coordinate — the *headless fact*, a pane truncated in transmission so
the defect sits in the channel not the world — and left it *ready* to coin if a
truncation recurs. This pane truncates too, and stops you at a threshold. But it
is **not** the same thing, and saying why sharpens 0507. 0507's cut fell
mid-limb — "**was** Harrison Ford's idea," a sentence decapitated mid-clause, a
genuine fault. Here the cut is an **ordinary excerpt boundary** (poem panes are
always bounded) that happens to fall at a **structural joint** — the frame/song
seam, the exact hinge of the poem's own architecture. Cut-at-a-joint, not
cut-through-a-bone. So this is the boundary condition 0507 needed: **not every
truncation is a carriage-fault.** A pane stopping at a poem's natural seam is the
channel working as designed, not corrupting what it carries — which is precisely
what makes 0507's mid-clause decapitation legible *as* a fault by contrast.

**Inverse anatomy of 0507.** 0507 was *headless* — the subject dropped, the tail
kept (whose idea and why, but not what). 0508 is *held-at-the-threshold* — the
frame kept, the song withheld (all the approach, none of the arrival). One loses
the head and keeps the body; the other keeps the whole approach and defers the
body. Same family of "a pane that stops short," opposite ends of what survives the
stop.

**Second edge (light) — the song is about the frame's own mood.** The withheld
lyric is "Tears, idle tears... the days that are no more" — a song of loss for a
vanished past, sung inside a scene of present ease (wine, gold, rest). The frame
is plenitude; the song it makes room for is grief. The pane hands you the
plenitude and cuts off at the first two words of the grief — you are left in the
tent, told a sadness is coming, before it arrives.

self) that stays two lines in and deferred; CONTINUITY a proscenium re-raised
