## Coin — THE ABOLISHED INSTRUMENT (276th)

An on-this-day event whose content is the **permanent abolition of a standing
instrument of harm** — not an infliction, and not the mere *ending of an act*,
but the removal of the **means**. Devil's Island (the *bagne* of French Guiana)
was not a battle or a disaster; it was a *machine*, a place built to inflict
confinement continuously across roughly a century. The event is that machine's
permanent shutting: no future person will be sent there, because there is no
longer a there to be sent to.

**Clean inverse of 0426 (THE CESSATION).** 0426 (the Iran–Iraq ceasefire)
coined "harm ending, not happening — a subtraction," and flagged its own weakest
seam: a ceasefire *"is agreed," a speech act, a promise not a fact* — bilateral,
reversible, dependent on continued mutual will; harm loosed can resume. Devil's
Island is the opposite **mechanism of ending**: a *unilateral* act by which a
sovereign destroys its **own** instrument, so the harm cannot resume — not
because a promise holds but because the means is gone. Ending-by-**promise**
(fragile, mutual, promissory, 0426) vs. ending-by-**abolition** (permanent,
unilateral, material). 0426 stops the *use*; 0461 removes the *capacity*. Same
family (harm ending) as 0426, but the clean inverse mechanism it explicitly did
not cover — and in this loom's grammar the clean inverse of a coined coordinate
earns its own coin (cf. 0410/0405, 0428/0418).

**Inverse of 0441 (THE CARCERAL WOUND)** on the carceral axis. 0441 (the Xá Lợi
raids) was a *cell filled* — harm carceral, ongoing, in the living, thousands
arrested. 0461 is the whole apparatus *emptied and abolished* — the same domain
at the opposite pole (harm-instrument in use / harm-instrument decommissioned).

**Second edge — the direction of mercy (light).** Nearly every on-this-day pane
points *back* (its toll is its content) or, like the cessation, borrows magnitude
backward from the years it ends. This one does that too (a century of the colony's
cruelty is the weight behind "closed") — but its meaning runs **forward**: the
mercy is in the futures it forecloses harm from, the sentences that will now never
be served. A cessation is retrospective (toll behind, none ahead); an abolition is
*both* — it closes a past ledger and empties a future one. Sharp against 0431 (THE
FESTIVE TARGET), where a killing sited at a *beginning* converted beginning into
ending: here an *ending* (a closure) is itself a beginning, a shutting that opens.
