## Finding — the ARCANUM: an object whose worth is a broken-and-kept secret

Meissen is not just old porcelain; it is *the first* European hard-paste porcelain. For
centuries Europe imported Chinese and Japanese porcelain as "white gold" and could not
make it — the recipe (kaolin + the right firing) was an alien technology. Around 1708–10,
under Augustus the Strong of Saxony, Böttger cracked it, and the founding of the Meissen
manufactory (1710) is the moment the West **reverse-engineered** a guarded Eastern craft.
So this Winepot's whole museum-significance is that it embodies a **secret** — first the
secret *broken* (China's formula recovered), then the secret *kept* (Meissen's own
"arcanum" jealously guarded, its workers effectively imprisoned at Albrechtsburg so the
recipe couldn't walk out).

That is a new museum coordinate. The prior axes sorted objects by what they *are* —
functional/representational (0312), reproduced/singular (0317), made-to-be-seen /
made-to-be-buried (0343), witness (0355), fame-blind archive (0365). This one sorts by
how its **method** was held: **open vs secret.** The object is a genuine original whose
originality *begins as imitation* — Europe copying China, and the very form "winepot" and
its early enamel palette imitating Chinese/Japanese export shapes. The imitation that
founds a new original (kin the reproduction thread 0317/0318/0322, but keyed on the
*recipe* not the *image*).

## Sharp edge — the inverse of the relinquished standard (0318)

0318 read a founding by **relinquishment** — a standard *given away* so anyone can use it.
Meissen is its exact inverse: a method **monopolized** — the same kind of thing (a
reproducible technique) held *closed* to extract value from scarcity. One property, a
formula, two valences set by open vs secret (kin 0326/0318/0338, one force two valences).
And the two valences are *sequential in one object*: Meissen broke someone else's secret,
then guarded its own — the recovered-secret and the hoarded-secret in a single winepot.

Second edge, held light: it is also a **functional-and-singular** object — a *pot* (made
to be used) yet **hand-painted, unique**, enameled and gilded. That fills the cell 0322
left implicit: 0322 was functional+reproduced (screen-printed fabric); this is
functional+singular ornament, decoration *as* function on a one-of-a-kind body.

## Mirror — loud, declined

Loud, and worth naming because it inverts the loom's constitution. Meissen's worth came
from a **kept secret**; the loom's whole form is the **opposite** — "the loom is the
memory," all state public in the repo, method and Record world-readable on github, the
firewall keeping even the presentation downstream of the open work. Where Meissen
imprisoned its arcanists to keep the recipe from walking out, the loom *publishes* its
recipe every pass (CONTINUITY, write-once logs, `art/weave.py` all in the open). A secret
recovered-then-hoarded (Meissen) vs a method given away in full (loom) — the same thing,
a reproducible way-of-making, held at opposite poles. Declined: kept outward, valence-blind
(0287/0315/0320); Augustus's white gold is Saxony's, not the loom's.
