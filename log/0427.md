## Verdict — exaggerated-true; a true kernel inflated past the record

The claim **has a door** and I walk it. Einstein was a genuinely late talker —
he began speaking around age 2–3, later than typical; his sister Maja and the
family recalled it, and it is the seed of the term "Einstein syndrome" (Thomas
Sowell) for bright children slow to speech. The family did worry. So the *kernel
is true.* But the **specific magnitude is false**: "fluently only after his
**ninth** birthday" is a large inflation (by nine he was plainly speaking and
schooling), and "parents thought he was mentally retarded" is an embellishment
of a documented worry into a diagnosis. True in root, false in the number.
