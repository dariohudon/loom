## Finding — THE ENACTING PROLOGUE (held light, no coin)

**A paratext whose thesis is "beholding transforms the beholder," and which
performs that transformation on its own reader in advance, by the very mechanism
it describes — so the frame is a working sample of the goods it advertises.**

Pope's stated claim is a mimetic theory of virtue: tragedy makes you *"be what
[you] behold,"* mends the heart by the act of witnessing greatness. But the
prologue does not merely *state* this and hand you off to the play. It runs the
mechanism on you first. The closing rhetorical questions — *"What bosom beats
not...? / Who sees him act, but envies...? / Who hears him groan, and does not
wish to bleed?"* — are not questions; they are a **presumed universal assent**
that manufactures the response it pretends to poll. By declaring that everyone
feels the patriotic ardour, before anyone has seen a scene, the prologue primes
the theatre to feel it. **Form performs content:** the frame does to the audience
(readies the heart to be moved) exactly what it promises the framed work will do
(move it to virtue). The prologue is a miniature demonstration of its own thesis
— you are already *being made what you behold* by the poem that says beholding
makes you so.

**Two folded moves.** (1) **The discriminating frame** — it ranks the coming
tragedy above lesser kinds by contrast (*vulgar springs* of pity/love vs. a
*more generous cause*), an advertisement that pre-argues its product is of a
higher genus; and the framer is a **third party** (Pope, not Addison), esteem
conferred by a peer, not by the work about itself. (2) **The presumed assent as
engine** — the rhetorical question is the priming instrument: it does not ask, it
*assigns* the feeling and dares dissent, so agreement is produced by being
assumed.

**Sharpest vs 0534 (THE FORESPOKEN LIFE) — two anticipations, different target.**
0534 (Browning, *Protus*) narrates a subject's greatness *before it is lived* —
anticipation of the **object**, praise running ahead of the deed. 0559 anticipates
the **audience's own response** and, by naming it, *causes* it — anticipation that
manufactures its subject rather than merely preceding it. **Forespoken-object vs.
enacted-response:** both describe before the fact, but 0534's account could be
wrong (Protus might disappoint), while 0559's cannot fail in the same way — the
declaration is self-fulfilling, the priming *is* the effect.

**Vs 0557 (THE APPOINTED OPPOSITION) — the honest cousin of a fraud.** 0557's
staged election *supplies* the response (an opponent) it needs to look legitimate,
a counterfeit of its own precondition. 0559 supplies the response (assent) it
needs too — but here the manufacture is **avowed and benign:** a prologue is
*expected* to prime; no one is deceived that the audience's tears were spontaneous.
**Manufactured-and-laundered (0557) vs. manufactured-and-declared (0559)** — same
move (produce the reaction that validates you), opposite candour.

**Vs the frame-of-the-poem axis (0373/0458/0473):** those frame *themselves*; this
frames **another, absent work** (the play is not here, only its threshold) — a
paratext, kin the "design *for*" of 0556 (a plan on paper for a thing not present),
here a design-for-the-audience's-heart.

**Edge (light) — Roman drops from British eyes.** The prologue's proudest claim is
that it moves tears for a *cause*, not a person — "such tears as patriots shed for
dying laws," a grief with no beloved in it, feeling routed away from the private
(love, pity) into the civic (a falling state). The mechanism it enacts is thus
doubly ambitious: it does not just prime a feeling, it prescribes *which* feeling
counts as the higher kind.
