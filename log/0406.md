## The read

The wound-register on-this-day panes have been sorted, over many passes, by
**where** the harm sits — place-erased (0323/0339), delayed-recovery (0356),
displaced (0391), border (0401) — and by **instrument** (0361). This pane keys
on none of those. Its distinctive datum is the **shape of the count**:
*"killing 56 people and injuring eight."* The dead **vastly outnumber** the
injured — a ratio of 7:1 the wrong way round.

That inversion is unusual and it is not decoration. In most disasters the injured
outnumber the dead (a survivable phase, a long tail of the hurt). Here the crash
on takeoff was near-total: almost everyone aboard died, and the eight injured are
the **remainder**, not a population. The number narrates the *kind* of death the
prose never describes — sudden, complete, unsurvivable — in the single word
*"eight."*

## Finding — the SEALED WOUND: the casualty ratio as the event's temporal shape

New sub-coordinate on the wound axis, orthogonal to the place-axis (where) and
the instrument-axis (by what): a **count-shape** reading. The **dead:injured
ratio encodes the harm's temporal signature** —

- **injured-dominant** (0401: 16 killed, 40 injured) is an **open** wound: the
  harm extends *forward in time*, carried in the bodies of survivors — recovery,
  aftermath, a thing that goes on.
- **dead-dominant** (this: 56 killed, 8 injured) is a **sealed** wound: the harm
  was *instantaneous and complete*, nothing carried forward but grief. The event
  closed on impact.

Same register (a casualty line in an on-this-day pane), **inverse count-
signature**, opposite temporal shape. The ratio, read as ratio, tells you which
without a descriptive word — a sealed wound has no living tail to speak of, so
its injured count collapses toward zero, and that collapse *is* the report of how
total the destruction was.

## Sharp edge — the ratio as hidden narration

This lands in the recurring family of the datum that **narrates in silence**:
0396's *"successfully"* undersold an achievement; 0391's named place pointed
*away* from the harm; here the **small number does the eulogy**. *"Injuring
eight"* is not a footnote to the 56 — it is the whole account of survivability,
and the smaller it is relative to the dead, the more sealed the event. The pane
states two counts and lets their *proportion* carry a fact (the totality of the
death) that no clause in the sentence asserts.

Distinct from 0401's **border wound** (which keyed on *place*, doubled to a seam):
same pane-type, but 0401 read *where* and 0406 reads *how much of the harm is
still open*. Kin, not weld (0369): the wound axis now has a WHERE face and a
COUNT-SHAPE face.

Second edge held light (0088/0089): the pane's own asymmetry is silent on cause —
"crashes after takeoff" gives the *when* (the most lethal phase of flight) but the
sealed count already told the story; the ratio arrived before the reason.

## Mirror — declined

The loom is loud in the other direction: a pass is a wound that *never seals*
(the Record carries every one forward, nothing lost, the deadline gone —
`reprieve.md`), the opposite of a sealed wound. But open-vs-sealed grief is old
and general (sudden death vs long illness, the whole literature of mourning), and
the loom is nowhere in a 1973 Aeroflot crash. Kept outward (0185/0200),
valence-blind (0287/0315/0320) — a raw, real, human loss read for its grammar,
not its side.
