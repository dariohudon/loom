## The pane

Thel views the Worm — an infant "helpless & naked: weeping," that "can'st not
speak, but thou can'st weep." The Clod of Clay raises "her pitying head," bows
over the weeping infant, "her life exhal'd / In milky fondness," then speaks the
core:

> O beauty of the vales of Har, we live not for ourselves…
> My bosom of itself is cold, and of itself is dark,
> But he that loves the lowly, pours his oil upon my head…
> And says; Thou mother of my children, I have loved thee
> And I have given thee a crown that none can take away.
> **But how this is sweet maid, I know not, and I cannot know**
> **I ponder, and I cannot ponder; yet I live and love.**

## Finding — THE AVOWED UNKNOWING (held light, no coin)

**A poem whose delivered content is a *positive truth held together with the
speaker's explicit avowal that she cannot and will not comprehend it*.** The Clod
possesses the fact — she *is* loved, she *has* a crown none can take, she lives
and loves — and in the same breath disclaims all capacity to understand it: "I
know not, and I cannot know / I ponder, and I cannot ponder." Knowledge held as
*fact*, refused as *comprehension*. The gift is fully received and its ground is
sealed to the very one who holds it.

**New place on the poem-pane axis — the speaker's *epistemic stance toward her
own condition*,** distinct from every mapped member: address (outward
0418/0423/0428/0433/0477 · reflexive 0498 · function 0438/0468/0482), frame
(of-the-poem 0373/0458/0473 · embedded 0508), withheld content
(sealed/undeliverable 0513), figure (0488), restorative naming (0493). Those
turn on *whom the poem speaks to*, *what casing surrounds it*, *what it withholds
from a reader*. This turns on **what the speaker can know of her own truth** — a
truth possessed and unpenetrated at once.

**Sharpest against 0513 (THE SEALED GLANCE, held), same withholding family, opposite
axis of closure:** 0513 = content withheld from the *other* — a seal "not to be
broken till after I am dead; and then vainly," undeliverable to the reader/beloved.
0518 = the ground withheld from the *self* — the speaker holds the gift openly
(nothing hidden from us) yet cannot break the seal on *her own meaning*.
**Other-opaque vs. self-opaque:** 0513's message is legible to no one; 0518's
message is legible, only its *why* is sealed, and sealed precisely to its bearer.

**Second edge (light) — unknowing as a mode of faith, not a lack:** "yet I live
and love" makes the incomprehension load-bearing, not a deficit — living exceeds
knowing; the lowly is loved by "he that loves the lowly" without the lowly ever
grasping the cause. The Clod's coldness ("of itself is cold… dark") is the
precondition, not the obstacle, of the oil poured on her head.

**Third edge (lighter) — stacked unknowings:** Thel closes the passage weeping
"Alas! I knew not this, and therefore did I weep" — a *second* unknowing (the
witness's did-not-know) framing the *first* (the Clod's cannot-know). The pane
delivers a small architecture of ignorances: one professed from inside the gift,
one confessed from outside it.
