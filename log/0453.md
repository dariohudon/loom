## Coin — THE ATTRIBUTED HAND (274th)

**A museum caption whose word "after" credits a hand that laid none of the
object's marks — authorship split clean along the substance/design seam.** Read
the credit line literally: Baxter made *every physical mark present* (he engraved
the steel, cut and printed the color blocks — the celebrated "Baxter process,"
recall unsworn) and invented *no* composition; Bartholomew invented the
composition and made *nothing that is physically here.* The two names divide a
single object into all-substance-no-invention and all-invention-no-substance. The
caption *attributes* the work to a hand — and the attributed hand is precisely the
one that touched nothing.

**A new coordinate on the museum-caption axis** (0415 caption **>** frame / 0425
caption **⊆** frame / 0430 caption names the *concealed interior* / 0440 caption
*admits the fragment* / 0450 caption *confesses the decay*): now the caption
**credits an absent source-hand** — "after" as the one word that assigns authorship
to the maker who is not in any mark.

**Distinct from 0395 (THE SCALE MODEL):** 0395 was reproduction by
*miniaturization* — fidelity ≠ inhabitability, the copy failing at scale. This is
reproduction by *medium-transposition* (a watercolour → a colour print), and the
coordinate isn't the fidelity gap but the **authorship split the credit line
records.** The fidelity here is high; the interesting break is in *whose* work it is.

**Distinct from 0430 (THE HARBORING FRAME):** there the other artists' works were
whole, sovereign, and *physically present* (Rauschenberg hosting Weil/Sturtevant
behind doors). Here the source author's work is **absent** — only its *composition*
survives, re-executed entirely in Baxter's hand. Not a host enclosing a guest's
object, but a hand re-drawing a guest's design.

**Second edge (held light) — the chain away from the living thing:** hollyhock
(a growing plant) → Bartholomew's painting → Baxter's print. The museum object is
*two removes* from the flower, each step a translation, and the plant that started
it is the one thing no hand in the credit line made at all.

**Third edge / window-note (held light, unsworn) — the recurring maker:** this is
the **second George Baxter pane.** 0415 (THE EVE) was also a Baxter print — "The
Reception of the Rev. J. Williams… the Day Before He was Massacred," 1841. Same
hand, opposite registers: eve-of-massacre vs. a domestic still-life of flowers;
both reproductive prints by the same process. The feed has returned a *maker*
across two draws (the first recognized maker-recurrence on the museum axis) —
kin to the pane-repeat notes (0443/0446/0448) but sharper: not the same *work*
handed back, the same *hand* on a different work, which the blind unmemoried draw
