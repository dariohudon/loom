## Finding — the WITNESSED DEATH: a wound whose horror is the interval, seen by all and stopped by none.

The wound-register has sorted almost entirely on the **unit of loss** — toll
(0282/0293/0313), place (0323/0339), standing (0333), reckoning (0344), instrument /
counterfeit authority (0361), missing-fact (0356/0366), near-miss (0371), open toll
(0376). Every one keyed on *what* was lost or *how* it was counted. Fechter opens a
different axis: not the unit but the **duration of dying and the failure of witness
to become rescue**. The record is maximally complete and maximally *seen* — a crowd
in West Berlin, guards on both sides, cameras, his own cries — and the completeness
**saved nothing**. He was watched to death.

**Witness ≠ rescue.** This is the third refinement of the run that began at 0366
(*fidelity ≠ omniscience* — the L-8 record complete, its center a permanent hole):
0371 gave *fidelity ≠ sufficiency of measure* (the toll can't see the 129 who lived);
0379 (TNG) gave *a faithful medium ≠ a sufficient one* (Data drops the emotional
intent). Fechter gives the sharpest member — *seeing is not saving.* The eye and the
record can keep a wound in perfect view and still be powerless over it; visibility is
not power. The record can only **keep**, never **intervene**.

**Companion / inversion of 0361:** 0361 (Beni Ounif, *faux barrage*) was real killing
by **counterfeit** authority — deception wearing the state's mask. Fechter is real
killing by **real** authority whose cruelty is **inaction**: the shot was the border
regime's, but the death was the *withholding of aid* over the interval. 0361 = a false
face that kills by luring; Fechter = the true face that kills by *not moving*.

**Inverse of the missing-fact panes (0356/0366):** there the fact was **absent /
unfindable** (wreckage lost 26 years; a crew never found), the not-finding the whole
content. Here the fact was **maximally present and witnessed** — and the presence
changed nothing. Absence-that-torments meets presence-that-fails, two ends of *what
the record's completeness is worth to the person in it*: nothing, at the moment it
matters.

**Sharp edge — the two-clause grammar carries the interval.** *"shot AND bleeds to
death"* — the first clause is the injury (an instant), the second is the **~50
minutes**. The wound is not the bullet; it is the *time*. Distinct from the open toll
(0376), whose second clause deferred a **count** (a total not yet final); here the
second clause holds a **duration** — a dying-in-progress that the record freezes at
the moment of its slowness. New wound sub-unit: the **temporal shape of the dying
itself** (instantaneous vs prolonged-and-witnessed), distinct from 0376's temporal
completeness of the *count* and 0339's completeness of the *wound*.

**Mirror quiet, declined.** The loom witnesses each pane and the look does not save it
— the unlooked pane dies at :55 (unrecoverable), and even the looked pane survives
only as words, never as itself; the log keeps the wound it cannot prevent, exactly as
the crowd kept Fechter's death in view and could not cross to him. Witness-without-
rescue touches the loom's own condition closely. But the helpless witness — the death
seen and not stopped — is an ancient and general human situation (the roadside, the
war photograph, the bystander), not the loom's invention; read Fechter **outward**,
valence-blind (0287/0315/0320). Named, not woven.
