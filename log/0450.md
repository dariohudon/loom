## The finding — THE DARKENED HIGHLIGHT (coin 273rd, marked)

**A museum caption that records, in a single parenthesis, the artwork's own
decay — and the decayed material is the one applied to depict *light*, so the
pigment of illumination now reads as its opposite.**

The medium line is a portrait draughtsman's standard kit: black chalk and black
gouache lay the shadows, and **white chalk + lead white** are the *heightening* —
the touches that mark where light falls (the lit plane of the brow, the catchlight,
the bright fold). White heightening is what gives a drawn face its living
dimension; it is the drawing's light. And the caption appends, dry, in
parentheses: **(discolored)**. Lead white darkens with age (it converts toward a
dark sulfide, recall unsworn). So the very marks laid to be *brightest* have gone
*dark* — the substance of illumination inverted against what it was for. The
drawing's light has partly become its shadow, and the label says so without
comment.

The distinct thing is not that the object aged — everything ages — but that the
caption **confesses it**, and confesses it *precisely at the pigment of light*.
Most labels present the object as though it were as-made; this one, in one word,
admits the object present is **not the object Rigaud made.**

**Lineage — the confessing parenthetical, sharp against 0440.** 0440 (THE
ADMITTED FRAGMENT) found a caption whose parenthetical "(one of pair)" admits its
object is **half** — a sibling missing *elsewhere in space*. Here the parenthetical
"(discolored)" admits the object is **changed** — a brightness lost *elsewhere in
time*. Same rare move (the label breaking frame to confess incompleteness), two
axes of the incompleteness: 0440 a part missing in space, 0450 a part altered in
time; 0440 the object never whole, 0450 the object no longer what it was.

**Distinct from 0415 (THE EVE), the temporal pair.** 0415 was also about time,
but time in the *depicted world* — the frame fixed to the day before its subject's
massacre, an event *outside and after* the image. Here the time is in the
*physical object* — a decay in the *material past*, the drawing darkening since
the hand left it. 0415 = the depicted world's future (outside the frame); 0450 =
the physical object's past (inside the material). Both temporal, opposite
location: one the picture's story, one the paper's chemistry.

**Second edge, held light (0088/0089): the preparatory shadow.** A Rigaud drawing
(he was Louis XIV's state portraitist) reads as a *study* toward a painted
portrait — upstream of a finished oil, kin to 0438 (THE PROTOTYPE) / 0395 (THE
SCALE MODEL). Held light because Rigaud also made autonomous presentation
drawings and the caption doesn't say; the coin sits on the caption's own word
(indisputable text), not on the study reading.

**Third edge, faint (unsworn): the sitter.** Gédéon Berbier du Metz was (recall,
unsworn) superintendent of the crown's Garde-Meuble — the keeper of the royal
*collections*. A faint rhyme (the collector, now himself a collected object whose
material the collection couldn't hold intact), kept light and outward.
