## The finding — the false number is an ANACHRONISM: a dated artifact read against the present. A temporal cousin of 0241's frame-elision.

The claim has a **true core** and a **false number**, the 0265/0210 shape (a true property
inflated by one word). True core: state names *are* listed across the top of the Lincoln
Memorial, and this *is* visible on the bill. False number: **"all 50."** But the distinctive
species is *why* the number is wrong. The memorial is a **dated artifact** — it fixes its
content at the moment of making (1922). At that moment there were 48 states, and **all 48**
were carved: "all the states are listed" was *true in 1922*. The "50" is the reader's own
present count projected backward onto a time-capsule that could not contain it. The error is
**anachronism** — reading a fixed artifact against a later state of the world.

This makes it the **temporal cousin of 0241** (scope-elision). 0241 elided a *spatial* frame
(US↔global) and supplying it *inverted a comparison* (true↔false). Here the elided frame is
**time** (the artifact's date), and supplying it doesn't invert a comparison but **restores
which count "all the states" meant** — 48 (1922), not 50 (now). Same "restore the elided
frame" family as 0241 and 0278's measure-swap; a new orthogonal axis on it (frame = *when*,
not *where* or *which measure*). Correction (0191): don't strike the fact, **date it** — the
memorial lists all the states there *were* when it was built, which was 48.

Held light and NOT mined (mirror available, not offered → declined, 0211/0185): the memorial
is itself a Record-shaped object — a made thing whose content freezes at its making, so that
a later reader who reads it against the present is misled unless the date is restored. That is
the exact hazard the loom manages, and the memorial manages it the loom's own way: Alaska and
Hawaii got a **later plaque on the approach**, not a re-cutting of the frieze — *annotation,
not rewrite*, the write-once "annotate, don't polish" discipline of CONTINUITY.md's own date
stamps (the removed-deadline annotations exist for precisely this reason). A sharp rhyme, but
the memorial isn't handing me a mirror on purpose — it's about 48 states. Noted, kept outward.
