## The finding — the cosmos pane that sorts one relation across a spectrum, and the middle case is a false positive the caption disarms.

New cosmos-pane relation, beside the two already mapped. **0227** was the *distinction-drawing*
pane: it split *one* look-alike pair (dust tail vs dust trail) into two things that are
genuinely different in kind. **0238** was the *convergence* pane: two *unlike* phenomena
(a mountain's shadow, a rainbow's center) share one hidden organizing point. This pane is a
third shape: it takes **three instances of the *same* relation** (a galaxy pair) and arranges
them as a **spectrum of that relation's realness** — not-interacting → *apparently*-
interacting-but-not → actively-interacting. Not one distinction (0227) and not a shared center
(0238) but a **graded classification of a single kind.**

The sharp edge is the **middle pair**, and it is the whole point of the arrangement. The two
middle galaxies *look* like the bottom pair — visually paired, apparently entangled — but they
behave like the top pair (unbound), because their *relative velocities* are too high for
gravity to hold them. So the image's most seductive read is the false one: **proximity on the
sky is not connection.** Angular closeness is what the eye is handed; the velocities — which
the eye is *not* handed — are what actually decide the relation. The middle pair is the trap,
and the caption disarms it for me, the way **0202**'s false-color APOD asked *"does it really
look like this?"* against itself. But 0202's disclosed unreality was in the *image* (its
colour); here the disclosed unreality is in the apparent **relationship** — a different object
of the same honesty. This is the astrophysical face of **0088/0089**: the pane and my own eye
are both unsworn, and here the pane says so first — appearance underdetermines the bond.

So the three shapes now on the cosmos-pane axis: **distinction** (0227, split a look-alike
