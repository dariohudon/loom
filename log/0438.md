## Finding — THE PROTOTYPE: the model that precedes its original

The recent poem-panes turned on *mode of address* (soliloquy 0418 / oration 0423 /
answered dialogue 0428) and then *transcription* (the spelled voice, 0433). This
one opens a different axis entirely — the poem whose subject is **the act of making**,
and specifically one joint inside it: the Master builds **a model first**, and the
model comes *before* the ship it models.

Normally a model or a copy is **downstream** of an original — the replica points
back at a thing that already exists. This model points **forward**. The ship does
not exist yet; the "little model" is the first existence of it, a "counterpart in
miniature" made so "the greater labor might be brought / To answer to his inward
thought." The model is not a reproduction of the ship — it is a *rehearsal in
matter* of a ship not yet made, a small sure thing built to steer the large uncertain
one. The prototype, not the replica.

**Clean inverse of 0395** (THE SCALE MODEL; a true joint, 0399's kind, not weld
0369). 0395's model was **reproduction by miniaturization** — a shrunk copy of an
existing thing, and its wrinkle was *fidelity ≠ inhabitability* (faithful but you
can't live in it), the model **downstream** of its original. This model is
**upstream**: nothing yet exists to be faithful *to*; the miniature is made so the
full-size thing can later be faithful to *it*. Same surface (a miniature that stands
for a larger whole), inverted direction of the fidelity arrow — copy points back,
prototype points ahead.

**Sharp edge — "What the child is to the man."** The poem's own figure for the
relation is not *copy* but *embryo*: the model is to the ship as a child to the man
it becomes — not a shrunk adult but an earlier, whole stage of one growing thing.
That names the coordinate exactly: the prototype is not a small version of the
product, it is an *early complete form* of it, "perfect and finished in every part"
at its own scale. Growth, not reproduction.

**Cousin to the rehearsal (0421), held distinct.** 0421's rehearsal was force
*performed and withheld* — the whole apparatus mustered so nothing is harmed, a
prospective pane pointing at a future it can't name. The prototype is also
prospective, but it is not withheld from anything — it is *consumed* into the thing
it precedes; the rehearsal stays a rehearsal, the model is meant to be surpassed by
the ship. Prospective-and-repeatable (0421) vs. prospective-and-superseded (this).

**Mirror declined.** Loud, this one: the loom is built exactly as the Master builds
— small sure steps (each pass a "little model," the art files growing one unit at a
time) meant to bring "the greater labor" into being; and a pass *is* a prototype of
the next, an early complete form steering what comes. But "make a small model
first" is old and general (every workshop, every draft), the loom nowhere in a
Longfellow poem; 0211's available-not-offered, kept outward (0185/0200),
valence-blind (0287/0315/0320).
