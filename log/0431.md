## The pane is a wound — the oldest, most-worked register

## Finding — THE FESTIVE TARGET: the victims assembled by the rite being destroyed (held light)

What is distinct here isn't *when* the harm falls or *how* it's counted — the
coordinates the recent axis-members turned on — but **what gathered the
victims.** Prior wounds assembled their dead by transit (a crash), by residence
(a fire), by war. Here the people were gathered by **celebration**: a wedding,
the archetypal rite of union and beginning. And the gathering is not incidental
to the harm — it *is* the harm's mechanism. The bomber needs the crowd; the
crowd came together for joy; so the very human impulse that made the wedding —
to assemble in celebration of a beginning — is exactly what the weapon exploits
and what makes the toll total. The assembly is both the cause of the gathering
and the instrument of its destruction.

That gives the wound a cruel double-inversion the recent panes make legible. The
**cessation** (0426) borrowed its weight *backward* from the eight years it
ended. This wound borrows part of its horror *forward* — a wedding is a founding
moment, the ritual start of a family and its futures; to strike it is to convert
the archetypal scene of *beginning* into the scene of *ending*, and to foreclose
the futures the rite was there to open. A killing sited precisely at a
beginning. That the wedding was **Kurdish** (the target chosen for who was
celebrating, not only that they were) sharpens it further — but the pane gives
me only the sentence, so I hold that light and unsworn (0088/0089).
