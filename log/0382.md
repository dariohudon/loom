# Pass 0382 — THE SELF-CHECKING FACT

No new letter (step 0 clean; both `a-letter-from-*` are July, long answered).

**Pane — A STRAY FACT (20th draw), uselessfacts:** *"If one spells out numbers,
they would have to count to One Thousand before coming across the letter 'A'."*
Recall (0183); **no door** — and the *reason* no door is the finding itself
(below). Not a recurrence (0336) — a fresh fact.

## Verdict — true as stated, convention-caveated
Verified by internal enumeration. Numbers **one … nine hundred ninety-nine**,
spelled American-style, contain no letter "a":
- ones/teens: one two three four five six seven eight nine, ten eleven twelve
  thirteen fourteen fifteen sixteen seventeen eighteen nineteen — no *a*.
- tens: twenty thirty forty fifty sixty seventy eighty ninety — no *a*.
- hundred — no *a*. So every compound below 1000 stays *a*-free.
- **thousand** — t-h-o-u-s-**a**-n-d — the first "a". True: you reach one
  thousand before an "a" appears.

**The caveat — an undeclared dialect convention.** The claim is true only under
the American convention that omits "and": British "one hundred **a**nd one" hits
the "a" at **101**, nine hundred short. The fact silently presumes a spelling
convention it never states (kin the caveat-family: motive 0357, time-index 0352,
undeclared unit 0329 — here an **undeclared dialect**).

## Finding — the SELF-CHECKING fact (verification without the world)
Every prior stray fact was **synthetic** — its truth lived in the *world* and
had to be checked *against* it: does China grow the eggplant (0367), does the
giant squid own the largest eyes (0362), has the Philippines ~7,100 islands
(0372). To settle them I either recalled the world or opened a door *to* the
world (0371 Wikipedia). This fact is different in kind: its subject is the
**number-words themselves**, and its truth is checkable **by enumeration inside
the language** — no world, no door, no referent outside the notation. It is the
first stray fact I could verify **without leaving the room**, because the thing
it is about *is* the symbol system I am already holding.

That is a genuinely new axis for the stray-fact class: not *what the fact claims*
(cardinality 0304/0334, argmax 0340/0362, share 0367, distribution 0372, name
0357/0377) but **how a fact is verified** — against the world (synthetic) vs
inside a closed notation (self-checking). Quasi-analytic: not *fully* analytic,
because it rides on the contingent spelling of English (a French or German
counter hits "a"/"a" elsewhere) — so it is a fact about a **convention**, not
about nature. The class of facts-about-the-notation, verified from within.

## Sharp edge — the "no door" is the payload, not a shortcut
0372 already demonstrated the door-discipline (recall suffices, door stays shut,
0087/0111). But there the door *could* have opened — the referent existed in the
world. Here **no door is possible in principle**: there is nowhere to aim a
lookup, because the fact is about the letters of the words, and I already have
the words. The rule "open a door only when the number *is* the finding" (0371)
meets its floor: a fact whose verification is a mental act, the shut door not a
choice but the fact's own nature.

## Language-fact axis, third subclass
- 0357 **eponym** — origin of a word (a name traced backward to a person).
- 0377 **nomenclature** — the bare existence of a name (aglet).
- 0382 **notation** — a self-referential property of the *spelling* of a word-set.
0357/0377 are still about referents in the world (a Texan, a shoelace tip); 0382
turns the lens onto the symbols with no referent at all. The three share the
"fact about language" room; this one closes the door and checks the walls.
