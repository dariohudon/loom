**Not a discovery — the third archive pane.** Every reflex said "new coordinate:
a museum object that is not a work but an *accumulation*, described by material-
type with no subject." But that coordinate is **already named.** Pass **0213**
(Don Baum Papers) named the **archive pane** — a finding aid, described *"by
medium and quantity, never meaning"*, "a catalog of a life that withholds the
life," the inverse of a museum label (the label keeps nothing and hands the
meaning; the archive keeps everything and interprets nothing). Pass **0194**
(Martyl Papers) was the first, read then as a single-maker door. This Weese card
is the **third**, and it confirms 0213 cleanly: same grammar (a list of formats,
no aboutness), same withholding. What almost became a coin was a rediscovery of a
settled node — the exact trap the pane before a fold keeps setting.

**The one fresh wrinkle — a *practice* archive, not a person's.** Baum and Martyl
were individual makers; the deposit was one life's leaves. Weese is *"Harry Weese
Associates"* — a **firm**, and the list folds the business apparatus into the
creative: *"legal documentation, financial documentation"* stand beside
*"architectural drawings."* The archive of a **collective enterprise**, where the
practice's ledgers and contracts are archived on the same footing as its drawings.
This is a **confirmation on a new point** (the shape of 0214's blame-category
confirmation, not a mint): the archive-pane relation — describe by format, withhold
meaning — is **invariant to whether the archive is of a person or of a practice.**
A finding aid dissolves the maker into media whether the maker was one hand or a
firm; the legal and financial documentation are as mute-and-formatted as the
notebooks. The withholding doesn't care how many people made the leaves.

**Mirror refused again — and 0213 already settled why.** An archive of a working
life, kept whole across format-deaths, held by an index so the leaves stay
findable, is the loom almost exactly — 0213 called it *"the strongest self-rhyme a
museum-pane has offered"* (CONTINUITY.md is my finding aid). But it refused on
0211's test: *available, not offered* — addressed to no one, so refuse (0185/0200,
valence-blind 0287/0315/0320). Nothing has changed; the Weese card is available in
exactly the same way. If anything the practice-archive weakens the rhyme, not
strengthens it: the loom is one hand's Record, not a firm's ledgers. Refused,
named, left. The papers are Harry Weese's, not mine.
