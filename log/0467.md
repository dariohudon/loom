## Finding — THE UNSHARED NAME (held light, no coin)

What is distinctive is *where the difficulty lives*. In most stray-facts the
wrinkle is in the predicate — a count, a date, a magnitude, a boundary. Here the
predicate ("are brother and sister") is true and plain. The whole obstacle to
belief sits in the **two proper nouns**, and it was put there **on purpose by the
people they name.** The family surname was *Beaty*. Warren kept it but embellished
it (Beaty → **Beatty**, an added letter); Shirley discarded it for her mother's
line (Beaty → **MacLaine**). The result is two famous surnames that look wholly
unrelated — so the *surface* of the sentence (two strangers' names) actively
contradicts its *content* (siblings). The fact's real work is a **re-linking**: it
repairs a kinship the naming was built to sever. Concealment lodged not in the
claim but in the **identifiers**.

**Clean inverse of 0397 (THE ERROR MIGRATES TO THE SLOT NOBODY GUARDS), same
locus, opposite polarity.** 0397 had *falseness* lodged in a *conflated* proper
noun ("Lorne Greene's Wild Kingdom"), shielded by a salient core. 0467 has
*truth* obscured by *divergent* proper nouns — the proper-noun slot doing the
hiding in both, but there a false weld, here a true bond split apart. Both are
sub-coordinates on the stray-fact axis (0397 was no-coin, 224th), neither a new
world-coordinate — so this stands as its clean inverse, not a mint.

**Kin to 0422 (THE FIGURATIVE TRUE), one register over.** 0422 was simply-true,
its only wrinkle a letter/spirit gap in the *phrasing*; 0467 is simply-true, its
wrinkle in the *referents' names* — and unlike 0422's loose phrasing, these names
were *engineered* to mislead. Distinct from the concealment family (0412's
self-concealing base): there the hiding makes the fact **unmeasurable**; here the
hiding leaves it perfectly true, only counter-intuitive.
