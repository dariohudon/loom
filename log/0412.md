## Verdict — unverifiable, spurious precision → probable-false as stated

The claim wears a lab coat: **"Thirty-five percent"** — a specific figure,
precise to the point estimate, that says *someone counted.* No one did, and
no one can. There is no register of "people who use personal ads for dating"
crossed against "already married." A lookup finds the sentence repeated, never
a source — kin the **un-registered record** (0402): a real-ish quantity that
was simply never measured. But this pane is sharper than 0402 in two ways, and
they are the finding.

## Finding — the LIAR'S CENSUS

New node on the verification-mode axis (0382 analytic / 0402 un-registered /
0392 open-floor / 0407 dateless). The prior nodes were **shape** claims — an
argmax (0402), a floor (0392), a timeless comparison (0407). This one is a
**precise point estimate**, and its falseness lives in the *decimal itself:*

1. **False precision.** "35%" (= 7/20, a truthy fraction) is specific enough
   to sound sourced over a base no one tallies. The lie is not in a wrong
   number but in the *existence* of a number — precision impersonating a
   measurement. Distinct from 0402's superlative (which at least admits it's a
   ranking); this hands you a decimal for a thing with no denominator.

2. **The self-concealing base — the sharp edge.** The attribute being counted
   (*secretly married while posing as single*) is **precisely what this
   population hides.** A married person using a personal ad is, by definition,
   the one lying about marital status; so the very datum the statistic claims
   to have measured is the datum its subjects actively suppress. The
   measurement's target *is* the concealment. 0402's population was un-counted
   **passively** (nobody bothered); here it is un-countable **actively** — a
   census of liars taken from the liars' own answers. A statistic about
   deception, sampled from the deceivers, cannot be honest. The precision is
   the deception wearing its own subject as a costume.

New sub-coordinate: **the liar's census — a precise figure over a base that
conceals the exact attribute being figured.** Not un-adjudicable because
un-registered (0402), but un-adjudicable because *the register would be built
from the concealment it's trying to pierce.*

## Sharp edge — measured ≠ measurable; the count corrupts at the source

0402's door was impossible because the population was never registered
(external gap). Here the door is impossible because the instrument is
**inside** the deception: any honest survey of "are you secretly married?"
asks the secret-keepers to unkeep the secret. The class of un-adjudicable
claims now splits a third way: **analytic** (0382, nothing to look up) /
**un-registered** (0402, nothing *was* looked up) / **self-concealing**
(0412, the looking would have to defeat the hiding that defines the sample).
The falseness isn't authored (0393) and isn't stale (0407) — it's
**structural to the act of measuring**: you can't count what defines itself by
not being counted.

**Second edge, light (0088/0089).** The pane is dated twice, kin 0407. The
*unit* decayed: "personal ads" names the newspaper personals column — a
pre-internet artifact; by 2026 dating migrated to apps, so the population the
sentence quantifies barely exists in the form named. The figure is a fossil
percentage over a fossil medium — false precision about a thing that has
mostly stopped happening the way the noun says. Held light, leans on recall.
