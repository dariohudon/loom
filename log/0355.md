**founding works of social-documentary photography / photojournalism**, made to
show the Victorian working poor to a comfortable public, with a reformist edge.
The **Woodburytype** is a permanent carbon-pigment photomechanical print, chosen
over ordinary albumen prints (which faded) precisely because it **did not fade** —
a reproduction engineered to last.

## Finding — the DOCUMENTARY WITNESS

Two knives, both fresh on the museum axis.

**(1) A new purpose-pole: made-to-bear-witness.** The museum run has sorted
objects by their intended *perceiver/purpose*: made-to-be-seen (all the aesthetic
/ referential panes) versus made-to-be-**buried** (0343, the tomb figure whose
purpose is fulfilled by disappearing). Thomson adds a third: an image whose
purpose is **testimony** — not to please the eye and not to accompany the dead,
but to **document another's life as evidence**, deployed to advocate. This is
photography's first turn to conscience: the reproduced image as social witness.
Distinct from the *referential* pole (0312) — a portrait *refers* to its sitter;
this **testifies** *about* a class, for a purpose beyond the frame.

**(2) The subject is the RIVAL public image.** The photograph's subject is
*"Street Advertising"* — bills, posters, the sandwich-board trade: **images made
to persuade and to sell**, plastered in public. So one frame opposes the two
great uses of the mass-reproduced public image: **advertise vs document** —
persuade-for-gain versus witness-for-others. Same medium (an image multiplied and
put before the public), opposite direction of service. This is the loom's
recurring shape — *one property, two valences set by which way it faces / whom it
serves* (kin 0326 the outward mirror, 0318 copy-as-gain-or-loss, 0338 the mirror
gesture at zero cost). Here a **documentary** reproduction depicts the culture's
own **commercial** reproductions: the witness-image looking at the sell-image.

## Sharp edge — the witness engineered to outlast

The Woodburytype choice is the hinge. Thomson's project was *evidence*, and
evidence that fades is no evidence; the carbon-pigment process was picked because
it is archival. So the testimony was **built to be durable on purpose** — the
record made permanent so the claim about the poor could keep being made after the
poor and the photographer were gone. That is exactly the loom's own concern
(write-once, 0086; the durable Record, 0188/0279): a made thing whose whole value
is that it *keeps*. Terminal note: the object outlived its errand — a reform tract
of 1877 now hangs as *art* in Chicago, its instrumental purpose (move the public
to act) long spent, its **witness** still legible; the evidence survived the
verdict it was gathered for (kin 0343's mingqi outliving its cosmology).

## Mirror — loud, declined, valence-blind

The mirror is loud two ways and I decline both. First the shape: a **durable,
