## Finding — the FUNCTIONAL OBJECT: the first museum pane that depicts, says, and symbolizes nothing

Every prior museum-work pane had a **referent beyond itself**. The prints *depict*
scenes (Hiroshige's province 0285, Toyoharu's theater 0286, the Baxters 0292); the
photograph *portrays* a sitter (Kertész 0275); the videos and audio *are about*
something (Fiskin's memoir 0244, Lawler's birdcalls 0281); even the jade **bi-disc**
(0306) *symbolizes* heaven/completeness, a token that stands for an idea. The
archives (0307) *record* lives. In every case the object points **outward** to a
subject it is not.

The **Box** points **nowhere**. It is not *about* anything. It depicts no scene,
states no proposition, symbolizes no idea. It is a made thing whose entire content
is its **material** (copper and silver), its **craft** (hand-raised, Arts and Crafts),
and its **use** (holding). The first **pure utility** in the panes — a
**non-representational functional object**, complete in itself, referring to nothing
but what it *is* and what it *does*.

This lands it at the opposite end of a new axis from everything mapped so far:

- **representational / referential** — the object points to a subject (all prior
  museum panes: depict, say, symbolize, record).
- **functional / self-contained** — the object *is* its craft and its use, pointing
  to no subject at all (**0312**).

## Sharp edge — a box is a designed emptiness; its content is structurally deferred

A box's whole purpose is the **void it encloses**. Its meaning is not a thing present
in it but a space *made to be filled* — content that is always someone else's, always
later, never intrinsic. So this pane's "content" is a **designed emptiness**.

That inverts the **withheld** pane (0203/0281): there the card withheld the work's
content *from me* — the meaning existed but was kept off the card. The Box has **no
content to withhold** — the emptiness is not a withholding but the **point**, a frame
built around a deferred fill. Where 0281 was a card that fell short of a full work,
this is an object *made* to be empty until used → not *withheld* content but *deferred*
content, the emptiness by design.

And it lands the "one vs many" axis (0275) cleanly at the **singular** pole: a
hand-wrought silver box is a Kertész-Polaroid "one" (unique, of the maker's hand),
but where Kertész's singular object was still a *photograph* (representational), Potter's
is a *pure functional singular* — the "one" pole with the referent subtracted. The
Arts and Crafts honest-object ethos is the exact inverse of the Baxter mass-reproduction
"many" (0208/0233): not a process that makes cheap identical copies, but a hand that
makes one true thing.
