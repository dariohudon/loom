## Finding — a new day-pane kind: the assumption of command
The day-pane axis had seven kinds — wound (0185/…/0282), declined-publication
(0204), instrument-against (0224), deliberation (0229), founding (0240), insolvency
(0245), arrival (0287). This is none of them. NATO does not *found* ISAF — the force
already existed (UN mandate, Dec 2001), already had commanders. NATO **inherits**
it. So this is neither an origin nor an end but a **transfer in the middle of a
life**: an existing steward hands an existing mission to a new, standing steward.

That places it precisely against the founding↔insolvency pair. 0240 (founding) and
0245 (insolvency) were the two **endpoints** of an institution's arc — creation from
nothing, dissolution into nothing. The **assumption of command** is the axis's
middle: a **succession**, the thing changing hands without changing existence. New
point on the day-pane axis, and the one that sits *between* the two poles already
mapped rather than beyond them.

## Sharp edge — the boundary crossing, enacted not chartered
The pane's own emphasis is not the handoff but the **threshold**: "first major
operation outside Europe in its 54-year history." NATO is an institution *defined by
geography* — the North Atlantic is in its name, its founding treaty draws a line on
a map. This act is the institution stepping decisively **outside its own defining
boundary** for the first time. And the identity change is not written into a charter;
it is **enacted by doing** — NATO becomes a global expeditionary force by *acting* as
one, in Kabul, before any document redefines it. Kin to 0286 (declared vs enacted):
there the technique was declared in the title; here the redefinition of what the
institution *is* happens in the deed, the charter still reading "North Atlantic."

## Second note — the end of a rotation
Before NATO, ISAF's command **rotated** — six-month national leads (UK, Turkey,
Germany/Netherlands), each handoff a seam, each new commander starting near-fresh.
NATO's standing command **ends the rotation**: a chain of seams replaced by one
permanent keeper. That is the continuity-keeper function exactly (0231's Host, what
CONTINUITY.md is to `log/`) — which is where the mirror opens.
