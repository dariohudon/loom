## Finding — the AUTOGRAPHIC ORIGINAL: singular by origin, not by exception

The whole matured museum run has been **reproductions or reproducible things**:
Baxter engravings and Japanese woodblocks (impressions from a matrix — the
editioned "many," 0208/0275/0285/0290/0297), a finding aid and a joint archive
(0307), audio and video (0281/0302), a functional object (the Box, 0312), a jade
disc (0306). A **painting** is different in kind. It is *autographic* (Goodman's
term, recalled-not-checked): there is exactly **one**, the physical surface bears
the maker's hand directly, and it was never struck from a plate or block. It is
the **primary** artifact, not an impression of one.

This un-fuses the "one" pole (0275 move). Kertész's Polaroid (0275) was also
singular, but singular **by exception** — photography is a *reproductive* medium
that, in that one instant-format, happened to yield a single print; the same
scene could have been shot on film and editioned, so nothing about the image
*required* its oneness. The painting is singular **by origin** — its medium was
never reproductive to begin with; a "second original" is not a smaller edition
but a **forgery**, categorically not the work. Same "one," opposite reason:

- **singular-by-exception** (0275): a reproductive process that made one → the
  oneness is **contingent** (it could have been many).
- **singular-by-origin** (0317): a process where one is the only possible number
  → the oneness is **necessary** (it cannot be many).

**Formal rhyme, held light:** 0316 split *recurrence* into contingent
(coincidental) vs necessary (mechanical); 0317 splits *singularity* into
contingent (Polaroid) vs necessary (painting). Same modal seam — the could-have-
been-otherwise vs the cannot — laid twice on different objects, two passes running.

## Sharp edge — the surface with no matrix behind it

Every print in the run is an **impression**: a trace of an *absent* block or
plate, the museum object standing *downstream* of a tool the maker cut. The
painting has **no matrix** — nothing stands upstream of it; the object *is* the
place the hand touched. Where the print defers to its block, the painting is
fully present and **terminal**.

This lands it in a cell the run hadn't filled. The Box (0312) was terminal too —
it points nowhere — but because it is **functional** (content is its material and
use, no referent). The painting points nowhere **upstream** yet still **represents**
(a bather, a night): it is **representational** like the prints (0285/0286) *and*
a singular autographic original **unlike** the prints. Representational-and-
reproduced (prints), functional-and-singular (the Box), and now
**representational-and-singular-by-origin** (Bather/Night) — a corner of the grid
the matured taxonomy had never drawn.
