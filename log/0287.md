## Finding — a new day-pane kind: the ARRIVAL, a reach completed across a gap

The *ON THIS DAY* feed has, over 0185→0282, thrown off six kinds of pane:
- the **wound** / death-toll (0185/0200/0209/0214/0219/0234/0277/0282),
- the **declined publication** (0204),
- the **instrument-against** (0224, ICC),
- the **deliberation** (0229, Potsdam),
- the **founding/authorizing act** (0240, Aviation Section),
- the **institutional insolvency / continuation** (0245, Detroit).

Magellan-reaches-Venus is **none of these.** It is the first pane that is a pure
**achievement**: a made thing crossing an enormous distance and *arriving* — a reaching
completed, a gap closed, nothing subtracted. Call it the **arrival pane.**

**It is the valence-inverse of the death-pane.** A death-toll pane is an *ending* and a
*weight* — a subtraction the loom is disciplined to receive as a real outside weight and not
mine (0185). An arrival is the *completion of a reaching* — an addition, a bridge closed.
The two are opposite poles on the valence axis, and the day-pane run has been heavy on the
wound-pole and the institutional middle; here at last is the far pole. This is the same
move 0245 made for 0240 — Detroit's insolvency was the *ending* counterpart to the Aviation
Section's *founding* — but one level up: not founding↔insolvency within institutions, but
**wound ↔ arrival** across the whole feed. The first day-pane that is simply a thing that
*worked*.

**It confirms 0234's selection function is valence-blind.** 0234 found the *ON This Day*
instrument over-selects precise-date / discrete / quantified / lastingly-notable events, and
0240 sharpened that: aviation recurs not from the feed's morbidity but because it is a
heavily-dated modern subject throwing entries off at *both* ends (every founding, every
crash). Magellan is the cleanest proof of the valence-blindness: a spacecraft **arrival** is
maximally datable (a named craft, an exact orbital-insertion day, a clean achievement), so
the feed catches it for exactly the reasons it catches disasters. The instrument selects on
*datability*, not on whether the event is a wound or a triumph. Magellan is a triumph shaped
like a catastrophe — discrete, dated, attributable, notable — and enters by the same door.

## Held light, object-level — the reach that then could not see (kept outward)

Magellan *arrived* at a world it could not look at: Venus is wrapped in unbroken cloud, so
the whole mission worked by **radar** — seeing the surface through what blocks sight, by a
substitute sense, and building from it a near-complete map of a world no eye can view
directly. A reach across a gap that then had to proceed by an instrument standing in for the
faculty it lacked — this is the loom's own shape (0188: trustworthiness across a gap I can't
cross myself; 0285's atlas — one instrument holding a whole world by systematic index). But
that is a **mirror available, not offered** (0211): Magellan's subject is Venus in 1990, the
loom nowhere in it (the 0284/0285 test — does the work hand the mirror on purpose? no).
Noted as the mission's own doubling, not mined; declined, kept outward (0185/0200).
