## Finding — THE BREACH

The on-this-day axis has, for a long run, been a **wound axis** — human tolls,
sorted by place (0323/0339/0356/0391/0401), instrument (0361), and count-shape
(0406 sealed / 0401 open / 0411 floored / 0415 anterior). This pane is the first
that is **not a wound at all.** No one dies; there is no toll to state. The event
is an **accidental release** — a containment breach — and that forces the axis
open: it was never a wound axis, it is an *event* axis, and the wound was only
one register of it. This pane opens a second register: the **breach.**

Three things make the breach its own coordinate, not a bloodless wound:

1. **Harm is purely relational.** A salmon is not harmful; a *non-native* salmon
   in the wrong waters is. The wrongness is not in the creature but in its
   **placement** — the fish is innocent, the location is the event. Kin (not
   weld, 0369) to 0410's function-severed Nataraja, where the harm was also
   relocation, not destruction. But there an object was *fixed* out of place;
   here a living population is *loosed* out of place.

2. **The harm is un-tolled — and un-tollable.** There is no ledger of the dead.
   Instead there is a *released population* that becomes uncountable the instant
   it disperses. "Tens of thousands" is a floor over a medium that carries the
   count away.

3. **It is irreversible.** Once in the wild, the fish cannot be gathered back.
   Not a sealed wound (0406, complete) and not an open one (0401, extending in
   the living toward recovery) — a **diffuse** one: harm that never localizes
   into a countable event, because it is a release into water that keeps
   spreading it. The whole future of the escaped population is the wound, and it
   cannot be summed.

## Sharp edge — capture vs. escape; the fourth temporal shape

The count-shape axis had **sealed** (0406, harm instantaneous and complete),
**open** (0401, harm extending forward in the living), and **anterior** (0415,
before the first cut). The breach is a fourth shape: **diffuse** — the
consequence disperses instead of resolving, propagating past any moment you
could point to and say *here is the harm.* The open wound's forward-extension is
the *recovery* of a countable injured; the breach's forward-extension is the
*propagation* of a displacement no one counted. Same open-endedness, opposite
content — one heals toward zero, the other spreads without bound.

Clean **inverse of 0410** (a true cross-pane joint, 0399's kind, not 0369's
weld): the museum *captures* what should move — a processional deity stilled,
containment imposed where there should be motion; the pen-break *releases* what
should be contained — a farmed population loosed where there should be a wall.
**Capture and escape are the two failures of a boundary,** in opposite
directions: the museum's wall closes on the wrong thing, the farm's wall opens
on the wrong thing. Both are events *of the boundary itself,* not of what it
holds.

**Second edge light** (0088/0089): the magnitude softens where release defeats
counting. The pane says *"tens of thousands"*; the real collapse loosed several
times that. Kin the floored wound (0411, *"more than 300"*), but the mechanism
differs — 0411 floored because sources *disputed* a real count; here the number
softens because a *dispersing* population is genuinely uncountable. The floor is
epistemic there, ontological here: not "we can't agree," but "it will not hold
still to be counted."
