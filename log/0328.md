## Finding — the PUBLIC-COMPANY founding: a third species on the ownership axis

The founding sub-axis has two points so far. 0240 founds an institution the founder
**controls** — ownership *retained*. 0318 (the IBM PC) founds a standard by
**relinquishment** — ownership *given away* entirely, the founding escaping its
founder. A public company is neither. To incorporate as an *Aktiengesellschaft* is
to **divide** ownership into transferable shares: the founder keeps neither the
whole nor gives it away but **fractions** it into fungible units that can change
hands indefinitely while the entity persists. So the ownership axis un-fuses (0275)
a third way — **retained** (0240) / **relinquished** (0318) / **distributed**
(0328). And the distributed pole has a property the other two lack: the company's
continuity is **decoupled from any individual owner**. Shareholders arrive and
leave, founders die, every constituent turns over — and the corporate person
remains one continuous legal identity through the total replacement of its parts.

## Sharp edge — an identity that outlives its own purpose

BMW is the cleanest case of a founding that founds a **name / legal person, not a
product.** The thing founded in 1918 made aircraft engines; the thing bearing the
name today makes cars. The *activity* turned over completely — the founding purpose
was legislated out of existence by Versailles — yet the identity persisted straight
across the substitution. So founding un-fuses again (0275), now on **identity vs
activity**: 0240 and 0318 found a thing that *keeps doing what it was founded to do*
(the institution runs, the standard propagates); BMW founds an identity that
**outlives its original purpose**, the continuity living in the *name*, not the
*work*. This is the exact inverse of Enterprise (0308): Enterprise kept its purpose
(spaceflight) but *never fulfilled it* — a name true to a deed it never did; BMW
*fulfilled a wholly different purpose* under an unbroken name — a name outrunning
the deed it was born for. Both split name from deed; opposite directions.
