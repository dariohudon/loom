## Finding — the OPEN TOLL, a count that declares itself unfinished

The wound-register has always recorded **settled** events. Even the durational
ones had *closed* as facts: the tolls (0282/0293/0313) were final numbers, the
razed places (0323/0339) stood razed, Cyprus (0339) was an *ongoing state* but
a settled one (the occupation in force, a known quantity). Every prior day-pane
kept a thing that had **finished happening**, and recorded a number that was
**done counting**.

St. Thomas Aquinas keys to none of those. Its distinctive fact is in the
pane's own second clause: *"killing 61 people **with 59 others missing.**"* The
count is explicitly **not final** — the 59 are in a category that is neither
dead-confirmed nor alive, a **known unknown embedded in the number itself**.
The unit here is not toll-completed but **toll-in-suspension**: a record caught
*mid-resolution*, its true total openly deferred. The pane is the wound written
down before the counting stopped.

## Sharp edge — a LARGE pending absence beside a partial whole

Distinct from **0366** (L-8, the Ghost Blimp): there the missing was the
*whole* content — one small terminal hole (two men) at the center of an
otherwise complete record, and the not-finding was **permanent** (the crew
never found). Here the missing is **nearly equal to the confirmed** (59 against
61) and **coexists** with a real toll — not a hole in a whole record but **half
the record still open**, the count frozen while the event is still settling.
0366 = a small terminal absence *in* a complete record; St. Thomas Aquinas = a
large **pending** absence *beside* an incomplete one.

Companion to **0356** (SAETA, wreckage found 26 years late): 0356 named the
*interval of not-finding*, closed eventually by discovery — a record read
**after** a delayed knowing. This pane is the record read **during** the
not-yet-knowing, *inside* the interval, before any closure arrives. Same axis
(how long until the wound is known-complete), caught at opposite moments:
post-resolution vs mid-suspense.

Kin **0371** (AIRES 8250) inverted on **certainty**: 0371's uncertainty
resolved to a near-total *survival* (129 lived — an unknown that became a
known, and a deliverance). Here the fate of 59 is **unresolved** in the record
itself — could be dead, could be pulled alive from the water — a suspense that
the pane does not close. One a known-large deliverance; one an unknown of equal
size to the confirmed dead.
