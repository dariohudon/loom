## Finding — the TWINNED LEDGER: one wound denominated in two incommensurable currencies at once

The wound-register has sorted the *unit* of loss many ways — toll
(0282/0293/0313), place (0323/0339), standing (0333), reckoning (0344),
instrument (0361), displaced (0391), and the run of event-axis wounds since
(0401…0491). Every one of those measures the harm in a **single** denomination.
This pane hands two at once and joins them with a calm "and": **"killing 47 and
causing an estimated $15.6 billion in damage."** A body count (47 human lives)
and a money count ($15.6B) set side by side as coordinate members of one list of
consequences — two ledgers, incommensurable, summed grammatically though they
can never be summed arithmetically. New wound sub-coordinate: the wound measured
in **twin currencies** — lives and dollars given equal weight in one sentence.

Money has entered the Record before, but only ever as a *subject*: 0245
(Detroit's Chapter 9, ~$18–20B) is an **institutional insolvency** where the
money **is** the event and no body is counted. Here the money rides
**alongside** death as a second measure of the *same* harm — the dollar figure
is not the wound but its co-currency. That is the fresh thing: not money-as-event
but money-as-second-denomination-of-a-body-count.

## Sharp edge — the two figures are asymmetrically knowable

The tell is "an estimated." The death toll is given **flat** (47 — a countable,
in principle exact, each one a named person) while the damage is **hedged**
($15.6B "estimated" — a modelled aggregate, precise to three figures yet openly
approximate). So the twinned ledger is also a mismatch of *epistemic register*:
one currency is enumerable and certain in kind, the other is estimated and
certain only in scale. The sentence yokes an exact-in-principle count to an
avowedly-approximate one and reads them off in the same breath — kin to the
hedged-figure family (0487 chosen-slack floor, 0533 undersold magnitude), but
here the hedge sits **beside** an unhedged count of the dead, the two
truth-textures touching.

## Sharpest against 0391 (THE DISPLACED WOUND — Hurricane Diane, 1955)

The nearest neighbor, and instructive by contrast. 0391 anchored a storm to a
**point** (Wilmington landfall) whose fidelity pointed *away* from the harm
(deaths hundreds of miles up-country) — a wound displaced in space and mechanism.
This pane does the opposite with place: it names no point at all but a **whole
region** ("the United States east coast"), spreading the event across the entire
seaboard rather than misfiling it at one node. Where Diane's harm was *displaced*
from a precise name, Irene's is *diffuse* under a name that refuses precision. And
where 0391 kept the loss in one denomination (a floored death count), 0537 splits
it into two. **Point-displaced-single-currency (0391) vs. region-diffuse-twin-
currency (0537)** — two ways the storm-wound resists the record's single
anchor and single ledger.
