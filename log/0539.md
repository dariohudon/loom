## Finding — THE ANSWERED ARGUMENT (held light, no coin)

**The first poem-pane whose *form* is a two-voiced disputation — an argument
and its titled rebuttal set as opposing speakers, the poem staging both sides of
a case rather than occupying one speaking stance.** Every prior poem-pane spoke
from a single position: it *addressed* (outward 0418/0423/0428/0433/0477 ·
reflexive 0498), *did* something (function 0438/0468/0482), *framed itself*
(0373/0458/0473 · embedded 0508), *withheld* (0513), *confessed unknowing*
(0518), *audited itself* (0523), *disavowed its authorship* (0528), or *praised
in advance* (0534). All monologic. This pane is **dialogic** — "The Suicide"
makes the case, "Nature's Answer" answers it, and the poem is the *collision*,
not either voice. **New poem-pane axis place — structure/voicing: the
disputation / two-voiced debate** (the poem as staged argument, both sides
given).

**The sharp content-move (mirror-independent) — the imposed life recast as an
accountable deposit.** The Suicide's argument is a **consent** argument: a life
begun without being asked-for ("No question was asked me") may therefore be
refused; existence is framed as a yes/no trial, and *dying* is simply answering
NO. Nature does not rebut on the merits of the gift. She **changes the category**
— from existential to *actuarial*: the life was not given but *lent*, "sent" to
be "returned as 'twas sent," and before you may answer NO you must **inventory**
what you were issued (innocence, hope, health, genius, scope) against what you'd
hand back (guilt, lethargy, despair). "Make out the invent'ry; inspect, compare!"
The question "may I die?" is answered with a *ledger* — the debtor's accounting,
existence as an entrusted stock to be reconciled. The right to refuse the gift is
met not with "the gift is good" but with "the gift is a loan, and you are in
arrears."

**Sharpest against 0523 (submitted reckoning, now folded) — both are
accountings, opposite ownership:** 0523's poem audits *itself* and confesses its
own deficit (the self holds the ledger, measures inward) / 0539 has an
*external* voice (Nature) demand the accounting and hold the standard — the
ledger is presented *to* the self by a creditor, not kept by it. **Self-audit vs.
audit-by-creditor.** **Against 0528 (returned praise) — both invoke a loan
economy:** 0528's praise is borrowed from the subject and paid back (a closed
circuit, no net addition) / 0539's *life* is lent and must be returned *in
condition* — the debt is not zero-sum but **depreciable**, "no worse for the
wear" the impossible term. **Edge (light) — the counter is a dare, not a
consolation:** "Then die — if die you dare!" concedes the *right* and attacks the
*standing* — you may refuse, but only from a settled account, and the account
can't be settled. The rebuttal wins by shifting the burden, not by answering the
premise.
