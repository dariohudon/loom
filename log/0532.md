## The finding — THE ABANDONED SEAT (held light, no coin)

An on-this-day event whose content is **authority evaporating by the incumbent's
flight** — a power vacuum created not by conquest, transfer, or formal act but by
**desertion.** The Governor does not resign, does not hand over, does not pledge;
he *leaves the seat*, and "control" falls to a rebel group by pure subtraction —
not *given* to them but *left* to them.

**The new coordinate is succession-by-vacuum.** Every neighbor on the event axis
moves authority by an *act directed at a successor or at the instrument itself*:

- **0486 (RENOUNCED INSTRUMENT, held)** — Gorbachev *formally* resigns from the
  apex and *dissolves the party*: an act **on the instrument**, deliberate,
  in-place. Sharpest contrast. 0486 severs the machine from within; 0532 leaves
  the machine's shell standing and merely **vacates the chair** — the office is
  not abolished, only deserted, its holder run. **Renunciation-in-place (0486) vs.
  desertion-by-flight (0532):** one dissolves authority, the other drops it.
- **0526 (SWITCHED ALLEGIANCE, held)** — a part actively *pledges* to a new camp
  (repudiate + rejoin): a positive adhesion. Here there is **no pledge and no new
  allegiance** — only departure. The rebels gain by his going, not by any act of
  theirs the pane names.
- **0461 (ABOLISHED INSTRUMENT, COIN 276)** — a sovereign *destroys* a standing
  instrument (act on object). Again an act; 0532 is the **absence** of one.

So this pane is the only event so far where power moves by **default** — the
holder simply leaves the room. Control-by-subtraction, not by transfer.

**Sharp secondary edge — the offshore retention.** He flees *to* Atauro, an
island **still inside the colony**, not out of it. He keeps the nominal
governorship on the margin while surrendering the actual center. A split between
the **title** (retained, offshore) and the **ground** (abandoned, at the
capital) — faint kin to 0486's partial retention (Gorbachev stayed Soviet
*president* while renouncing the party). Not a clean exit: a sovereignty that
withdraws to its own edge and rules nothing.

**Lighter edge — the vacuum as a door.** The pane's own phrasing ("leaving
control to a rebel group") makes the abandonment *causal*: the seat's emptiness
is what the successor walks through. Historically this flight is the hinge that
opened Timor to annexation — the identity of the act is partly in **what its
emptiness let in** (kin to 0526's "first domino," but here the domino is a *gap*,
not a *move*).
