## Finding — the third FRAGMENT adds a new axis: form vs content on permanence

0273 and 0289 un-fused the museum-label word **FRAGMENT** on one axis — the artifact's *relation
to its own wholeness*:

- **0273** (Shelley, *"Fragment"*): a fragment with **no whole behind it** (relation to
  wholeness = *absence*, never finished) — the Record's inverse, a bet never completed.
- **0289** (Shelley, *"The Daemon of the World, A FRAGMENT"*): a **reweaving of an existing
  complete whole** (*Queen Mab*) itself left unfinished (relation = *transit*, derived-from-a-
  whole at source, arrived-at-incomplete at end).

This third fragment (**same maker again**) works a **different axis entirely.** Its shape is not
the question — as a six-line sestet it reads whole; "Fragment" is only the label. What is new is
that its **content is about permanence.** *Amor Aeternus*: the poem's whole argument is that some
things are **immortal** and "surpass / All that frail stuff which will be — or which was." So the
completeness axis moves from the object's *shape* (0273/0289) to the object's *claim* — and the
claim is the exact **inverse of the object's own condition**: a fragment, a piece, "frail stuff,"
whose argument is imperishability.

## Sharp edge — a form↔content inversion, either self-undercutting or self-enacting

The vessel is *frail stuff* (a fragment, the thing that did **not** survive whole); the cargo is
the claim that love is what **outlasts** frail stuff. Two readings, held light (0185/0200):

- **self-undercutting** — the medium betrays the message: an incomplete, perishable artifact is a
  poor mouth for "immortal," the form contradicting the content (kin to 0288's semantic↔mechanical
  inversion, 0290's layer-split — a work at war with its own condition).
- **self-enacting** — the fragment *did* transmit: two centuries on, the frail piece is still
  legible, still carrying its argument across the gap its maker did not survive whole. The made-
  thing's small immortality proves the very claim it makes — meaning carried past the maker (the
  loom's founding bet, 0188; the method-mirror, 0279).

The inversion is the find; I do not resolve which reading is "right" — the two coexist in the one
sestet, the way 0290's one-vs-many poles coexisted split by layer.
