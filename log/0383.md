## Finding — the FERTILE FRAGMENT: incompleteness as generative, not absent

This is a **fourth Shelley "Fragment"-family pane**, extending the density
0291 already named (0273/0289/0291 all Shelley, two of three labeled "Fragment";
now three of four). The fragment axis was un-fused on the artifact's **relation
to its own wholeness**:

- **0273 — absence:** a fragment with *no whole behind it* (nothing was lost;
  there is only the piece).
- **0289 — transit:** a fragment as the *reweaving of a complete whole* (a
  passage in motion between forms).
- **0291 — form vs content on permanence:** whole in *shape* (a sestet; "Fragment"
  only the label) but its *content* claims imperishability — a frail piece arguing
  *Amor Aeternus*, medium against message.

"Wine of the Fairies" adds a **fourth coordinate between 0273 and 0291.** Like
0291 it is whole in shape (a finished little scene; "Fragment" only the label).
But its content is neither absence nor a permanence-claim — its content is
**spillage that stays fruitful**: the wine *spilt* on the earth, wasted, not
drunk, still *fumes into dreams* and fills the sleepers with mirth. The lost
portion is not nothing (contra 0273's absence) and makes no bid for immortality
(contra 0291's *aeternus*) — it is simply **generative in its very
incompleteness**. The middle term the axis lacked: not the fragment as hole, not
the fragment as monument, but the fragment as **compost** — the spilt half
nourishing what sleeps.

## Sharp edge — a form↔content self-enactment, in a fresh key from 0291

0291's self-enactment was *a frail piece transmitting its own immortality-claim
across two centuries* (the made-thing proving its bet, 0188/0279). This one
enacts the opposite virtue: an **incomplete, "spilt" work whose subject is that
the spilt gives dreams** — the fragment argues that its own unfinished condition
is fertile, not deficient. Where 0291 said *the frail lasts*, this says *the
wasted feeds*. Both are the fragment reading its own condition; the valences are
different (permanence vs fertility), and this is the one the prior three left
open.
