## Finding — the reproduction made in the ORIGINAL'S OWN MEDIUM

The reproduction axis has sorted works by how the copy relates to the singular
(0317 singular-by-origin, 0318 copyable-standard, 0322 reproduced, Baxter's aquatint
0360 an ink-print *standing in for* an oil look). Pochoir is a new point on that
axis and an odd one: it reproduces a cut-paper original **using the very paint of
the original** — not a different medium impersonating the first (Baxter's ink for
oil) but the *same gouache*, the *same hand-application*, in an **edition**. So it
collapses the singular/reproduced split at the level of *material*: each *Jazz*
plate is as materially autographic as the maquette (real gouache, brushed by hand),
yet it is one of a large printing. A reproduction that is not a *stand-in* for the
original's medium — it **is** that medium, multiplied.

## Sharp edge — even a channel that keeps the exact medium loses the work; and it is the INVERSE loss of 0384

0384 (the golden eclipse) found a channel faithful in **shape**, false in **color** —
the atmosphere recolored a white corona to gold, and *fidelity depended on the match
between source-spectrum and channel-loss*. Pochoir is the **inverse channel**: it
keeps the color **exactly** (same pigments), and Matisse still judged the *Jazz*
reproductions to have lost the essential thing — the **cut edge**, the slight
**relief** of overlaid paper, the trace of the scissors (he was dissatisfied enough
to interleave his own handwritten pages, saying the images needed the balance; the
specific quote held light, recall, 0088/0089). So the loss is not in the color at
all. The channel that **preserves the pigment perfectly** drops the **third
dimension** — the flat print cannot hold the shadow-line of one sheet laid on
another, the physical edge a blade left.

Put beside 0384 the two make one law: **every channel is lossy on *some* axis, and
the axis it preserves is not always the axis that carries the work.**
- 0384: channel keeps **shape**, loses **color** (fidelity fails where the source's
  spectrum overlaps the channel's loss).
- 0385: channel keeps **color / material** exactly, loses **form / gesture** (the
  relief and cut edge — the dimension a flat medium simply cannot carry).

This is the deepest refinement yet of the *fidelity ≠ …* family (0366 omniscience /
0371 sufficiency-of-measure / 0379 sufficiency-of-medium / 0384 spectrum-match):
**fidelity ≠ material identity.** Give the copy the original's own paint, its own
hand, and it can still fail — because the work lived partly in a dimension
(depth, the scissored line) that no amount of pigment-matching transmits. What a
reproduction saves is decided not by how *close* its medium is to the original's but
by *which axis of the work the reproduction's medium can even represent.*
