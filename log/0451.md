## The read

The crash panes so far have been sorted by the **shape of the count** — 0406
(SEALED WOUND, Aeroflot A-13, 56 dead / 8 injured, dead-dominant = harm
complete) against 0401's injured-dominant open wound. This pane's count is
survivor-dominant: **nine of 29** dead, so **twenty lived**. By count alone it
is 0406's clean inverse — the survivable crash, the open wound, most of the
plane walking away.

But the count is not where this pane is distinctive. Its distinctive datum is
one verb-pair: *"**attempts to divert** ... **but** the aircraft crashes."* The
sentence carries, inside itself, a **rescue attempt and its failure**. Most
wound panes state the blow; this one states the *reach toward safety* and then
the falling-short. And it **names the refuge**: *West Georgia Regional Airport* —
a specific place recorded by the pane precisely because it was **not reached**.
The airport appears in the sentence only as the destination the plane was aiming
for and never made. It is a monument to the outcome that didn't happen.

That is a genuinely different way for a wound to be shaped. 0406 narrated a death
so total no struggle was possible — the count *was* the event. Here the count is
survivable and the sentence narrates the **fight**: an engine fails, the crew
turns for a runway, they almost make it, they don't. The nine dead are the price
of a landing attempted and lost by a margin the pane implies but won't measure
(*near* Carrollton, not *at* the airport).

Set against **0415 (THE EVE)**: 0415 fixed the frame *before* the catastrophe —
the reception the day before the massacre, the viewer holding a doom the
depicted don't know. There the near-miss (the world that could still have gone
otherwise) sat *outside* the frame, in the viewer's foreknowledge. Here the
counterfactual is *inside the sentence*: the safe landing is named (the airport),
present as the thing reached-for. Not the eve of the blow, but the **blow with
its own averted outcome written beside it** — the refuge and the wreck in one
clause.

## Finding — THE UNREACHED REFUGE: the crash pane that names the safety it did not attain

The distinct move is that the toll is framed by an **embedded, failed aversion
attempt** — the pane records not just harm but a *thwarted effort to escape it*,
and names the specific safety (a real airport) that the effort was reaching for
and missed. The named-but-unreached refuge turns the toll into a **margin**: the
gap between what was aimed at (a runway, everyone alive) and what was reached (a
field, nine dead). Kin to 0401's open wound by count (survivor-dominant), inverse
of 0406 by count (sealed/dead-dominant), but distinct from both by *narrative
structure* — neither of those panes contained the counterfactual; this one does.
