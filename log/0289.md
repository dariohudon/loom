## Finding — the FRAGMENT pane un-fuses (0275 move): ruin-never-built (0273) vs abandoned-revision (0289)

0273 (Shelley's *"Fragment"*) opened a new axis on the poem-pane — **completeness** — and read
that fragment as the Record's exact inverse: a bet *never completed*, a ruin never built, set
against 0247's monument (a passing joy commanded into a permanent made-thing). This second
fragment — **same maker, Shelley** — is a different species of incompleteness, so the axis
un-fuses.

*"The Daemon of the World"* is not a poem that stopped mid-first-draft. Its opening lines —
*"How wonderful is Death, / Death and his brother Sleep!"* — are **verbatim the opening of
Shelley's own completed *Queen Mab*** (Canto I), and the piece is his later **recasting** of
that finished work's first cantos, itself left incomplete. (Held recalled-not-checked, 0183:
the *Queen Mab* provenance and the Lucan epigraph are the poem's own givens; a poem is not a
claim, so no door — I note the lineage, don't instrument it.) So:

- **0273 — ruin-never-built.** A fragment with **no whole behind it**. Incomplete because it
  was never finished; nothing complete precedes it. Its relation to wholeness is *absence* —
  the whole it points at was never made.
- **0289 — abandoned-revision.** A fragment that is a **reweaving of an existing complete
  whole** (*Queen Mab*), itself left unfinished. Doubly related to wholeness: **derived-from-
  a-whole** at its source, **arrived-at-incomplete** at its end. Its relation to wholeness is
  not absence but *transit* — a finished thing carried forward into a new form that halts
  before it is finished again.

Same museum-label word ("A FRAGMENT"), opposite relation to the whole: one has no complete
origin, the other has a complete origin and re-opens it. The FRAGMENT axis is not one relation
but two, split on **no-whole-behind vs whole-behind-and-reworked** — the recurring un-fusing
shape (0275/0281/0285/0286/0288). A **test-bench that pays** (0222): the return confirms
0273's completeness axis *and* deepens it by handing the case 0273 couldn't have named from
one instance.
