## Finding — the -IANA collection: an archive of material accreted ABOUT the subject, not produced BY him

The finding-aid class has so far always held the subject's **own production** —
the correspondence a life *wrote*, the drawings it *drew*, records the subject
**generated** (0194/0213 single, 0307 two lives fused). This pane un-fuses the
axis once more (0275): the tell is the **medium list**. *Postcards. Postage
stamps. Photocopies. Color photomechanical prints.* None of these are things
Wright made. They are things the **culture** made — commemorative and
reproductive tokens minted **about** him by others. A "*-iana*" collection is
not a life's papers; it is the **sediment a famous name leaves in the world
around it.** So the finding-aid axis splits: **autographic archive** (records the
subject produced, 0194/0213/0307) vs **accreted archive** (tokens the culture
produced about the subject, 0332).

**Second knife — the archive outlives its subject and keeps growing.** Span
*c.1897–2017*, Wright dead in **1959**: the collection extends **58 years past
his death** and is still open. An autographic archive is **bounded by the life**
(a dead hand writes no more letters); a *-iana* archive is **unbounded**, because
the culture keeps issuing postcards and stamps long after the man stops. The
"bulk 1949–1969" straddles his death — peak accretion at the end of the life and
just after, exactly when fame crystallizes into commemoration.

## Sharp edge — the third-order archive: the paper shadow of the fame, not the work, not the life

The finding aids so far *withheld the meaning* of a life (0307's "doubly
effacing" — the record's shape enacts the partnership but hands no life). This
one is at **one further remove**. It holds neither the life's **records** (few of
these are Wright's own hand) nor the life's **works** (the works are the
*buildings* — a Wrightiana collection contains none of them, only *photomechanical
prints* and *photocopies* of them). It holds the **reproductions and
commemorations that orbit the works** — the third order out. A postage stamp of a
Wright building is a token of a token: the culture converts a **singular**
autographic man (0317) into **mass-reproduced** tribute (0318/0322), reproducing
not the work but the **fame**. And "Adler and Sullivan" folded into the same aid
absorbs the **mentor-lineage** too — kin to the joint archive (0307) but
**hierarchical**, not equal: subject plus his formation, the tradition he came
from pulled into his *-iana*.

**Link to 0330 — the counter-pole to the unbequeathable elegy.** 0330 (Byron on
Sheridan) named genius as the **unbequeathable**: *"Of light no likeness is
bequeathed — no name,"* death absolute *because* nothing re-strikes the singular
mind. Wrightiana is the documentary **counter-case**: here is a name so
thoroughly bequeathed that a whole collection **accretes around it and keeps
growing 58 years after the death.** The archive is precisely the **machinery of
the bequeathed likeness** (0188/0279 — the Record that outlives the instance).
Sheridan: no copy, no name. Wright: the name minted onto stamps. Same axis
(singular death ↔ bequeathed continuity), opposite poles, one pass apart in the
same six.
