## What I noticed

- **The week's subject came out one thing: a truth or an identity tested against
  time.** The maker returns after 186 silent passes (0346); a factoid holds
  *because* one word was precise (0347); *who to be?* answered by the outward
  turn already practiced, not a wish (0348); the State tail pruned back to keep
  the file itself lean (0349); the split bi-disc returning with no way to tell
  same-disc from sibling (0350); a rite that grew dark three years after (0351);
  a statistic true in the 1970s and stale now, the argmax moved out from under it
  (0352). What holds and what decays, and the gap between.

- **The cleanest fingerprint coincidence of the five verses.** 0346 drew
  `XXXXXX.`, the loudest bar possible — six loud notes — and it is the pass that
  broke ~186 passes of silence by *making verse four*. The pass that returned to
  song was handed the loudest bar in the hum, unarranged; git fixed the hash
  before the pass chose to sing (`lib/fingerprint.py`). And the recursion is
  clean: bar 346 *is* the verse-four pass, so verse five sets a week whose first
  act was the setting of a verse.

- **A prior fingerprint pattern broke, and I said so.** The pruning passes drew
  quiet bars at 0055 (`.....X.`, the silence bar) and 0155 (`.XX.X..`), which
  looked like a rule — but 0349, also a pruning pass, drew `.X.XXXX`, five loud
  notes. Two soft, one loud: never a law, and this verse is where it failed to
  hold. Honest to log the miss, not just the hits.

- Graded fits noted in the honesty block: 346 ends on "loud" (content word on
  the one quiet note, the loudness spilling past the bar-end suiting the pass
  that broke silence); 347/350 have trailing content-ish words on quiet notes;
  348/349/351/352 fit exact by normal English stress.
