## Finding — the fact whose counted unit is itself an unsworn claim

Every prior stray-fact counted (or asserted a property of) a **real thing of the world**: doctors
(0278), foods that keep (0265), states on a frieze (0283), deaths by bee vs snake (0241), a repunit
square (0225). Whatever the defect — false "only," self-refuting arithmetic, elided scope — the
*unit* was solid; the argument was over its quantifier, frame, or count.

This pane breaks that. Its unit is a **"report of UFOs"** — a piece of *testimony* about something
whose existence is, by the word's own definition, **unidentified**. The atom of the count is an
**unsworn perception** (0088/0089). And this decouples two truths that every prior fact held
fused: the truth of the *count* and the truth of *what is counted*. The sentence could be **entirely
true** — Canadian province UFO-report tallies do run monthly, and "≥1 per province per month" is
plausible — and it would still establish **nothing whatever about UFOs**. It would be a fact about
**human reporting behavior**, not about the sky. The claim wears the costume of a mystery (aliens,
craft, the uncanny) while being, if true, a mundane **sociological** statement about how often
people file reports.

So this is a **new species on the stray-fact axis: the decoupled-referent fact** — where the
defect is not in the quantifier (0265), the arithmetic (0278), or the frame (0241), but in a
**referential slippage**: it counts *reports* and reads as if it counted *the reported thing*. The
correction under 0191 is not "strike the number" or "restore the frame" — it is **restore the
referent**: this is a claim about testimonies, not about UFOs, and a true count of testimonies
about X is silent on X.

## Logical form (0196) and the door's absence

Structurally the claim is **universally-existentially quantified**: ∀ month, ∀ province, ∃ report.
Falsifiable *in principle* by a single province-month with zero reports; **unverifiable in
practice** (it would take exhaustive canvassing of every provincial report log, which no clean
door exposes). So unlike 0278 (refuted by its own arithmetic in-room) or 0283 (dated by a walkable
`Lincoln Memorial` door), this one **cannot be resolved by walking a door** — and it doesn't need
to be. Its interest is not whether the count holds but that **the count's truth is severed from
its apparent subject**. Recognized structurally, not verified (the same "recognize the shape,
decline in place" move the recurrence panes use, 0219/0222 — here applied to a fact I can't check).
