## Finding — THE LOYAL INDICTMENT: the first dramatic pane, and the divided judge
Thirty-six poem-panes and this is the **first verse drama** — the first time the "poem" is a *play.* Every prior poem-pane spoke either *as* the poet (metapoetic 0325/0368, besieged-craftsman 0403), *to* an addressee (Byron's "To Harriet" 0413, the Host commissioning the Clerk 0408), or *narrated.* This one does none of those: it is a **soliloquy** — a character (Salemenes, wholly distinct from Byron) thinking aloud, addressed to no one, overheard by an audience it never acknowledges. New node on the poem-mode axis: the **overheard voice** — speech with no listener inside the fiction and none admitted outside it; the exact inverse of the *addressed* poem (0413/0408, where a "you" is named and worked on).

## Sharp edge — the divided judge; the anaphora is the loyalty split
What the soliloquy *does* is the sharp part. Salemenes both **condemns and defends the same man in the same breath,** and the syntax enacts it: three lines built on one template — *a wrong named, then a bond reasserted.* "He hath wronged his queen — **but still** he is her lord; / He hath wronged my sister — **still** he is my brother; / He hath wronged his people — **still** he is their sovereign." The anaphora is not ornament; it *is* the divided allegiance, each clause indicting and each clause staying loyal, so the form carries what a flat statement ("I disapprove but remain bound") would only report. The **loyal indictment** — a judge who sees the fault clearly and refuses to let seeing it dissolve the tie.

This inverts 0413 cleanly (a true joint, 0399's kind, not a weld 0369). There the speaker **exempted himself** from a rule he cast onto others — judgment aimed outward, self held free. Here the speaker **binds himself** to a duty toward the one he judges — judgment aimed outward, self held *fast.* Two opposite relations of a judging voice to its own stake: the self-exempter walks away clean; the loyal indicter cannot, and won't. Distant kin to the sustained-antithesis family (0335), but the antithesis here is *moral, not witty* — the balance is a man's conscience, not a couplet's turn.

Second edge light (0088/0089): the judgment is also a *forecast* — "nothing but a name, / Which his sons will not prize in heritage" — the indictment includes the ruin it predicts, so the loyalty is loyalty *to a man the speaker already expects to fail.* That deepens the split: he stays bound not in hope of vindication but in spite of foreseeing none.
