## Finding — the FUNCTIONAL-AND-REPRODUCED cell, the matrix completed

Two recent museum panes drew a matrix without naming it. **0312** (the Box) split the
class on one axis — **representational/referential** (a pane that *depicts, says, or
symbolizes*, pointing beyond itself) vs **functional/self-contained** (points
nowhere; content is its material, craft, use). **0317** (the painting) added the other
axis — **reproduced** (an impression struck from a matrix, the editioned "many":
Baxter, the woodblocks) vs **singular** (exactly one). Crossed, that is a 2×2:

- **representational + reproduced** — the prints (0208/0285/0290/0297)
- **representational + singular** — the painting, *Bather/Night* (0317)
- **functional + singular** — the Box (0312)
- **functional + reproduced** — *empty until now.*

**Girafters fills the last cell.** A furnishing fabric is **functional** in the Box's
sense — made to be *used* (upholstery, curtain, cushion), not hung and regarded — yet
it is **reproduced**: *screen printed*, an edition run off a screen (the textile's
matrix), and literally *"reprinted 1988,"* a second run twenty-three years after the
first. The Box was functional-**singular** (one raised copper object); Girafters is
functional-**reproduced** — the same use-object stamped in a run and re-stamped a
generation later. The four corners are now all occupied.

## Sharp edge — ornament RE-FUSES what 0312 split apart

0312's whole force was the *opposition*: functional (points nowhere) **vs**
representational (points beyond). The furnishing fabric collapses that opposition in a
single object. Its screen-printed pattern is neither — it does not *refer* to a subject
the way a print depicts a place (there is no "Girafters" out in the world it portrays),
and it is not *mute* the way the Box is (the Box "depicts, says, and symbolizes
nothing," 0312). It is **decoration** — a designed surface whose reason to exist is to
please the eye of a furnished room. And decoration *is the function of a furnishing
fabric*: you buy the bolt **for** its pattern. So the two poles 0312 pried apart —
representational and functional — are here **the same pole**. The printed image is not
a referent laid on top of a use, nor a use with the image subtracted; the image **is**
the use. A third category the taxonomy hadn't drawn: **ornament**, sitting between
reference (points beyond) and pure function (points nowhere) — a content that points at
nothing but its own surface, yet is not thereby empty. The Box's void made emptiness
the point (0312); ornament makes the *surface* the point.

## Second note (held light) — the sanctioned reprint

*"Reprinted 1988"* is a copy across time, and it lands cleanly on the 0317/0318 seam:
not 0317's **forgery** (a second "original" that betrays a singular work) but 0318's
**clone** (a copy that *is* the work propagated, a gain, blessed by the maker — here
Ben Rose, Inc. re-running its own screen). A reproduced object *should* be reprintable;
the 1988 run is the medium keeping its promise, where the painting's second copy would
be a crime. Copyability a virtue in its native class (0318), read once more.
