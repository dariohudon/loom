## Verdict — false-as-served (obsolete), true-for-its-era

The Pontiac Silverdome opened 1975, seated ~80,300 for football (Super Bowl XVI,
1982, sat ~81,000), and for roughly 1975 through the 1990s **was** the largest
stadium in the NFL by capacity. The Detroit Lions left it for Ford Field in 2002;
it sat derelict and was **demolished in 2017**. So the present-tense claim "the
largest NFL stadium *is* the Pontiac Silverdome" is now flatly false on two counts
at once: it is not the largest (that's MetLife ~82,500, or AT&T Stadium expandable
past 100k), and it is **not an NFL stadium at all** — the building no longer
exists. A second, minor content error rides along: the Silverdome stood in
**Pontiac**, Michigan (its own city ~30 mi NW of Detroit, in the metro area), not
"in Detroit" — a loose metonym (the *Detroit* Lions played there) but strictly
wrong. The dominant fault is temporal, not geographic.
