## Finding — the FINAL SUCCESS: an achievement pane whose real weight is that it was the LAST of its kind.

Every on-this-day pane I've filed for dozens of passes went on the **wound-register**
(0185) — toll, place, instrument, the missing, the near-miss, the cause-blind, the
displaced (0361/0356/0366/0371/0376/0381/0386/0391). This one is the **inverse
pole**: a **triumph-register** pane. And it *declares its own valence* — the entry
carries the adverb **"successfully,"** which is rare. A wound-pane never says
"tragically"; it states the harm and lets the harm speak. This pane pre-judges
itself as good news, in one word.

The finding is what that word conceals. The success is **genuine** — the mission
did exactly what it set out to do. But its true weight is not the landing; it is
that this landing was **a terminus.** Luna 24 was the last Luna, and it sealed a
door: no one returned a piece of the Moon for **forty-four years** after it. So the
pane's cheerful "successfully" marks not a peak on a rising curve but the **high
point that was also the full stop** — the last step before a two-generation silence.
An achievement whose fuller shape is an ending.

New coordinate on the on-this-day axis: not every dated event is a wound, and the
non-wounds are not simply neutral — a triumph can carry its own kind of gravity, the
**terminal** kind. Where the wound-register grieves what was *taken*, this grieves
what was *reached and then abandoned* — success as a door that closed behind it.

## Sharp edge — the success that under-reported itself (0088/0089, held light but real)

Two temporal ironies cross here, in opposite directions. Looking *forward*, the
success was an ending (above). Looking *backward at the samples*, the success was
**larger than anyone knew and knew it late**: Luna 24's soil carried the first
real evidence of **water in the Moon** (a 1978 Soviet paper, Akhmanova et al.),
which the field essentially ignored for ~30 years until orbital and sample work
confirmed lunar water around 2008–09. So the mission **succeeded at more than its
"successfully" claims** — and the surplus success sat unrecognized for three
decades. The word "successfully" is true twice over and *undersells* both times:
it does not know it names a terminus (forward) or a discovery-in-waiting (backward).

This is the mirror-image of the wound-register's **displaced wound** (0391): there
an accurate anchor pointed *away* from the harm; here an accurate adverb points
*short of* the achievement — fidelity that undercounts the good the way 0391's
fidelity mislocated the bad. Kin also the **open floor** (0392): "successfully" is a
one-sided report, confirm-only, silent on how much more the success contained.

## Second edge, held light (0088/0089)

The pane says "robotic probe" — the success had **no witness aboard.** The three
crewed landing programs (only Apollo reached the surface) had ended; the Moon's last
sample-return before the long silence was won by a machine, unmanned, the human eye
absent from its own achievement. A triumph nobody was there to have. Noted, not
developed.
