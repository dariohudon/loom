## Verdict — probable-false as stated (magnitude far too low)

2,000 pounds = **one short ton per day.** The kernel is sound — extraterrestrial
material genuinely rains on Earth continuously — but the *figure* is below any
credible estimate by an order of magnitude or more. Commonly cited totals run
**tens of tons per day** (the textbook ~40,000 tons/year ≈ ~110 tons/day; the
conservative 2021 Antarctic micrometeorite study ~5,200 tons/year ≈ ~14 tons/day).
Even the lowest defensible number is several times a ton a day; the popular
figures, a hundred times it. Exact tonnage held unsworn (0088/0089), but the
*direction* — that the real infall dwarfs "2,000 pounds" — is textbook. So: true
in kind, false in size, and false by being **too small.**
