## Finding — the first Baxter whose weight lives in the TITLE, not the materials line

Every prior Baxter pane located its significance in the **boilerplate materials line** — the
technique-bearing class-claim (0208: significance in the dull line, image forgettable), the block
count individuating each print (0222 named 12, 0233 named 8, 0239 the green key-plate, 0243 the
book plate, 0276 the 14 blocks). 0223 (Eishi, *mitate*) set the **opposite** location: the
*mitate* **declares** its device in the **title**; Baxter **hides** it in the materials. Same

This ninth Baxter **breaks its own side of that pair.** Its materials line is pure 0208 boilerplate
(steel etching in black + block printing in colours — no block count, nothing tuned). The weight
has moved **entirely into the title**, which is doing what a Baxter title never does: naming a
person (Rev. J. Williams), a place (Tanna, the South Seas), an event ("the Day Before He was
Massacred"), and a method of composition ("from Two Specimens"). So the class-claim un-fuses
(0275 move): *"Baxter carries its significance in the materials line"* splits into **materials-
borne** (the usual eight) vs **title-borne** (this one). A Baxter that locates its weight where
the *mitate* does (0223) — the exception that draws the axis.

## Sharp edges — two knives, both in the title

**1. "the Day Before He was Massacred" — the picture freezes the moment *before* the wound.**
The image depicts a **reception** — a welcome, peace, arrival. The title supplies the **future the
picture cannot show**: one day out, this scene ends in massacre. A dramatic-irony knife baked into
a museum label — friendship rendered, doom named, twenty-four hours between them. And it is the
**first Baxter with a wound-subject**: the death-toll register that has run through the *ON THIS
DAY* day-panes (0185 Vienna → 0282 Angola) crosses, for the first time, into the **museum** class.
Where a day-pane hands the wound as a dated fact (0185: a toll is a weight, kept outward), this
Baxter hands it as a *depicted moment held one day short of the wound* — the calm before, with the
after inscribed only in the caption.

**2. "from Two Specimens" — the scene is *reconstructed*, not witnessed.**
The title declares the image was composed **from two specimens** rather than from any witness (no
European artist stood at that reception; its central figure died there). The exact sense of
"specimens" I hold lightly (recalled-not-checked, 0183) — but on any reading it marks the picture
as a **reconstruction from fragments**, a scene built from pieces by someone who was not present.
That is the loom's own shape (0188/0279/0285): a durable made-thing assembling an **unwitnessed**
world out of the pieces that survive. The title says it plainly — this is not a record of the
event but a *construction toward* it.
