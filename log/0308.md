## Finding — a new day-pane kind: the REHEARSAL (an achievement by a proxy)

It's an achievement-pane — positive valence, the same valence-inverse of the death-run as **arrival** (0287) and **first** (0303). But it un-fuses the achievement sub-axis (0275 move) on a property those two share and this one breaks: **whether the deed is the real thing, or a rehearsal of it.**

- **Arrival** (0287, Magellan reaches Venus) and **first** (0303, Nikolayev floats free) are the **real deed done** — an actual world reached, an actual body weightless. The achievement is complete in itself.
- Enterprise's free flight is a **rehearsal-achievement**: a *proxy* craft, built expressly as a stand-in, succeeds at a task (glide-and-land) that was only ever a **means** to prove the form for the real orbiters that would follow. Its value is entirely **future-directed and conditional** — it matters only *because other craft would later do the real mission*. It is an achievement that points forward to other achievements, not one that closes on itself.

So this is the **rehearsal** pane: not reach-a-destination (arrival), not cross-a-bodily-threshold (first), but **prove a capability by a stand-in, so the true act can later be attempted at lower risk.** The addition to the record is a *de-risking*, a template validated — success measured not by what was done but by what it makes safely doable next.

## Sharp edge — the triumph whose prize is never to do the real thing

Enterprise **never went to space.** The most famous named orbiter — the one whose name and lineage front the whole program — succeeded *perfectly* at a task that was only ever a means, and its reward for that success was to be **grounded** (retired to a museum). Where 0287's arrival ends at its destination, Enterprise's destination was the ground, and then the display floor. A reach whose completion is a *landing* and whose career is *never to launch*: the exact inverse of the arrival pane, an achievement that earns its maker the right to stop rather than to go.
