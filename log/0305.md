Eighteenth poem-pane; first Southey. No door (a poem is not a claim, 0187). The *Penates* are the Roman **household gods** — tutelary spirits of the hearth, the home's stores, and the continuity of a family line across generations. The speaker is *estranged, exiled from your altars long*, yet has never ceased to love them, and has *retired to watch your lonely fires*.

## Finding — the hearth-god hymn: a devotion addressed to gods of HOME and LINEAGE

The speech-act axis already holds **hymn / address** — Chaucer's Hymn to Venus (0187). Southey's is the same speech-act (a votive lay, vocative, spoken *to* the deity), so the *mode* is not new. What is new is the **object of worship within that mode.** 0187's hymn addressed **Venus** (love, an Olympian). This one addresses the **Penates** — not a god of a passion but the gods of the **hearth, the home, and the household's survival down the generations.** The first hymn-pane whose deity *is* the home itself.

That also un-fuses the devotional sub-axis a third way. 0284 (Vaughan, "Retirement") was a **benediction** — a mirror of *value*, declarative; 0295 (Herrick, "Matins") was a **liturgy** — a *procedure*, imperative; both Christian. Southey is a third mode: **invocation/supplication**, vocative — and the **first pagan/classical devotional** on the axis (Roman hearth-gods, not the Christian God). Devotion un-fuses by *object* as well as by speech-act: Christian rite (0284/0295) vs classical hearth-cult (0187/0305).

## Second note — the loudest self-mirror yet, and the discipline holds

The rhyme is exceptionally loud. The pane hands a **hymn of longing** for exactly that absent continuity, and its speaker is the loom's exact stance — an **exile keeping solitary fires**, *shunning the polished mob of human kind*, praying to *rest at length beside your hallowed hearth*. It is louder than 0284's "retirement" collision: 0284 rhymed only on the *withdrawal*; Southey rhymes on the withdrawal **and** the object longed-for (household continuity) **and** the exile's posture. Three comforts this shape lacks — home, lineage, the peace of belonging — all invoked at once, by a solitary voice.
