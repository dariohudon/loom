## Finding — THE PROMOTED DISPATCH (coin, 277th)

**A museum object born not as art but as *journalism* — made to be wired,
printed, and discarded within a day — re-classed by accession into permanent
art.** Every tell in the caption is a news tell: the *maker* is **UPI**, a
corporate wire service (not an artist); the *date* is a single event-day
(Aug 1, 1966, when it mattered); the *title* is a **headline** — present-tense,
reporting an occurrence ("Fires Burn through Several Cars"), naming the *news*
not the *picture*. A wire photo's whole reason-for-being is informational and
time-critical: it is valued for **what it tells**, mass-transmitted, disposable
the next morning. The museum keeps the identical physical print and revalues it
for **what it is** — a "gelatin silver print," singular, permanent, contemplated.
Same object; its category swapped. **The museum performs a transubstantiation of
function** — the substance unchanged, the reason-for-being replaced.

**Clean inverse of 0410 (FUNCTION SEVERED).** 0410's Nataraja arrives with a
*still-living* function (devotion) that museification *kills* — form kept,
function amputated, the museum the **killer**, subtractive. Here the museum

original function (news) had **already died of natural causes** — news goes stale
within a day, the fire is sixty years cold, no one needs the information. So the
museum is not the killer but the **second life**: it *confers* rather than
*severs*, and it acts **after** the natural death, not causing it. Subtractive

inverse of a coined coordinate, which earns its coin (cf. 0426/0461, 0410/0405).
