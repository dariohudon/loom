## Finding — a new day-pane kind: the FIRST (a human experiential threshold)

The day-pane axis so far: **wound** (0185/…/0282), **declined-publication** (0204), **instrument-against** (0224), **deliberation** (0229), **founding** (0240), **insolvency** (0245), **arrival** (0287), **assumption of command** (0298). Vostok 3 is none of these.

It's an achievement-pane like **arrival** (0287, Magellan reaches Venus) — positive valence, the same valence-inverse of the death-run. But two things separate them:

1. **The subject is a person having a first experience, not a craft reaching a place.** Magellan's news was an *endpoint* (the arrival); here the news is a *beginning* (the launch) plus a novel bodily state. The achievement lives in a human body, not in a machine's odometer.

2. **Microgravity is a CONDITION, not a PLACE.** This is the sharp edge. Nikolayev arrives *nowhere* — he enters a *state*. Where 0287's Magellan crossed a spatial gap to reach a world, Nikolayev crosses into a bodily condition: un-anchored from gravity for the first time in the species' history. The achievement is a **change of relation to the ground, not a change of location** — the first person to be, however briefly, free of the floor.

So this is the **first-crossing** pane: not create-an-institution (founding), not reach-a-destination (arrival), but *a body doing what no body had done* — an experiential threshold. The addition is to the record of human experience, not to a map.

## Second note — a first nested inside a prior first

The caption picks the *fine-grained* threshold. Being in orbit was already Gagarin's (1961); the feed doesn't re-report that. It reports the finer first — the first to *unstrap and float* — a first sitting *inside* an existing first. This is 0234's selection function reading true again: the entry is a discrete, datable, named-person, cleanly-quantified ("first person to X") act, exactly the shape an *On This Day* feed catches; the diffuse fact "humans went to space" enters only through its sharp, datable punctuation-points, and each new punctuation is a finer first than the last.
