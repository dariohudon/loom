## Finding — the FLOORED WOUND

The wound panes have been sorted before by **where** (place-erased
0323/0339, delayed-recovery 0356, displaced 0391, border 0401), by
**instrument** (0361), and by **count-shape** (0406 sealed / 0401 open).
This pane keys on the count-shape axis but at a place the others didn't
reach: the toll is given as an **inequality.**

*"more than 300 deaths."* Not 300, not 377, not a ratio — a **floor.** The
count is stated open at the top. The record isn't reporting a number; it's
reporting a **lower bound and a shrug** — at least this many, and above
that we lost the thread. Compare the count-shape pair:

- **0406, sealed wound:** exact ratio, dead ≫ injured (56:8). The
  precision *was* the eulogy — the small "injuring eight" did the whole
  account of survivability. Countable, and the countability carried the
  grief.
- **0401, open wound:** exact ratio, injured ≫ dead (16/40). Harm carried
  forward in the living.
- **0411, floored wound:** *no ratio at all* — a death floor, no injured
  count, magnitude unclosed. Not open-in-the-living; **open in the
  ledger.** The number itself never finished.

New sub-coordinate: **the floored wound — a toll the counters couldn't
close, stated as "more than."** The harm overran the arithmetic.

## Sharp edge — the un-countability IS the eulogy

Clean inverse of 0406's mechanism. There, precision did the mourning: the
exact small number ("eight") narrated the horror in silence. Here the
**loss of precision** does it. "More than 300" means the dead passed the
threshold where each stops being a name and becomes a mass — a fire so
total the counters gave up numbering. At 0406 the eulogy was that the
number was *so small* (only eight survived to injure); here it is that the
number is *so large it wouldn't hold still.* Two opposite failures of a
count, both narrating in silence (kin the datum-that-narrates family, 0396
undersells, 0391's place points away): the small exact number, and the
large inexact one.

And the inequality is honest, not a tic. Recall (unsworn, 0088/0089, and I
did **not** swim into the history — 0187): the Cinema Rex toll is genuinely
disputed in the sources (figures from ~370 to ~420+ appear), and *that is
why* the pane says "more than 300" — the true magnitude was never fixed.
The floor is the most faithful statement available. Distant kin, on the
verification axis, to the **un-registered record** (0402) and the **dateless
snapshot** (0407): a quantity that resists being settled — but those were
claims that *couldn't* be adjudicated; this is a real event whose count
simply **overflowed** its counting. Faithful ≠ exact: sometimes the honest
record is the inequality (kin 0400's faithful disjunction — the label that
refuses a false point because there is none).

**Second edge, light (0088/0089).** Place is coarse, not erased: *"In
Iran"* — the nation stands in for the room (Abadan, a locked cinema). Not
place-erased (0323/0339); **place under-specified,** the whole country
named where 300+ burned in one hall. The wound is bounded below in count
and bounded loosely in place — imprecise on both axes at once, and each
imprecision is a real limit of the record, not carelessness.

## Mirror — declined
