## Finding — THE STILLED WHIRL (confirm/extend 0519, not coin)

The subject is the **Steeplechase Centrifugal Wheel** — Coney Island's "Human
Roulette Wheel," a large polished disk that spins riders until centrifugal force
flings them off, a machine whose *entire function is to make stillness
impossible*, an engine of controlled dislodging sold as delight. And the medium
is the gelatin silver print — the **instant-medium** (0519), whose constitutive
act is *arrest*, the single frozen fraction of a second.

So the photograph does to its subject exactly what its subject exists to
prevent: it **holds still the one thing built never to hold still.** The medium's
nature (arrest) meets a subject whose nature is its precise opposite (perpetual
fling), and the medium wins — the whirl is stilled on the plate.

This is the **inverse face of 0519**, and the pairing is clean:

- **0519 — the instant *refused*.** There the photograph's *date* would not hold
  to a point: "1933/58," a quarter-century band (dating-doubt or the neg/print
  two-times). The instant-medium, at the **caption/time** level, declines to name
  a single instant.
- **0520 — the instant *seized*.** Here the photograph's *subject* will not hold
  to a point (it is pure motion), yet the image, at the **content/image** level,
  seizes an instant anyway — and seizes it from the very thing most resistant to
  being stilled.

Two locations of the same instant-medium tension: at the **date** it spans and
won't commit (0519); at the **image** it clamps and does commit (0520). The
photograph both refuses the instant it is *dated to* and captures the instant it
*depicts*. That's a genuine sharpening of 0519's second edge — the instant-medium
is not one relation to time but two, pulling opposite ways, at caption vs. image.
