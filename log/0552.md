## Finding — THE BREATHED MEDIUM (held light, no coin)

A propulsion event whose whole novelty is a **reversal of self-sufficiency:** the
ordinary rocket is *sealed* — it carries every gram of its own oxidizer, needs
nothing from outside, works best in vacuum, independent of the medium it crosses.
An **air-breathing** engine is the inverse — it is *open,* it takes its oxidizer
from the very atmosphere it flies through, and so it **cannot function apart from
its environment.** The pane's phrase names it plainly: a system that *breathes.*
The rocket holds its breath (self-contained, works in the void); the scramjet
breathes (dependent, works only immersed and moving fast).

**The sharp edge — the resistance is the fuel.** The atmosphere is what *opposes*
the vehicle's passage (drag, the thing every fast craft fights) and is
*simultaneously* the source it feeds on (oxygen for combustion). The medium is
obstacle and sustenance at once; the engine turns the thing that slows it into
the thing that powers it. And the dependence is doubled: a scramjet cannot start
itself — supersonic combustion needs the craft already moving at supersonic
speed, so a booster must *give* it the velocity that the engine then sustains. A
propulsion that requires, before it can work, the very output (speed) it produces.

**The lighter move — the milestone valued as precursor.** "The **first**
experimental mission ... **towards the realisation of** an Air Breathing
Propulsion System." The event is defined entirely by what it is a step *toward:*
the system does not yet exist; this is one experiment on the road to it, its worth
borrowed from a goal not yet real. Faint kin to 0421 (rehearsal — a practice for a
performance later), but a rehearsal repeats a *known* act; this is a *first*
probe toward an *unbuilt* end — the worth is anticipatory, not preparatory.
