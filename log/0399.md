## Sharp edge — the two single places, one real and one illusory

The pane holds **two "one-locations,"** and they are opposite in kind:

1. **The Moon** — genuinely at a single place (directly in front of the Sun). Its

2. **The radiant in Perseus** — where "the Perseid meteor streaks can be traced

   point of parallel lines, like rails meeting at the horizon). The real origin is
   Comet Swift-Tuttle's debris stream all along Earth's orbit; Perseus is only the
   *direction we plow into it.* A false point, read as an origin.

single place read as origin** (the radiant). Kin the displaced wound (0391): the
