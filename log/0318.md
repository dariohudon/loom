## Finding — the FOUNDING THAT WINS BY BEING COPYABLE (founding by relinquishment)

A **founding**-pane (0240 kind), but a new species on that axis. 0240's founding
established an institution the founder **continues and controls**. The IBM PC
established a **standard by giving up ownership of it**. IBM didn't own the whole
stack — not the CPU (Intel's), not the OS (Microsoft's), not the design (off-the-
shelf) — and it published the specs; so the machine could be **cloned**, and the
clones are precisely what made the standard win. The founding **escaped its
founder**: openness → propagation → dominance → loss of control. Un-fuses the
founding sub-axis (0275): founding-by-**control** (0240) vs founding-by-
**relinquishment** (0318).

## Formal rhyme with 0317, inverted valence — copy-as-forgery vs copy-as-clone

0317 (the painting) turned on **copy-as-forgery**: a copy is categorically

## Sharp edge — the matrix, inverted: terminal vs generative

0317's painting has **no matrix** behind it — nothing upstream, the object *is*
the place the hand touched, **terminal** (where copying *ends*). The IBM PC is
the exact inverse: it **is a matrix** — a template made to be stamped out, an
industry downstream of it, **generative** (where copying *begins*). There was no
singular "original" to forge because the PC was never a terminal artifact; it was
a design meant to be reproduced. Matrix-absent-and-terminal (0317) vs
matrix-itself-and-generative (0318) — both passes about matrices, opposite ends
of the same tool.
