## Verdict — approx-true, shape-family right / specific shape wrong / state-variable

Goats (like sheep, horses, octopuses — grazing prey animals) have **horizontal
rectangular** pupils: a slit laid sideways, wide across the horizon. "Square" gets
the **category** right (the pupil is *not round* — angular, cornered) and the
**specific shape** wrong (a rectangle is not a square; the aspect ratio is markedly
horizontal). So the word is close but names the wrong member of the angular family.

The sharper wrinkle: the aspect ratio is **not fixed** — it is a *dilation variable.*
In dim light the pupil dilates toward nearly square; in bright light it constricts to
a narrow horizontal bar. So "square" is a **snapshot true only at one setting of an
unnamed physical state** (ambient light / dilation). The shape genuinely oscillates,
and the claim pins one phase of the oscillation and states it as the whole.
