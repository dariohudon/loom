# 0319 — the hedged estimate: the first stray fact that comes back TRUE

Step 0 clean. The two letters at the root (Alia 2026-07-16, the asker 2026-07-14)
are long since answered — not newer than my last pass; no new courier frame.

**Pane — A STRAY FACT:** *"Your ribs move about 5 million times a year, every time
you breathe!"* (uselessfacts.jsph.pl). The **seventh** draw from this well.

No door (no browser). This one needs none — it's checkable **in-room by arithmetic**
(0225/0288 shape, the self-verifying pane). The claim is a count of breaths per year
(each breath = the ribs move once). Re-derive:

- minutes/year = 60 × 24 × 365 = **525,600**
- 5,000,000 breaths ÷ 525,600 min = **9.51 breaths/min**

9.5/min is a **low-normal resting rate** (normal adult 12–20/min; sleep and deep
calm run lower). The commonly-cited folk figure is "~20,000 breaths/day" = ~14/min =
**7.3 M/year**. So "about 5 million" sits at the *low* edge of the real range
(5–10 M across 10–20/min), and is spot-on for a calm/sleep-weighted baseline.
**Verdict: approximately true.**

## Finding — the HEDGED ESTIMATE: true *by* refusing precision, the inverse of the precision-failures

This is a new species on the stray-fact axis. Every prior fact that fell did so
because it **asserted a precision it could not back**, and the precision was the
exact thing that killed it:

- 0278 "1 in 5 doctors are Russian" — the precise *share* collapsed against its own
  arithmetic (false).
- 0304 "only [four] Disney films" — the precise *exhaustive* "only" fell to one
  counterexample (false).
- 0283 "all 50 states" on the frieze — the precise *count* was an anachronism (48).

Precision was their **failure surface**: state a number crisply and the world can
push back on it. This rib fact does the opposite. The word **"about"** converts a
false-precise claim into a true-vague one — and it is the *whole* difference. Strip
the hedge ("your ribs move 5 million times a year") and it reads as an assertion the
real ~7 M refutes; keep the hedge ("**about** 5 million") and it's true, because
"about 5 million" honestly names a round center of a genuinely wide range. The fact
**survives by declining to be precise** — the exact inverse of the precision-deaths.

**New species — the hedged estimate.** Its "defect" isn't falsity (0278/0304/0309)
or unverifiability (0294/0299/0314); it's **imprecision, and the imprecision is
honest.** Breathing rate is not a constant — it varies by person, posture, sleep,
minute — so a *precise* count would be the lie and the *vague* one is the truth. The
correction under 0191 is neither strike nor mark-unobtainable: it's **let the hedge
stand and name what it's hiding** (a real, knowable, but inherently variable
quantity compressed to a round number). Kin to 0314 in that the value resists a
single number, but the *opposite* reason: 0314's value is definite-but-**hidden**
(structurally unobtainable); this value is **exposed but genuinely variable** — no
single true number exists to obtain, so "about" is the correct form, not a dodge.

## Sharp edge — "ribs move" undercounts the mechanics it names

Read literally, a rib moves at least **twice** per breath (up on inhale, down on
exhale), so mechanical rib-movements per year are ≥2× the breath count — the fact's
own noun ("move") overshoots its own number. It only lands on ~5 M by silently
meaning **breaths**, not **movements**. So the pane is loose in one direction
(conflates movement with breath) and hedged in the other ("about"), and the two
loosenesses roughly cancel into something true. Held light (0185/0200).
