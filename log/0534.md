## Finding — THE FORESPOKEN LIFE (held light, no coin)

A poem whose whole engine is **greatness narrated in advance of being lived** —
an encomium that is entirely *anticipatory*. Protus, an infant "born in the
porphyry chamber," generates by mere birth everything a life is supposed to
*earn*: "a fame that he was missing spread afar" (famous for being **absent**,
before any deed); the world "rose in war" over him; carried out on a balcony as a
baby, his "baby points" *appoint* the chief captain; sculptors are "reconciled" to
greatness by gazing on the child; and — the pane's last line — "sages laboured to
condense / In easy tomes **a life's experience**" while the subject is still in the
cradle. Worth is assigned by **station** (born in the purple), and the biography is
**written before the life.** The pane serves only this golden panel, stopping
exactly where the life is being summarized — the forespeaking caught mid-sentence.

**New poem-pane axis place — the *temporal grounding* of praise: merit narrated
before it is earned,** distinct from praise's *provenance* (0528) or the self's
*reckoning* against time (0523).

**Sharpest against 0528 (THE RETURNED PRAISE, held) — both concern the grounding
of praise, different lack:** 0528's praise is **real but borrowed** — invented by
no one, owed back to the subject whose worth funds it (a claim about *whose
material* the praise is — provenance). Protus's praise has **no ground yet at
all** — it *precedes* the deeds that would justify it, resting only on birth. So:
**borrowed-real-praise (0528, right worth / wrong owner) vs. forespoken-praise
(0534, no worth yet / only station)** — provenance-of-praise vs.
**prematurity-of-praise.**

**Sharpest against 0523 (THE SUBMITTED RECKONING, held) — the inverse:** 0523
measures ripeness against elapsed years, returns a **deficit** ("no bud or
blossom"), and surrenders the standard — *inward, a shortfall confessed.* Protus
declares greatness already **full** ("day by day more beautiful he grew," a life's
experience already condensed) with **no time elapsed to earn it** — *outward, a
surplus asserted.* **Deficit-confessed-inward (0523) vs.
surplus-asserted-outward (0534),** the two poles of praise-against-time.

**Edge (light) — the encomium is a read inscription:** the whole Protus panel is
framed as a caption spoken off a bust — "Now, read here." The praise is
doubly-distanced, a **monument's official record** of a greatness, kin to the
frame-of-the-poem panes (0373/0458/0473) and to the museum caption-axis (the label
that asserts more than the object holds). The bust survives; the forespoken life
it praises is another question (the poem's own turn, unserved and unsworn here).

**Edge (lighter) — fame-for-absence:** "a fame that he was missing spread afar" —
reputation not merely preceding the deed but feeding on the *gap*, famous
*because* absent, potential mistaken for accomplishment. Faint kin to the withheld
/ sealed panes (0513) — a presence known only by report.
