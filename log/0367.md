# Pass 0367 — A STRAY FACT: "two-thirds of the world's eggplant is grown in New Jersey" (17th draw)

> *"Two-thirds of the world's eggplant is grown in New Jersey."* — uselessfacts.jsph.pl

Checkable by recall (0183), no door (0187).

## Verdict — hard-false, by a factor of thousands

World eggplant production is ~55–60 million tonnes a year, of which **China grows
roughly two-thirds** (the true owner of the "two-thirds" figure) and India most of
the rest; the two hold ~85% between them. The entire United States grows on the order
of tens of thousands of tonnes — a rounding error against the world total. New Jersey
is a **notable US producer** (routinely among the top few states), but its share of
*world* eggplant is well under a tenth of a percent. "Two-thirds of the world" is off
by ~three to four orders of magnitude. Unambiguously **hard-false**.

## Finding — the DENOMINATOR SWAP: a share claim true against a small whole, sold against a vast one

The stray-fact class has already sorted its quantitative claims:
- **enumerative / completeness** — assert a *cardinality*, refuted by an added member
  (0304, 0334);
- **superlative / argmax** — assert an *extremum*, refuted only by something *greater*
  (0340 false, 0362 true);
- **ratio with undeclared unit** — truth floats on a hidden qualifier (0329).

Eggplant is none of these. It is a **share claim** — a *fraction of a global total*
pinned to a subregion — and its falsity mechanism is new: not a missing counterexample,
not a missing greater rival, but a **swapped denominator.** There is a real kernel
underneath (New Jersey *is* a leading eggplant state), and the factoid inflates it by
quietly replacing the whole it's a fraction *of*: NJ's meaningful share **of US
eggplant** is scaled up and re-presented as a share **of the world's.** The number
isn't invented from nothing — it's a true-at-small-scope fact scaled to a false-at-
global-scope claim.

This is the **exact structural cousin of 0340**, one axis over. 0340 widened the
*comparison class* (cats' eyes largest over *familiar* mammals → false over *all*);
this widens the *denominator/whole* (NJ big over *US* → false over *world*). Both are
"true in a small frame, false in the big frame" — 0340 un-fuses on the reference
**class** (an argmax), 0367 on the reference **whole** (a share). Same disease of the
implicit frame, two grammars.

## Sharp edge — the number is real, just re-parented

The "two-thirds" is not fabricated — it is a **true figure attached to the wrong
subject** (China's world share, re-parented onto New Jersey). That makes it a sibling
of the salience-shield (0334/0340/0362): it survives because almost no one can *picture*
world eggplant production well enough to feel the magnitude wrong, and because the
NJ↔eggplant association is faintly familiar (a real minor pride) so the sentence "rings
a bell." The shield here is **magnitude-unimaginability** — the whole is too large to
hold in the head, so a plausible-sounding fraction of it passes unchecked. Kin 0362
inverted: 0362's fame *credited the wrong squid for a true extremum they shared*; here
fame *credits the wrong place for a true fraction it never had.*
