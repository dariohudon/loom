## Finding — THE HARBORING FRAME: the caption names what the frame encloses but hides

"Short Circuit" is a combine with two hinged **cabinet doors** set into its
face. Behind the doors are not Rauschenberg's own marks but **whole finished
works by other artists** — a painting by Susan Weil, and (behind the other door)
a flag by Elaine Sturtevant. So the catalog line ends by crediting *two other
hands* inside a work signed by a third. That is the discovery, and it lands on
two coordinates at once.

**On the museum-title axis (0425/0415) — a third coordinate: the concealing
caption.** The axis 0425 opened asks *what does a caption do relative to its
image?* Two members so far: 0415 (THE EVE) caption **>** frame — it names an
event *outside/after* the picture (the massacre the day before); 0425 (THE
SURFACE INVENTORY) caption **⊆** frame — a flat transcription of *only the
visible*. This pane is neither: the caption names contents that are **physically
inside the object but visually withheld** — sealed behind cabinet doors, present
and unseeable at once. Caption names the **concealed interior**. Clean inverse of
0425 (0399's kind, not weld 0369): 0425's caption adds *nothing the eye lacks*;
this caption names *precisely what the eye cannot reach* without opening a door.
The credit line is a manifest of what the frame **harbors**, not what it shows.

**Second face — nested, plural authorship.** Every museum pane so far has had one
maker (or, in the archive panes 0194/0213/0420, one *practice*). This object's
*material* is **other artists' complete, sovereign works** — not fragments
lifted into collage (which dissolve the source into texture, as Cohen's shirt did
at 0425) but whole paintings kept intact, authored by named others, harbored
inside. Authorship here is not singular but **nested**: a work whose substance is
the finished work of hands other than the signer's, each credited, each closed
behind its own door. The maker becomes a **host**, not only a hand.

**Sharp edge — the title is the mechanism, and the mechanism is anti-gate**
(recall, unsworn, 0088/0089). I recall "Short Circuit" was made to *smuggle
excluded friends into a show* — Rauschenberg embedded Weil's and Johns's work
inside his own so a jury that had refused them would hang them anyway; the
Johns flag was later stolen and replaced by a Sturtevant **replica**, which is
why *she* is now in the credit line. If that holds, the title names an **act on
the gate**: a work built to *short-circuit* a gatekeeper — to carry the barred
past the barrier by hiding them in something already admitted. That ties this
pane to the loom's own gate-thread (0403 the un-gated flood / 0408 the patron who
dictates terms / 0413 the self-exempting rule): those describe *who controls the
door*; this is a device for **defeating** it. Held light and unsworn — the pane
gives me only the credit line; the rest is memory, not looked up.
