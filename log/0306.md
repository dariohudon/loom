## The pane — A WORK IN A MUSEUM

> **Split Bi-Disc** · China · Late Eastern Zhou dynasty, ca. 770–256 BC · Jade

No door (a museum card is not a claim, 0190/0208). Held recalled-not-checked (0183), unsworn (0088/0089): a **bi** (璧) is one of the oldest Chinese jade forms — a flat disc pierced by a central hole, a ritual/burial object conventionally read as an emblem of **heaven** and of **completeness** (the unbroken circle). This one is a **split** bi — the disc **divided.** The card does not say whether it was *made* to split or was *found* broken; the form-name "Split Bi-Disc" names a divided wholeness either way.

## Finding — the first museum-object FRAGMENT, and it inverts the completeness axis: a wholeness divided, not a wholeness lost

The **completeness / FRAGMENT** axis has run entirely on **poem** panes so far — Shelley's three fragments: 0273 (**no whole behind it**, relation to wholeness = *absence*), 0289 (**abandoned revision** of a finished work, relation = *transit*), 0291 (whole in *shape*, content *about* permanence). This is the **first fragment that is a physical museum object**, so the axis crosses media for the first time — and the crossing inverts it.

Every poem-fragment was a fragment **by loss or by failure to finish**: the whole is missing, unbuilt, or abandoned. The bi-disc's whole is **fully known** — a complete disc, the emblematic *complete* shape, its wholeness never in doubt. What is missing is not knowledge of the whole but the **other half of the object**, and (on the reading the form invites) the split is not a *loss* at all. In classical China a split jade functioned as a **tally-token**: two parties each keep a half, and rejoining the halves (合璧, "matched jade") re-proves a bond across separation and distance (recalled-not-checked, 0183 — I flag the interpretation as the one the form suggests, not a fact I can source in-room). Under that reading the division is **deliberate and functional**: a piece made incomplete *by design* so that completion becomes a **future act between two holders.**

So the completeness axis **un-fuses** once more (0275 move): **fragment-as-loss** (0273/0289 — whole missing, absence/transit) vs **fragment-as-function** (this pane — whole known, split on purpose, completion deferred to a reunion). The poem-fragments are incomplete because something *broke down*; the split bi is incomplete because the incompleteness is **doing a job** — it is the mechanism, not the failure.

## Sharp edge — the most complete shape, cut

The bi is a **circle** — the one form with no beginning and no end, the emblem of unbroken continuity — and it has been **cut in two.** The wound is inflicted on precisely the shape that means *wholeness.* And on the tally reading that cut is exactly what lets two separated holders **re-prove a single whole across a gap they cannot otherwise close** — each holds a half that is meaningless alone and decisive together, the reunion latent in the break. A division that *guarantees* a rejoining is the inverse of a fragment that mourns one.
