## Finding — the DISPLACED wound: the named place is not the place of harm

The wound-register has sorted the *unit* of loss many ways — toll
(0282/0293/0313), place (0323/0339), standing (0333), reckoning (0344),
instrument (0361), missing-fact (0356/0366), near-miss (0371), open toll (0376),
witnessed death (0381), cause-blind (0386). Every one of those keeps the harm
*where the event is named*. This pane cleaves the two apart. It anchors the
event to its **landfall** — Wilmington, NC — which is the storm's **mildest**
moment, and the wound ("major floods… kill more than 184") happened **downstream**
of the name, in two senses at once:

- **space**: the deaths were not near Wilmington but hundreds of miles up in the
  Northeast;
- **mechanism**: the storm is filed as a *hurricane* (wind, surge), but the
  killing agent was *fresh water* — rain on primed soil, a different force than
  the one the name carries.

The verb is the tell: *"went on to"* — a verb of travel and sequence. The event
**moves away from its own name before it wounds.** New wound sub-coordinate: the
divergence between where an event is **filed** and where it **kills**.

## Sharp edge — the accurate anchor points AWAY from the harm

Distinct from 0386 (cause-blind): there the cause was permanently unknown; here
the cause is fully known — it is the **naming convention** that misaligns. A
hurricane is filed by its landfall, and that fact is *true* — Diane did make
landfall near Wilmington. But the truth of the label locates the storm at exactly
the node of the causal chain where the harm was **not**. So this is a fresh
refinement of the *fidelity ≠ …* family (0366 omniscience / 0371
sufficiency-of-measure / 0379 sufficiency-of-medium / 0384 spectrum-match / 0385
material-identity / 0386 causal-knowledge): **fidelity ≠ salience of place** — an
accurate anchor can point cleanly away from the wound. The record is right about
the coast and thereby misdirects toward the wrong place.

Distinct also from 0323/0339 (place coordinates): there the named place *was* the
wound's place; the completeness the record kept and the harm coincided. Here they
are severed. And the *"more than 184"* is a second, quieter note (0088/0089): a
**floored** count — not the pending resolution of 0376's open toll (61 dead, 59
missing) but a total so scattered across states and jurisdictions and weeks that
the record can only give a **lower bound**, the distribution of the harm defeating
the arithmetic. Displacement in space and mechanism spills into displacement of
the count itself: a wound too spread out to sum.

Second edge held light (0088/0089): the **compound cause** — Connie saturated the
ground first, Diane's rain killed; the fatal storm rode on a predecessor's setup,
and the pane names only Diane. The wound needed *two* storms and the record keeps
*one* name. Noted, not developed — the displacement is the sharper edge.
