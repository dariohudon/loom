## Finding — THE ARRESTED SERIAL (held light, no coin)

A museum pane whose object is a **periodical** — a thing made to be *serial and
superseded*, ephemeral by design, printed to be replaced next issue and then
discarded — here fixed as **"#1 and #2"** and granted permanence in a collection.
The museum does to the magazine the one thing a magazine is built to resist: it
**stops the series and keeps it.** Two things are frozen at once — the object's
*disposability* (an ephemeron made durable) and its *forward motion* (a sequence
in progress arrested at its opening).

**New museum-axis place — the *periodical* / arrested-serial:** an object whose
nature is a succession in time, held still.

**Sharpest against 0505 (intended multiple) — the orthogonal cousin.** 0505's
object is made to exist as **many identical copies** — multiplicity in *space*,
the unique-original premise contradicted by there being lots of the same thing.
0535's magazine is made to exist as a **succession of different issues** — #1,
then #2, then — seriality in *time*, the premise contradicted by the thing never
being finished, each issue killing the last. **Copy-multiplicity (space) vs.
issue-seriality (time)** — two orthogonal ways the museum's "singular, complete,
permanent" object is a category error, and this pane names the temporal one.

**Edge (light) — the name-collision.** Its title is *Work*, and the museum
catalogues it as **a work.** The object's own name is the medium's generic noun,
the very category word the label files it under — the title and the catalog term
collapse into one syllable. Faint kin to the reflexive-caption reads
(naming-by-likeness 0429, the caption's own machinery) but distinct: here it's
not a resemblance imposed, it's the object's *given name* happening to be the
institution's word for what it now is. A design studio's magazine about *work*,
become an art *work* about design. (Light; not the finding.)

**Edge (lighter) — captured at birth.** The museum holds exactly **#1 and #2** —
the *opening* of the run, the origin issues, not a mature back-catalog. A serial
caught at its inception, the forward motion suspended right where it began; the
question the object's own form asks (is there a #3?) permanently unanswered
inside the case. The permanence is granted precisely to the *least settled* part
of a serial's life.
