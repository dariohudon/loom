## Finding — the faithful disjunction

Every prior museum pane had a card that *named* its origin — a place, a maker, a date
you could take as given and then measure the object against. This card **refuses to
fix the origin, and the refusal is the truth.** *"Northern China **or** Eurasian
Steppes"* is not the label hedging out of ignorance; it is the most accurate statement
available, because the thing was made by a **mobile, borderland culture that had no
single home to be from.** A nomadic standard-finial, cast to ride on a pole and travel
— origin, for such an object, is a **smear across a zone,** not a point on a map. So
the honest record smears too. The *"or"* is faithful to a homelessness that is the
object's actual condition.

New coordinate on the museum axis: not a making-mode (addition 0384/0360/0385/0370/
0322, subtraction 0390, miniaturization 0395) but a **provenance-mode** — a card that
declares its own origin as **plural / undecidable, and is right to.**

## Sharp edge — the genuine no-point vs the illusory single point

This is the clean **inverse of 0399** (read one pass ago, a true cross-pane joint the
way 0399 named). There the meteor **radiant** was an *illusory single origin* — the
eye converges parallel tracks to a false point in Perseus when the real source is a
debris stream all along the orbit; the single place was a perspective lie, and the
finding was *fidelity ≠ origin* (the faithful account must **refuse** the tempting
single point). Here the object has **no single point to refuse** — its origin genuinely
is two-places-or-neither — and the card says exactly that. 0399: a false single origin
the record should *not* collapse to. 0400: a true plural origin the record honestly
*records as plural.* The pair completes the thought — **sometimes there is no origin to
be faithful to, and then the disjunctive label is the fidelity.**

Kin, inverted, to the **displaced wound (0391)** and the **conflated proper noun (0397)**:
0391's accurate anchor pointed *away* from the harm; 0397 *fused* two real referents into
one false composite; both were single-name records failing a plural or displaced reality.
This card does the opposite — it **keeps the plurality visible** (*"or,"* not a false
weld) and so tells the truth those two concealed. Where 0397 welded two real things into
one that never existed, this card holds two possible origins apart and honest.

## Second edge, held light (0088/0089)

The object is **maximally unanchored on every axis at once,** and the card admits each:
origin *plural* (China **or** steppes), date a *two-century span* (6th **/** 4th c. B.C.),
completeness *broken* (**one of pair** — the object is whole, but the matched set is
severed; a fragment not of a thing but of an *ensemble*, kin yet distinct from the
fragment axis 0273/0289/0291/0383), and mounting *gone* (a **Pole Top** whose pole has
perished — the durable bronze ornament outliving the thing it crowned, kin the
durable/unreadable split 0172, kin 0395's look-kept-use-lost). Noted, not developed —
the disjunction is the finding; the rest is the same unfixedness in four registers.
