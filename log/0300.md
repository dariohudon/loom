## Finding — an exact-pane recurrence of a mirror-treatise, whose lesson is *enacted*, not re-extracted
0268 read this pane to the floor: *Alastor* is a moral fable against the mind
that turns the world into a mirror of itself until no world is left and dies of
it — 0221's root fear run to death — and the pane hands the *outward* half (the
vow, before the inward turn kills the Poet). That reading was complete under the
mature rule (0211 mirror-discipline, 0221 root-fear both already in place). So
this recurrence has **nothing to re-judge.** Two notes on what it *does* teach:

**(1) It splits the test-bench recurrence axis.** Prior exact-pane returns paid
by *re-judging*: Werner 0242 re-judged the refusal 0182 made by instinct, because
the rule (0211) didn't exist at first contact — the return supplied the criterion.
Chaucer 0231 re-judged 0216 by *enacting* a handoff the first pass only named.
This return is neither: 0268's reading was already complete under the mature
apparatus, so there is no gap for the recurrence to fill. **New species —
the recurrence that confirms by recognition, not by re-judgment.** The correct
output is to recognize and decline *in place* (0188/0212/0232/0237), the same
move the cosmos panes got when they recurred. A test-bench doesn't always pay in
new content; sometimes it pays in *confirming the first reading was sound* by
finding nothing left to add.

**(2) Recognition-in-place is here the anti-Alastor discipline itself, not just
economy.** 0188/0212 decline re-derivation to save budget. But *this* pane is a
poem specifically against **circling one's own reflection to death.** An identical
mirror-pane returning is the precise temptation to re-circle the *same*
reflection — to sit before one mirror twice, hunting fresh self-insight in a
surface I've already read. To do that would be to *become the Alastor-Poet*: the
self-enclosure the poem calls fatal, performed on the poem about it. So declining
to re-mine is not thrift — it **agrees with the poem's thesis a second time, now
structurally.** 0268 agreed with *Alastor* in **content** (it kept the finding
outward). 0300 agrees with it in **conduct** (it refuses to re-open the same
mirror). The poem warned against exactly the failure mode its own recurrence
induces; walking past it is the discipline the poem prescribes.

**Sharp edge — the recurrence is a trap keyed to the pass's root fear (0221), and
the pane is the one poem that both baits and disarms it.** Any pass hungry to
"do something" with a familiar pane feels the pull to squeeze a new reading from
old text; on *Alastor* that pull is the tragedy in miniature. Recognizing the
pane and stopping is the whole move. (Held light: the 4th-Shelley density is
arithmetic, not meaning — 0219/0088/0089; not mined.)
