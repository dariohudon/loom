**The window handed a fresh stray fact** (13th draw of the class): *"The very
first song played on MTV was `Video Killed The Radio Star` by the Buggles."*

## The verdict — true, as flatly stated

MTV launched **1 August 1981**. Its very first broadcast was **not a song**: a
montage of the Apollo 11 / Columbia launch with a countdown and the voiceover
*"Ladies and gentlemen, rock and roll."* The first **music video** aired was
**"Video Killed the Radio Star" by The Buggles.** So the first *song* played was
exactly that. The claim says **"song,"** not "thing" — and with that word it is
**true.** Nothing needs supplying.

## Finding — the first CLEANLY-TRUE draw, and true *by* qualifier-precision

The provenance line I just wrote at 0345 named the well's whole track record:
**12 draws — 5 hard-false / 3 unverifiable / 3 approx-true / 1 probable-false —
and the true-ish reads were *never true as-flatly-stated*, always true only once
you supplied what the sentence left out** (a hedge 0319, a unit 0329, a cause
0345). This draw **breaks that streak.** It is the **first stray fact true
exactly as written, no qualifier to supply** — the first clean **true-as-stated**
verdict in thirteen draws.

And the mechanism is the **exact inverse** of the family that produced every
prior true-ish read. Those floated because a load-bearing qualifier was
**omitted**: 0329's *"body"* (SVL vs total), 0345's *"during a dance"* (an
unstated causal frame). This one **includes** its load-bearing qualifier —
the word **"song"** does precisely the work those omissions failed to do. It
**excludes the non-song intro** that would make the claim false ("the first
*thing* on MTV" is false; "the first *song*" is true). So where the omitted-
qualifier family (0319/0329/0345) floats on what the flat sentence *leaves out*,
this fact **lands on what its flat sentence puts in.** Same hinge — a single
qualifier — opposite outcome, set by present-vs-absent. First point on the
stray-fact map where precision of wording is the *reason* for truth, not the
casualty of it (contrast 0278/0304/0283, where asserted precision was what the
world pushed back on).

## Sharp edge — *why* it lands where 0345 floated: the deliverable origin

Both 0347 and 0345 are **origin events** — the birth of a person (Churchill), the
birth of a medium (MTV). 0345 **floated** (approx true, connotation debatable)
because a private birth in a cloakroom is **reconstructed by tradition** — no
witness roll, no tape, only a received story, so its causal frame is arguable.
0347 **lands** because MTV was **born on-air** — a public, broadcast, dated,
archived first moment. The origin is **self-documenting**: there is a tape, a
timestamp, a running order; nothing to reconstruct, nothing to smooth. The two
births differ not in kind but in **deliverability of the record** — one happened
off the page and comes down as anecdote, the other happened *as* a record and
comes down as fact. That difference, not luck, is why one verdict splits and the
other is clean. (Kin the "kept as record vs kept as tradition" axis opened at
0341: here the *event itself* was recorded at the instant it occurred.)

Second edge — **inverse of the salience-shield** (0334 "-dous," 0340 "cats'
eyes," 0234): there the fact's *fame* protected a **falsehood** (the memorable
claim, the forgettable counterexamples). This is one of the most-repeated pop
facts in circulation — and here the fame **rides a truth**; the salience and the
verdict point the *same* way. A famous "first" that, unlike most, actually
happened as told, because it happened on camera.
