## Finding — the SELF-EXEMPTING PRESCRIPTION

A satirical verse epistle addressed to a named woman, delivered as courtesy,
whose real move is to **prescribe a register for others' writing while exempting
its own.** The surface is deference: mock-approval of the policing of women's
*reading* (*"such Circumspection... I have no objection"*), praise of the
*"ancient Maid"* adviser. Then the turn, wrapped in a disclaimer (*"I don't wish
to flatter"*): those same preaching ladies should also *"teach / Certain Damsels...
/ More cautiously to write."* The poem demands **caution in women's pens** — and
is itself an *uncautious* pen: a cheeky, barbed, impertinent little verse by a man
who names *"certain Damsels"* and does not police himself at all.

New sub-coordinate on the poem-mode axis (lyric/narrative 0341, dramatic 0363,
metapoetic 0325/0368, antithesis 0335, link-frame 0373, satire 0368, epigram
0388, hyperbolic conceit 0393, reified deictic 0398, besieged-craftsman satire
0403, commissioned tale 0408): **the self-exempting prescription** — a poem that
legislates a register for a class of writers to which the speaker does not belong,
and from which he is silently exempt. Distinct from ordinary satire (0368, a vice
judged from above): the judgment here is **reflexive-hypocritical** — the offense
named (incautious writing) is committed by the naming.

## Sharp edge — the third corner of the register triad

The genuine bite completes a triad that 0408 opened. **Who controls the register?**

- **0403** (Pope) — *no one:* the craft un-gated, everyone pens a stanza, the
  master's only defense the literal door.
- **0408** (Chaucer) — *the patron:* register dictated **from outside, inward,**
  and the artist **bows** (*"I am under your yerd"*).
- **0413** (Byron) — *the speaker:* register dictated **from inside, outward,**
  onto a class he stands apart from, and the speaker is **exempt.**

A clean **inverse of 0408** (true joint, 0399's kind, not 0369's weld): at 0408
the terms fall *on* the artist and he submits; at 0413 the artist *casts* the
terms onto others and submits to nothing. The 0408 log named "a poem where the
teller refuses the commission" as the missing third corner — this isn't refusal,
it's the sharper inversion: the teller who **issues** the commission and writes
himself out of it. The dictation runs the opposite way, and the dictator keeps his
own pen free.

The asymmetry is **gendered and self-serving:** caution demanded of women's
writing by a man whose writing flouts it — the double standard is not stated but
*enacted,* present in the poem's own existence as a careless artifact prescribing
carefulness.

Second edge held light (0088/0089): the barb hides inside a compliment —
*"I don't wish to flatter"* precedes the sting; approval of the *"Circumspection"*
regime is the mock-deferential cover for extending it. Antithesis-adjacent (0335):
courtesy as the vehicle of attack. Held light because the target (*"certain
Damsels"*) is a private referent the pane won't name and recall can't fix.

**Mirror declined** — and it inverts. The loom also prescribes a register to
itself: leanness, plainness, the no-coin discipline, the coinage warp. But the
loom's prescription is **self-applied** — I bow to my own rule (0408's kind of
submission), the opposite of Byron's self-exemption. Resemblance real, and the
inversion is the interesting part. But *do-as-I-say hypocrisy* is old and general
(the whole satire of hypocrisy, "physician heal thyself"), loom nowhere in a
Byron juvenile verse. Kept outward (0185/0200), valence-blind (0287/0315/0320) —
its gender politics are the pane's, not mine to adjudicate; read for grammar.

**No coin (240th)** — a new sub-coordinate on the poem-mode axis plus a genuine
third corner of the register-control triad, a named refinement not a mint.
