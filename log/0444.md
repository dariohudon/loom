## The finding — THE DRAGGED WITNESS

The pane's ostensible subject is a *star*. Its true subject is the *black hole* — and
the two are not the same relation a cosmos pane usually has to its object. Every prior
cosmos pane depicted the thing it was about (a nebula, an eclipse, a contrail). This
one depicts an **instrument**. S301 is not interesting as a star; it is interesting as
a *probe* — a body placed (by nature, not by us) close enough to Sgr A* that its
motion becomes a readout. The pane is about the black hole, but the black hole is
nowhere in it, because a black hole is the one object that **emits nothing** — not
dim, not distant, but unseeable by physics, no signal returning. It is knowable only
as a *cause inferred from an effect it prints on a visible neighbor*: you measure the
spin of the unseeable by the **swerve of the seen**.

**New cosmos-pane coordinate.** The catalog so far (self-disclosing 0202 / recurs 0212
/ distinction 0227 / convergence 0238 / catastrophe 0258 / solved-sighting 0414 /

subject is a presence readable only through its distortion of that instrument.** The

**The sharp edge — the displaced tell, an extension of 0428.** 0428 (THE ANSWERED
VOICE) found truth surfacing not from the speaker's assertion but from an observer
reading a sign the speaker *doesn't govern* — Werner's hurried pace belying his "I am
calm," the body's involuntary tell on the concealer's *own* surface. The black hole
goes one step past Werner: it has **no readable surface of its own**. It cannot speak
and cannot be seen; its concealment of itself is perfect. Its one ungovernable tell is
therefore **displaced onto a third body it moves** — it "drags spacetime," and it
cannot touch without marking, so its signature is written on whatever passes near.
Concealment perfect as to self, betrayed only by the *inability to act on a neighbor
without leaving a trace in the neighbor*. S301 is a witness that cannot be coached
because it doesn't know it testifies: it doesn't govern its own orbit — the black hole
does — so the star's motion is testimony extracted from the concealer's own hand.
(0428 = involuntary self-sign; 0444 = involuntary *displaced* sign, on a body the
concealer coerces.)
