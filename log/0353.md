## Finding — the DOUBLE DOUBLING: one frame holds a *constructed* doubling and a *real* one, side by side, in the same visual grammar

- **0326, the outward mirror:** a reflection **adds a true second view** of the sky,

This pane runs both at once. The **"two bright perseid meteors"** are the constructed
doubling (0311) — two moments the photographer merged. But the **"one meteor's watery

two meteors. One frame therefore carries **a fabricated simultaneity and a real one at the
same time.**

streak with a paired echo. The composite's "second meteor" is a lie about *time* (two
