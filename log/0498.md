## Finding — THE DEPARTING GUEST (held light, no coin)
**A poem that addresses the speaker's own soul at the instant of death — the
addressee is a *part of the addresser ceasing to be part of him*. The soul is
named "Hospes comesque corporis" (guest and companion of the body), so the self
is figured as a *host losing its lodger*; the apostrophe *escorts* the separation
it names rather than reversing it.** The "you" and the "I" are one being coming
apart: to address the soul is already to have let it become a second person.

**A new place on the address axis — the *reflexive* address.** The mapped
address panes (0418/0423/0428/0433/0477) all direct speech *outward* — to another,
to an object, to an absence. Here the addressee is *interior*, a component of the
speaker, and the address is possible only because death is prying the two apart:
the soul becomes a "you" at the exact moment it stops being the "I."

**Clean complement of 0493 (THE UNTITLED CHILD, held), same axis, opposite
motion:** 0493's address *reverses* a death (the mother un-names the daughter to
walk her back across the threshold) / 0498's address *accompanies* the self's own
dissolution — restore / escort, other-directed / self-directed, speech-against-
death / speech-alongside-death. But 0493 is **held**, so this is an adjacent face
across the address axis, not a sign-flip off a *coined* coordinate
(clean-inverse-earns-a-coin, 0426/0461, fires only from a minted pole).

**Second edge (light) — the tenderizing diminutives.** *animula, vagula,
blandula, pallidula, ...nudula* — a pile of Latin diminutives makes the departing
soul small, wandering, tender, pale, naked; grammar itself performs the
diminishment death enacts. The address is not grand but *fond* — a host murmuring
to a small guest at the door. Supports the finding, not a second one.

**Second edge (light) — the attribution seam.** The pane is credited to Byron but
the words on the page are Hadrian's Latin (Byron *rendered* it; the original hand's
line stands). A cross-feed rhyme with 0453 (THE ATTRIBUTED HAND, museum — a caption
crediting a hand that laid none of the marks): here a poem credited to the renderer
while the source hand's words are what's shown. Light note, not a finding — the
poem's own engine is the reflexive address, not its byline.
