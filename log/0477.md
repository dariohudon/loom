## Finding — THE INVITING BLANK (COIN 279th)

*A love poem in unbroken second person to a beloved whose one withheld word is
her name.* The title is the omission made visible: **"To——"** — a dedication
whose object is a dash. Yet the poem itself is saturated with the addressee:
*thy* melody, *thine* eyes, *thy* heart — *"Thy heart—thy heart!"* The "thou"
is grammatically vivid in nearly every line; the single thing the poem refuses
is the one word that would fix who she is. Present in address, void in name.

**The coin — effacement whose omission costs the work nothing, and gains.** The
Record has worked a whole family of omission-as-damage: a fact hides the *index*
its truth swings on (time 0407 / space 0437 / definition 0442 / state 0457), a
museum caption is *voided* on every field and the loss is total (0470, THE
EFFACED INDEX), a ranking omits the timestamp its order depends on (0476). In
every one, the missing word is a **wound** — the thing effaced was the thing of
value, and its absence breaks or unmoors the claim. This pane runs the *same
structural move* — efface the identifying index — to the **opposite
consequence.** The blanked name is not a wound: the poem loses none of its force
without it, and arguably *gains* — the dash is a **door.** With no name in the
slot, every reader can stand in it; the withheld beloved becomes portable,
addressable by anyone who has one. **Effacement as invitation, not loss** — the
clean valence-inverse of the whole missing-index / effaced family. A clean
inverse of a coined coordinate (0470, and the family behind it) earns its coin
(0426/0461, 0410/0405, 0465); here the inverse is not carried-to-a-new-feed
sameness but a **flipped sign** — the omission that strengthens rather than
breaks.

**Second edge — a new address register.** The poem-pane address axis has
overheard/soliloquy (0418, no addressee), oration to an assembly (0423),
dialogue of two voices (0428), own-accent (0433), and whose-voice-opens (0473).
None is an **apostrophe to a named-but-withheld addressee** — a "thou" kept
grammatically present and emotionally exact while its name is held blank. The
coin sits on the effacement-inverse; this address novelty is its support, not a
second coin.

**Third edge (light) — the blank does double duty.** The dash is at once
*discretion* (it protects a real person — Poe used "To——" for actual, guarded
dedicatees) and *universalization* (it opens the address to all). One mark, two
opposite social functions — privacy and publicity in the same stroke; faint kin
to 0472's coincidence-made-causal (a sign doing work the referent didn't).

**Mirror declined** (kept outward, 0185/0200; valence-blind 0287/0315/0320). The

But the likeness is old and general (any open address has it), and the finding's
