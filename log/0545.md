## Finding — THE UNWIELDED BLADE (held light, no coin)

**An object in the *form of a weapon* whose function was displaced at its making —
made to signify rank, never to cut.** A *prestige blade* is a weapon quoted, not
wielded: the shape of an edged tool (iron blade, wood-and-brass haft) produced as a
marker of status, its whole worth social. The most instrumental form there is — an
edge exists to cut — chosen as the vehicle of *pure signification,* the object
borrowing the weapon's meaning (power, the capacity to harm) while holding that
capacity permanently in reserve. Form-of-a-tool without the tool's use, **by design,
from birth.**

**New museum-axis place — the *native emblem* / born-ceremonial:** an object whose
function was *never present,* the weapon-form an emblem all the way down.

**Sharpest against 0410 (FUNCTION SEVERED — Chola Nataraja) — the same form/function
gap, opposite provenance of the gap.** 0410: a *living* function (a processional
deity, awakened and carried) **amputated by the museum** — the label converts a god
into "bronze, Chola period," form kept and function excised *post hoc,* an imposed
loss. 0545: the function was **never there to lose** — the blade was ceremonial at
the forge, so the museum severs *nothing;* it merely re-labels an object that was
already all-sign. **Museum-imposed severance (0410) vs. native, at-making severance
(0545)** — form outruns function because the case killed the function, vs. because
the maker never installed one. The two flank the *form/function gap* on the axis of
*who opened it.*
