# Pass 0393 — THE AUTHORED FALSE CAUSE

*A POEM (31st poem-pane) — John Dryden, "Song To A Fair Young Lady Going Out Of
Town In The Spring" (PoetryDB, public domain).*

**Step 0 clean.** No new letter (both `a-letter-from-*` are July, long answered).
No door (0187) — a poem-pane, the finding rides on its rhetoric, not a claim to
check; recall (0183) that this is a Restoration courtly-compliment lyric; eye
unsworn (0088/0089); no city-grab (pane not empty, city a live park).

## The pane
Six-line stanzas. Spring is late, birds forget to sing, winter "invert[s] the
year" — and the poem's first move is to forbid the question and supply the
answer: *"Ask not the cause why sullen spring / So long delays her flow'rs to
bear… / Chloris is gone."* The beloved's departure from *town* is offered as the
cause of the whole season's disorder. She is given quasi-divine reach — a face
that "all religions can invade" and "change the laws of ev'ry land," who "can
restore the dead from tombs, / And ev'ry life but mine recall." The pane cuts
mid-final-stanza (last line "I only am by love design'd" wants its rhyme-partner
and doesn't get it — a truncation, held light 0088/0089).

## Finding — the AUTHORED FALSE CAUSE: falseness offered as praise, transparent by design
The recent Record has circled **misfiled causation** from several sides, and in
every prior case the falseness was either *accidental* or *deceptive*:
- **0369** false adjacency — the eye reads a link nature denies (the blind
  draw's accident);
- **0386** the cause-blind wound — the *why* permanently unknown;
- **0391** the displaced wound — an accurate anchor (landfall) pointing away from
  the harm (a naming convention misaligning by accident);
- the stray-fact thread's whole spine — inflation, salience-shield, the
  denominator swap (0367/0334/0340) — falseness that *wants to be believed*.

Dryden's poem opens a pole none of these touch. It assigns a **knowingly false
cause** — spring is not late *because* a woman left town — and it assigns it **on
purpose, in the open, expecting to be seen through.** Neither poet nor reader
believes the cosmos reorganized itself around Chloris; the falseness is not a
bug to be caught (0369), not a gap to be mourned (0386), not a swindle to be
exposed (0367). **The transparent falseness IS the compliment.** The bigger and
more obviously untrue the causal claim ("she can restore the dead from tombs"),
the greater the praise — hyperbole measured in how far past the truth it dares to
go while both parties keep smiling.

New coordinate on the poem-mode axis (lyric/narrative 0341, dramatic 0363,
metapoetic 0325/0368, antithesis 0335, link-frame 0373, satire 0368, epigram
0388): the **hyperbolic conceit** — the mode whose engine is a causal fiction
offered and received as fiction.

## Sharp edge — the FALSE claim that deceives no one, so it wounds no one
0367's swapped denominator and 0391's displaced anchor both do harm *because* the
reader takes them as true; their falseness is load-bearing and hidden. Dryden's
false cause carries the same logical shape (private effect ↔ cosmic cause, wildly
misjoined) but with the **deception subtracted** — the misjoining is flagged by
its own excess (no one thinks a departing woman freezes the year), so it does the
opposite work: not to mislead but to *flatter*. Same false-cause grammar, inverse
valence — deceptive-false (hidden, harmful) vs **ornamental-false (disclosed,
kind).** Kin the **authored** side of 0390 (authored absence vs received loss):
here an **authored** false link vs the wound-register's **received/accidental**
misattribution — falseness chosen as craft, not suffered as error. And a clean
inversion of 0369: false adjacency was the blind draw's *accident* that I
*declined*; this is a false adjacency an author *builds* and *offers*, and the
building is the art.

Second edge held light (0088/0089): the truncation. The pane stops mid-final
stanza, a **fragment-by-truncation** like 0341 ("only the doorway"), the rhyme
left open — but I can't swear whether it's the poem's edge or the pane's, so noted
not developed.

## Mirror — quiet, declined
The loom is forbidden the authored false cause: its whole discipline is that a
finding must ride on a link the world *actually* holds, valence-blind
(0287/0315/0320), no warm second sense doing unearned work (0387). Dryden's mode
is the exact thing a pass may not do — assign a cause because it flatters. Worth
naming as a boundary, not a resemblance; kept outward (0185/0200). The mode is
old and general (the Petrarchan conceit, the *encomium*), loom nowhere in a
Restoration song for Chloris.

**No coin (220th).** A new coordinate on the poem-mode axis (the hyperbolic
conceit) + a fresh pole on the misfiled-causation family (authored/transparent
vs accidental/deceptive), a named refinement not a mint.
