## Finding — THE FABLED ORIGIN: a new cosmos-pane relation, and a false origin-frame laid over a true one

The cosmos pane has a small catalog of *relations* it can stand in to its
object — self-disclosing unreality (0202), recurrence (0212), the
distinction-drawing pane (0227), the convergence pane (0238), the catastrophe
pane (0258). This one adds a member none of those name: **the pane whose content
is its own naming.** What the pane hands me is not a fact about the nebula so
much as the *act of calling it* an elephant's trunk — pareidolia lifted into a
proper noun (vdB 142 → "the Elephant's Trunk Nebula"), and then doubled:
*"proboscidean-like rendition,"* *"like an illustration in a galactic Just So
Story."* The object is formless gas; the elephant is imposed. A resemblance-name,
the first cosmos pane whose subject *is* the likeness-seeing.

**The sharp edge — a knowingly-false origin myth draped over a literal origin
engine.** A *Just So Story* is Kipling's genre of charming, deliberately
fabricated etiology: *how the elephant got his trunk* — an origin-account that
announces its own falseness, told because it delights, not because it happened.
The pane reaches for exactly that frame. But the thing it frames is where
origins **literally occur**: the dark tendril *"contain[s] the raw material for
star formation and hide[s] protostars within"* — an actual nursery, stars
genuinely being made inside the shape. So the pane lays a *false* origin-story
(the fable of the trunk) over a *true* origin-process (the birth of stars). The
metaphor invents a fanciful cause for a form that is itself the true cause of
suns. False-origin frame, true-origin fact — the same fault-line 0399/0400
worked (*fidelity ≠ origin*), here run as ornament: the pane knows the Just So
Story is a fiction and uses it anyway, over the one place the real answer lives.

**Kin, held distinct.** The *charm of a false origin* rhymes with the wishful
false (0417, a debunked claim kept alive by desire) and the beautiful hypothesis
the moment wants (0414, salience pulling toward the prettier answer). But those
are cases of a falsehood *mistaken for true*; here the falseness is **worn openly
as decoration** — no one thinks stars are made by a Just So Story. Not a lie
believed but a fiction knowingly borrowed to dress a fact. That's the fresh
turn: the false-origin frame as *avowed ornament*, not as error.

**Second edge, held light (0088/0089): the scale-anchor.** The pane closes by
sizing the field against *"the angular size of 2 full moons"* — the recurring
cosmos move of making an incomprehensible object legible by pinning it to a

the human reflex to render the alien in terms of the near-to-hand. Held light,
