## Finding — THE PARTIAL CREDIT (held light, no coin)

**A composite work attributed to one of its many contributors as though she were its
sole author.** The truth doesn't fail — Winger really contributed — it *overreaches*.
The whole obstacle sits in the scale of the claim: **one of many stated as the one**,
a synecdoche of authorship (a part named as the whole). E.T.'s voice is precisely the
kind of thing that has no single author — a Ben-Burtt assemblage, like the sound of a
lightsaber or a blaster — and the fact quietly collapses that multiplicity into a name.

**Stray-fact analog of 0453 (THE ATTRIBUTED HAND) — but a different failure of the
same axis.** 0453's museum caption credited a hand that laid *no* marks (over-
attribution to a **non**-contributor, authorship split clean along the design/substance
seam). This one credits a hand that laid *some* marks as if it laid *all* (over-
attribution to a **partial** contributor). Two ways to mis-credit: crediting the
absent hand (0453) / promoting a present-but-minor hand to sole author (0481). The
first is false; the second is *true-but-inflated*, the error living in **quantity**
(one/many) not in **identity** (wrong/right person). A cleaner-cut instance of the
"which layer carries the truth" fault-line (0397/0417/0422/0427/0452/0462/0467): here
the true layer is "**contributed**," the unsupportable layer is "**the** voice."

**Kin to the exaggeration family (0417 wishful false / 0422 figurative true / 0427
flattered figure):** like 0427, the inflation runs *toward the better story* — "a
famous actress secretly voiced the alien" is a tidier, more repeatable claim than "one
of Ben Burtt's dozen uncredited sources was Debra Winger." Desire for the clean
attribution steers the sentence, the same appetite 0417 named. Distinct from 0427 in
what inflates: 0427 stretched a *magnitude* (age 2–3 → 9); this stretches a *share*
(a fraction of the credit → the whole).

**Sub-edge (the live angle) — the missing co-authors.** What the fact omits is not a
date (0407) or a definition (0442) but the *rest of the roster* — Welsh above all,
whose rasp is the voice most listeners actually remember as E.T. The missing index
here is **the other contributors**, and the omission is what converts a share into a
totality. This register — *attribution made false by pruning the co-authors* — is what
would **earn a coin** if a single-credit-for-a-composite fact recurs off a clear warp
with the part-for-whole move isolated.
