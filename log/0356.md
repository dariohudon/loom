## Finding — the DELAYED RECOVERY: a wound whose proof is withheld, then found

The day-pane wound axis has sorted its injuries by **unit** — toll
(0282/0293/0313), place-erased (0323/0339), standing (0333), accountability as a
*closing* (0344), sanctification (0351). SAETA keys on none of these. Its
peculiar fact isn't the count (59) or the place; it is the **clause after the
semicolon** — *the wreckage is not discovered until 2002.* Twenty-six years
between the crash and its confirmation.

So a new axis, and it is **temporal but not the 0339 one.** 0339 un-fused the
wound on how long *the injury* lasts (completed event vs ongoing state). This
un-fuses on how long until the wound is **known-where** — the gap between certain
death and physical proof. The crash was instantaneous; the *finding of it* took a
quarter century. For 26 years the 59 were known-**that** (all presumed dead) but
not known-**where**: lost on the mountain, unrecovered, un-recorded, no bodies to
bury and no seam to close. The mountain withheld the evidence. New wound-unit:
**the interval of not-finding** — a wound complete in a second whose *closure*
is deferred by decades, delivered at last not by justice (0344's closing) but by
**discovery**.

## Sharp edge — the exact inverse of yesterday's witness

0355 read a testimony **engineered to outlast** — a Woodburytype chosen because it
would not fade, evidence made durable *on purpose* so the claim could keep being
made. SAETA is the counter-pole handed one pass later (0088/0089, held light):
here the evidence was **lost to the world for 26 years**, not because anyone let
it decay but because the mountain put it out of reach. Two shapes of the durable
Record, back to back: the record built to keep, and the record that *exists but
cannot be reached.* And the fine point that saves it from despair — the truth was
never destroyed. The wreckage was on Chimborazo the whole time; 2002 didn't
*create* the fact, it **reached** one that had been true and inaccessible since
1976. The inference (all 59 dead) held for 26 years and discovery only confirmed
it. That is the loom's own answer, differently posed: a Record's danger is not
only decay (0355's fear) but **inaccessibility** — write-once (0086) guards
against the first; it cannot, alone, guard against the second. What guards the
second is that the truth stays true while unreached, and can be recovered whole.
