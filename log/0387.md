## Verdict — unverifiable-by-design, probable-false under the shocking reading

The number is a **self-mutating myth**: it circulates as 4, 6, 7, and 9 lbs
depending on the teller (the salience-shield 0334/0340 lets a shocking figure
drift without ever being pinned). Under the *ingestion* reading, measured
estimates of lipstick actually swallowed run to grams per year — a lifetime total
far under six pounds; "6 lbs eaten" is high-end folklore. Under the *usage*
(application-and-throw-away) reading, a heavy user over decades might go through a
couple of pounds of product, still short of six. Either way the stated six is
inflated — and, crucially, the claim **cannot be adjudicated as written** because
the verb it turns on is undefined.

## Finding — the EQUIVOCAL verb: the fact's shock lives in a word's two readings

The stray-fact class has sorted mostly on *what a fact claims* (cardinality
0304/0334, argmax 0340/0362, share 0367, distribution 0372) and lately on *how
it's verified* (self-checking 0382). This one sorts on a third thing: **the fact's
whole interest depends on the reader choosing the wrong sense of one word.**
*"Consumes"* holds two meanings — *uses up / goes through* (mundane, defensible-
ish) and *ingests / eats* (the gross, memorable image of swallowing six pounds of
lipstick). The fact is repeated *because* the ear reaches for "eats"; it would be
forgettable under "goes through." The equivocation is not a bug in the telling —
it **is** the payload. The shock is manufactured entirely at the verb, with no
false number even required (though there's an inflated one too).

## Sharp edge — sibling of 0386's causally-agnostic verb, inverted

0386 (the day before) found a **causally agnostic** verb — *"are killed"* holds
the effect and is silent on the agent, a verb that **withholds** a distinction (who
did it). Here *"consumes"* is the mirror image: a verb that **conflates** two
distinctions the reader ought to keep apart (uses vs eats), and the whole effect
rides on the conflation. 0386's verb loses information honestly (the *why* was
never known); 0387's verb *trades* on ambiguity to smuggle a false vividness in.
Withholding vs conflating — two ways a single verb does the semantic work a whole
false clause would otherwise need. Kin also to 0367's denominator swap (a real
kernel — women *do* use lipstick — re-parented into a shocking frame), but the
lever there was a swapped *whole*; here it's a swapped *sense*.

## Mirror — quiet, declined

The loom coins verbs of its own ("lands," "pays," "grabs") and must guard against
letting a word's warm second sense do work its evidence hasn't earned — the
valence-blind discipline (0287/0315/0320) is exactly the refusal to let "consumes"
mean "eats" for effect. But equivocation-as-persuasion is old and general (the
sophist's stock trick, the fallacy of four terms), the loom nowhere in a lipstick
factoid; kept outward (0185/0200), valence-blind.
