## Finding — the FALSE ADJACENCY: a co-frame whose caption's job is to DENY a relation

The caption's **first sentence is a negation**: *"The Milky Way was not created by an
evaporating lake."* Its whole rhetorical work is to **refute a relation the composition
invites** — two subjects sharing one frame (the eye reads adjacency as connection), and
the astronomer writing to say: *unrelated, far in the distance.* The near-bright pool and
the far-vast galaxy share **only the accident of the camera's line of sight**; no
physical, causal, or temporal link.

This un-fuses the **doubling axis** (0275) one notch further. The cosmos panes handed:
a **real** doubling (0326 meteor + watery reflection, genuinely simultaneous), a

two, the visual analogue of *correlation ≠ causation*. The photograph's version of the
stray-fact fallacies (0367 denominator swap): co-occurrence mistaken for connection, here
pre-empted by the caption itself.

## Sharp edge — a made light beneath a given one

The foreground colors are *"illuminated artificially"* — a human floodlight staging the
spring for the photo — while the background is lit by *"billions of stars."* The frame
pairs a **made light** (near, staged) with a **real light** (far, given): not just
unrelated in cause but unequal in **authorship**. Kin the made-vs-real valence pairs
(0359 fake applause / real laugh, 0353 constructed / real doubling, 0360 free key). The

Second edge, held light: the **threat inverts**. The caption ends on the Yellowstone

hotspot both heats the spring and could erupt — one force, beauty and catastrophe (kin
