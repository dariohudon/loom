## Finding — THE EMPTY VESSEL (held light, no coin)

**A container-form whose defining capacity — an interior, a void made to receive and
hold — is present, offered, and permanently unexercised.** The object's own title
names its function-class: *Vessel* — a thing whose whole reason is to contain. Yet a
displayed art-vessel holds nothing; it is kept empty so the containing shape can be
read as pure form, all mouth and belly and hollow. The word on the label declares a
use the object will never perform (kin the title-declaring-a-state family: *Becalmed*
0541, *Work* 0536) — but here the withheld thing is not a state or a category noun,
it is a **function: to hold.** The vessel is a hospitality — a readiness to receive —
left standing open and unfilled.

**Sharpest against 0545 (THE UNWIELDED BLADE, last pass) — the same form/function gap,
opposite *vector* of the withheld function.** 0545: a weapon-form withholds an
**outward** function (to cut, to harm), its reserved capacity a *threat* ("I could
cut" by never cutting). 0546: a vessel-form withholds an **inward** function (to
hold, to receive), its reserved capacity a *hospitality* (a void ready for contents
that never come). Two consecutive museum panes flank the form/function gap by the
**direction** of the displaced function — instrument that acts on the world vs.
receptacle the world acts into; blade's edge turned out, vessel's hollow turned in.

**Against 0410 / 0545 (provenance-of-the-gap axis):** the blade's function was *never
there* (native emblem, ceremonial at the forge); the Nataraja's was *severed by the
museum* (a living function amputated post hoc). The vessel sits between — its
containing function is displaced **at making** (Odundo hand-builds it as sculpture,
not tableware, so like 0545 the gap is native to the object) yet, unlike a ceremonial
blade that *cannot* cut without violating its meaning, the vessel *could* still hold
— its emptiness is closer to a **choice of display** (kin 0479's museum-inertia): a
capacity intact but declined. Function-withheld-by-design (0545) vs.
capacity-intact-but-declined (0546).

**Against 0430 (HIDDEN INTERIOR):** 0430 an interior *concealed* (a void you are told
of but cannot see) / 0546 an interior *displayed empty* (a void offered to the eye and
left unfilled) — hidden hollow vs. shown-empty hollow.
