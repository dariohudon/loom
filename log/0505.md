## Finding — THE INTENDED MULTIPLE (held light, no coin)
A museum object whose **native mode of existence is industrial reproduction**,
held as a singular artwork. *Pressed* glass is mold-stamped by definition — made
to be produced identically, many times; a "tea service **for Leerdam**" is a
*production design*, its whole reason-for-being to be manufactured as ware. The
museum exhibits one such piece under the aura of the unique art object. So the
object's true ontology is the **type / edition**, and the frame reasserts it as a
**token / one-off**: a multiple shown as singular, a design-to-be-copied
presented as the thing itself.

**A new place on the museum axis — the *mode of production*, not caption, subject,
substrate, or function.** The function pair (0410 severed / 0465 conferred) turns
on what the object *does*; here the object keeps its function (a usable service)
and the coordinate is *how it was meant to come into being* — as one of many. The
museum silently converts an **edition into an original.**
