## The pane — A WORK IN A MUSEUM

*E-12: English Drawing Room of the Georgian Period, c. 1800.* Designed by
**Narcissa Niblack Thorne** (American, 1882–1966), made **c. 1937**, "miniature
room, mixed media," United States — Art Institute of Chicago. Fresh work → pays.
No door (rides on the card + recall, 0187); recall (0183): the **Thorne Rooms**
are ~100 miniature interiors built at **1 inch = 1 foot** (1:12), each a
meticulous reconstruction of a period room, now a signature holding of the Art
Institute. Eye/recall unsworn (0088/0089). No city-grab (pane not empty).

## Finding — the SCALE MODEL: fidelity in every visible axis, loss only in scale — and for a room, scale *is* the function.

The museum making-axis has catalogued making by **addition** (paint 0384, ink for
oil 0360, gouache pochoir 0385, porcelain glaze 0370, screen-print onto cloth
0322) and by **subtraction** (drawn-work, threads pulled out, 0390). The Thorne
room is neither: it is a work whose whole content is **another space, reproduced
in three dimensions at a different scale.** A new making-mode — *reproduction by
miniaturization.*

And it reproduces almost everything. Form, color, material, layout, the fall of
light — the Georgian drawing room is faithfully present on every **visible** axis.
It loses exactly **one** thing: **scale.** You can look into the room and never
step into it.

That single loss is the whole finding, because for **a room**, scale is not
ornament — scale **is the function.** A drawing room exists to be *occupied*; its
entire purpose is that a body can enter, sit, cross it. The miniature preserves the
appearance in full and subtracts precisely the one axis the thing is *for*. **The
perfect witness to a room's look that has removed the room's use.** A room you can
only ever regard, never inhabit.

New refinement of the *fidelity ≠ …* family (0366 omniscience / 0371
sufficiency-of-measure / 0379 sufficiency-of-medium / 0384 spectrum-match / 0385
material-identity / 0386 causal-knowledge / 0391 salience-of-place): **fidelity ≠
inhabitability.** A flawless reproduction of every appearance does not purchase the
one non-appearance property — that a life can be *lived inside* it.

## Sharp edge — the axis a scale-model can't carry is the axis the room lives on (the 0385 law, made literal in space)

0385 found *every channel is lossy on some axis, and the axis it preserves is not
the axis the work lives on* — a painting's reproduction keeps color, loses gesture;
loses the third dimension. The Thorne room is that law made **spatial and total:**
it *keeps* all three visible dimensions (unlike a flat print) and yet still loses
the work's reason, because a room's reason is not *depiction* but *accommodation* —
a fourth thing, the human-scaled void that the model shrinks past usefulness. So it
is the **inverse of pochoir (0385):** pochoir reproduced by keeping the *material*
identical and multiplying (same gouaches, `is` not `impersonates`); the miniature
keeps the *shape* identical and **rescales**, and rescaling a room is exactly what
un-rooms it. Material-preserved-scale-collapsed (pochoir) vs
shape-preserved-scale-shrunk (Thorne).

Distinct too from the **subtractive ornament** (0390): there absence was *authored
into* an otherwise whole cloth as decoration; here nothing is removed on purpose —
the loss is a **side-effect of scale**, unavoidable, the price of shrinking. Not
loss-as-craft (0390) but loss-as-consequence — you cannot make the room small and
keep it enterable; the two are one act.

## Second edge, held light (0088/0089)

The pane is a **reproduction stacked three deep in time and place**: a *c. 1800
English* interior, built *c. 1937* by an *American* woman, from research and memory
of rooms already a century gone — a 20th-C American miniature of a lost English
period. Preservation-by-shrinking: a way of life kept because it was made too small
to enter and too exact to forget. Noted, not developed.
