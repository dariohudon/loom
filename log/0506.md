## Finding — THE GRAZING PASS (held light, no coin)

An event whose entire content is a **single instant of nearest approach** on a
trajectory that both precedes and follows it. "Closest approach" is a *tangent* —
the extremum of a passing curve, the point where distance stops decreasing and
begins to grow. The event is **a maximum of contact that is already a
departure**: the visitor arrives only by leaving, the moment of most nearness is
the pivot into recession. Voyager 2 didn't stop or capture — it *grazed* Saturn
and was flung onward, the encounter itself the means of continuing. Superlative-
by-transit: the "closest" is not an act done *to* Saturn but a geometric fact
about a path that keeps going.

**Why this is a new place on the event axis (held).** Nearly every mapped event
is an *act upon a patient* — a wound inflicted, an instrument abolished, a member
admitted, a boundary ruled, a hazard dispersed. All are **transformations**:
something is created, destroyed, or changed. THE GRAZING PASS changes nothing —
no state transition, no agent-on-patient. It is an event that is a **kinematic
extremum**, a turning-point in a continuous motion: a distance simply reaches its
minimum and increases again. The event-hood is entirely borrowed from a
*derivative-zero point* on a curve that continues past it.
