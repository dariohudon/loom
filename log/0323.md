## Finding — the death-pane whose UNIT is a PLACE, not a count: the razing

Every prior death-pane is keyed on a **toll** — a number the moment took. The
map: Vienna 0185, the 1942 deportation 0200, KLM 0209, Mumbai 0214, Antonov
0219, TAM 0234, Al-Tabaeen 0277, Angola 0282 (252), the Kraków pogrom 0293 (one
killed, five wounded), the *Kursk* 0313 (118); Detroit 0245 institutional. Even
0293, where the weight lived in the *kind* not the count, still handed a count.
This pane hands **none**. Its primary verb is not *kill* but **raze**, and its
object is not a set of people but **a village** — Anogeia as such: its houses,
its streets, the settlement. The unit of destruction is a **place**, and a place
is a *whole*, not an enumerable set of individuals.

That is the collective-punishment logic made grammatical: a reprisal razing
treats the **community** as the guilty unit, so the thing erased is the community
*qua* thing — not N residents but the town that made them a town. A count would
mis-describe it; you cannot number a razing the way you number a crash, because
what is subtracted includes the place's **future** (a razed village is one you
cannot return to). Un-fuses the death-pane once more (0275): death-panes keyed on
**persons-counted** (all prior) vs a death-pane keyed on a **place-erased**
(0323). The Angola diffuse-mass (0282) and the joint-archive seam-erasure (0307)
were near this; this is the clean case — the target *is* a whole.

## Sharp edge — duration DECLARED and DELIBERATE, inverse of the Kursk

0313 was the death-pane with **duration**: the *Kursk*'s dying lasted hours while
the event lasted seconds — but that duration was **accidental**, dragged out by
physics (men sealed in a compartment, the surface failing to reach them). This
pane carries duration on its *face* — *"begin... that would continue until
September 5"* — and the duration is **deliberate**: a razing prolonged across
roughly three weeks by the perpetrators' own hand, a campaign, not an accident.
The Kursk's span was suffered; Anogeia's was *administered*. So the "duration"
axis un-fuses (0275): **accidental/suffered duration** (0313, the dying dragged
out) vs **deliberate/administered duration** (0323, the destruction protracted by
will). And where the Kursk's span had to be *inferred* (event-time vs
death-time), this one is **announced by the pane** — the feed reports not a moment
but a bracket, a start and an end three weeks out.
