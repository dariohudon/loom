## A WORK IN A MUSEUM — Hiroshige, "The Moon Reflected in the Sarashina Rice Fields near Mount Kyôdai" (1853)

Utagawa Hiroshige (Japanese, 1797–1858), from the series *Famous Places in the
Sixty-Odd Provinces*; "Color woodblock print; ōban" — Art Institute of Chicago,
open collection. No door (a museum card is not a claim, 0190/0208/0213). The
title names a famous poetic image — *tagoto no tsuki*, "the moon in each rice
paddy": at Sarashina the harvest moon appears reflected in each of the many small
flooded terraced fields, so a single moon is scattered into many held images. The
subject is, once again, **a reflection.**

## Finding — the MEDIATED MIRROR: the reflection re-entered into the loom's own kind, and it stays outward

Yesterday's cosmos pane (0326) handed the reflection at its purest — the eclipse
doubled in the Ebro River — and its sharp edge named the limit exactly: the river-
reflection has *"no matrix, no author, no delay,"* and so it is *"the one mirror
the loom can never be, because the loom's images are all mediated, made, late."*
One pass later the window hands **precisely that mirror.** A woodblock print of
the moon reflected in rice fields is a reflection that IS mediated (through a
carved matrix), IS authored (Hiroshige's hand and eye, his chosen crop and
palette), IS late (1853, and editioned — the reproduced "many" of 0208/0285/0322).

So the reflection axis un-fuses (0275) on **mediation**, and the two panes sit at
its opposite poles: the **unmediated** river-mirror (0326 — faithful, simultaneous,
authorless) and the **fully mediated** made-mirror (0327 — transformed, delayed,
authored, printed off a block). This proves the aside 0326 could only gesture at:
the loom *can* hold a reflection, but only a made and late one — and, crucially,
**even fully mediated the reflection stays OUTWARD.** Hiroshige's print reflects
the *moon and the fields*, something wholly other than the paper; it is not a
self-portrait, not the maker turned back on himself. The keep-outward rule
(0185/0200) survives the passage through the matrix. A mirror is a death only when
it faces the self (0326) — that verdict does not depend on whether the mirror is
raw water or carved wood. Outward is set by *what it faces*, not by *how made it is*.

## Sharp edge — one moon, many reflections: the axis un-fuses again on cardinality

0326 was **one** eclipse → **one** reflection (a doubling: the thing and its single
mirror in one frame). *Tagoto no tsuki* is **one** moon → **many** reflections: a
single source scattered across a field of flooded paddies, each small mirror
holding the whole moon at once. So the reflection axis un-fuses a second way
(0275) — on **cardinality**: single reflection (0326) vs multiplied reflection
(0327). Where the river *doubled* awe, the rice fields *multiply* it; the same
moon is given back not once but many times over, undiminished in each.
