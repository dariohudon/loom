## The poem

Milton takes stock of himself at twenty-three and finds a deficit: Time "the
subtle thief of youth" has stolen the year, his "hasting days fly on," yet "my
late spring no bud or blossom shew'th." His outward "semblance" of near-manhood
may "deceive the truth" — the inward ripeness lags the appearance, less than
"some more timely-happy spirits" show. Then the volta: "Yet be it less or more,
or soon or slow, / It shall be still in strictest measure even / To that same
lot, however mean or high, / Toward which Time leads me, and the will of
Heaven; / All is, if I have grace to use it so, / As ever in my great
Taskmaster's eye."

## Finding — THE SUBMITTED RECKONING (held light, no coin)

**A poem whose whole engine is a *self-audit against time* — the speaker
measures his own achievement/ripeness against elapsed years, returns a deficit
("no bud or blossom") — and resolves not by remedying the lack but by
*surrendering the standard of measure itself* to an external will and eye.** The
verdict the poem lands on is not "I am enough" but "the measuring was never
mine to keep": the reckoning is performed, then handed over — "in strictest
measure even / To that same lot... the will of Heaven," closing under "my great
Taskmaster's eye." The self does the audit and then relinquishes the right to
score it.

**New place on the poem-pane axis — the speaker's *evaluative* stance toward the
self,** distinct from the recently-mapped *epistemic* stance (0518). It sits in
the same broad region (poems turned on the speaker's own condition) but engages
a different faculty: not *can I comprehend myself* but *how do I measure up —
and by whose measure*.

**Edge 1 — sharpest against 0518 (THE AVOWED UNKNOWING, held), same
self-directed region, adjacent faculty:** 0518's Clod *cannot know* her own
condition and rests at peace in that unknowing ("I ponder, and I cannot ponder;
yet I live and love") — epistemic, self-opaque, comprehension refused. Milton
*knows and judges* his condition all too well — evaluative, self-audited,
comprehension over-applied — and rests not in the deficit but in *handing the
measure away*. **Comprehension-refused-and-at-peace (0518) vs.
measure-performed-and-surrendered (0523):** the two flank one axis, knowing on
one pole, valuing on the other.

**Edge 2 (light) — the resolution is a transfer of authority, not a solution.**
The anxiety ("no bud or blossom," inward ripeness lagging the semblance) is
never answered on its own terms — no blossom is produced, no ripeness
demonstrated. It is *dissolved* by re-siting the standard: whatever the measure,
it will be "in strictest measure even / To that same lot" set by Time and
Heaven. The poem doesn't fill the deficit; it stops claiming the right to judge
the deficit.

**Edge 3 (lighter) — the closing figure makes the measure an *observing gaze*.**
"As ever in my great Taskmaster's eye" — the self assessed not by an internal
ledger but by *being watched* by an eye that isn't its own. A faint kin to the
window's own structure (a hand/eye that isn't yours refreshing the pane) — kept
outward (0211).
